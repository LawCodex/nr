## PART 1 — PRELIMINARY

#### 1 Short title and commencement

(1) This Act may be cited as the Telecommunications Service Tax Act 2009.

(2) This Act shall come into force on the date on which it receives the certificate of the Speaker and came into effect on 28 August 2009.

#### 2 Interpretation

In this Act:

‘Minister’ means the Minister responsible for Finance;

‘monthly statement’ means the statement prescribed in Section 6(2);

‘records’ means the records described in Section 6(1) including the monthly statements prescribed in Section 6(2);

‘Secretary’ means the head of the Department of Finance;

‘service’ means a telecommunications service;

‘service provider’ means a provider of a telecommunications service or authorised under the Communications and Broadcasting Act 2018;

‘tax’ means the telecommunications service tax levied under Section 4; and

‘telecommunications’ means the carriage of communications by means of guided or unguided electromagnetic energy or both as provided for in the Communications and Broadcasting Act 2018.

#### 3 Act to bind the Republic

This Act binds the Republic.

## PART 2 — TELECOMMUNICATIONS SERVICE TAX

#### 4 Telecommunications service tax

(1) A service provider is liable on the last day of each month to pay to the Republic, the telecommunications service tax calculated in accordance with subsection (2) in respect of the telecommunications services provided by it in that month.

(2) The amount of telecommunications service tax payable is 10% of the total gross sales revenue received by the service provider in respect of sales of telecommunications services in a calendar month.

[subs (2) am Act 47 of 2016 s 4, opn 8 Sep 2016; Act 2 of 2022 s 4, opn 8 Feb 2022]

(3) A service provider shall pay the telecommunications service tax within 1 month of having become liable under subsection (1) to pay the tax.

(4) A service provider shall submit the monthly statement within 1 month of having become liable to pay tax under subsection (1).

[subs (4) am Act 52 of 2016 s 4, opn 4 Nov 2016]

## PART 3 — ADMINISTRATION

#### 5 Payment and collection

The mode of payment and collection of tax shall be determined in accordance with Part 7 of the Revenue Administration Act 2014.

[s 5 am Act 52 of 2016 s 5, opn 4 Nov 2016]

#### 6 Records

(1) A service provider shall keep complete and accurate records of prices charged for each of the telecommunications services it provides and of the details of all transactions for the sale of telecommunications services.

(2) A service provider shall produce a monthly statement that includes a full and true disclosure of all transactions for the sale of telecommunications services in a calendar month.

(3) The Secretary or his or her delegate is authorised to inspect the records of any service provider for the purposes of administering this Act.

(4) Where the Secretary authorises a delegate to inspect the records of any service provider he or she shall issue the delegate with a written and signed authorisation, which authorisation shall relate to the specific inspection proposed, and the delegate shall produce such written authorisation upon the request of the specified service provider.

(5) In addition to any penalty to which a service provider may be liable under Section 12, 14 or 15 for an offence relating to records, a service provider that fails to keep records as required under this Section may have their licence or authorisation suspended or cancelled.

#### 7 Administration of this Act

This Act shall be administered by the Secretary.

[s 7 am Act 52 of 2016 s 6, opn 4 Nov 2016]

#### 8 Regulations

The Cabinet may make regulations prescribing matters necessary or convenient to be prescribed to give effect to this Act, including provisions for waiver or exemption from tax liability on specified grounds.

#### 9 Application of revenue

All revenues received by the Republic from the collection of the tax shall be paid into the Treasury Fund.

#### 10 Interest in respect of tax defaults

(1) If a tax default occurs, the service provider is liable to pay interest on the unpaid tax.

(2) Interest is calculated at a reasonable commercial rate on a daily basis from the date the tax default occurs to the date of payment. s 10LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

## PART 4 — OFFENCES AND PENALTIES

#### 11 Tax evasion

(1) A service provider shall not evade the tax.

(2) A service provider that commits an offence under this Section is liable to a penalty of $100,000.

#### 12 Falsifying or concealing records

(1) A service provider shall not falsify or conceal records.

(2) A service provider that commits an offence under this Section is liable to a penalty of $50,000.

#### 13 Failure to pay tax

A service provider that fails, without lawful excuse, to pay the tax in accordance with Section 4(1), (2) and (3), commits an offence and is liable to a penalty of $50,000.

[s 13 am Act 47 of 2016 s 5, opn 8 Sep 2016]

#### 14 Failure to keep records

A service provider that fails to keep records in accordance with the requirements of Section 6, commits an offence and is liable to a penalty of $10,000.

#### 15 Failure to submit monthly statement

A service provider that fails to submit the monthly statement required under Section 4(4), commits an offence and is liable to a penalty of $10,000.

[s 15 am Act 47 of 2016 s 6, opn 8 Sep 2016]

#### 16 Application of Revenue Administration Act 2014

The Revenue Administration Act 2014 is subject to this Act and applicable only for the purposes of the administration of this Act.

[s 16 insrt Act 52 of 2016 s 7, opn 4 Nov 2016]
