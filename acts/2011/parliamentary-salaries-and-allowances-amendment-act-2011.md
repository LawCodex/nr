# Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act 2011

Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act 2011 ______________________________ Act No. 15 of 2011 ______________________________ Contents REPUBLIC OF NAURU Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act 2011 ______________________________ Act No. 15 of 2011 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008 to increase salaries of members of Parliament Certified on 16 August 2011 Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act 2011.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act is taken to have commenced on 1 July 2011.

#### 3 Act amended

The Schedule amends the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008.
