# Nauru Phosphate Royalties (Payment and Investment) (Amendment) Act 2011

Nauru Phosphate Royalties (Payment and Investment) (Amendment) Act 2011 ______________________________ Act No. 21 of 2011 ______________________________ Contents REPUBLIC OF NAURU Nauru Phosphate Royalties (Payment and Investment) (Amendment) Act 2011 ______________________________ Act No. 21 of 2011 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Nauru Phosphate Royalties (Payment and Investment) Act 1968 Certified on 20 December 2011 Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Nauru Phosphate Royalties (Payment and Investment) (Amendment) Act 2011.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification.

#### 3 Amendment of Act

The Schedule amends the Nauru Phosphate Royalties (Payment and Investment) Act 1968.
