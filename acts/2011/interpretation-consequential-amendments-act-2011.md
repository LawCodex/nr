# Interpretation (Consequential Amendments) Act 2011

Interpretation (Consequential Amendments) Act 2011 ______________________________ Act No. 18 of 2011 ______________________________ Contents Interpretation (Consequential Amendments) Act 2011 As certified, and in force from 3 November 2011 REPUBLIC OF NAURU Interpretation (Consequential Amendments) Act 2011 ______________________________ Act No. 18 of 2011 ______________________________ An Act to make amendments to various Acts following the enactment of the Interpretation Act 2011 Certified on 3 November 2011 Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Interpretation (Consequential Amendments) Act 2011.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day it is certified by the Speaker under Article 47.

#### 3 Amendment of Acts

The Schedule amends the Acts mentioned in it.

As certified, and in force from 3 November 2011

#### 5 Parental responsibility for conduct of child

A parent of a child commits an offence if:

(a) the child engages in conduct that would constitute an offence against this Act if the child were capable of being criminally responsible for the conduct; and

(b) the child is not capable of being criminally responsible for the conduct; and

(c) the child engages in the conduct in the presence of the parent; and Interpretation (Consequential Amendments) Act 2011 As certified, and in force from 3 November 2011

(d) the parent does not take action to remedy the result of the child’s conduct.

Maximum penalty: $300.
