An Act to provide for the salaries and allowances of Judges of the Supreme Court and the Clerk of Parliament.

Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title and commencement

This Act may be cited as the Statutory Salaries Act 1974 and came into effect on 5 December 1974.

#### 2 Salaries of Judges and the Clerk of Parliament to be fixed by

regulations

(1) The Cabinet shall from time to time by regulations fix the salaries and allowances of Judges of the Supreme Court and the Clerk of Parliament:

Provided that:

(a) the salaries and allowances payable to a person office of a Judge of the Supreme Court or of the Clerk of Parliament shall not be altered to his or her disadvantage during the term of his or her appointment; and

(b) the salary and allowances of the Clerk of Parliament shall be fixed by the Cabinet only after consultation with the Speaker.

(2) The salaries and allowances payable to Judges of the Supreme Court and the Clerk of Parliament shall be paid out of, and be a charge upon, the Treasury Fund.

#### 3 Salaries and allowances of Judges and the Clerk of Parliament

before the commencement of this Act For the avoidance of doubt, it is hereby declared that all salaries and allowances paid in respect of any period before the commencement of this Act to Judges of the Supreme Court and to the Clerk of Parliament are to be deemed for all purposes to have been salaries and allowances prescribed by law.

#### 8 When salary payable

(1) Where the employment of a Judge is on a full time basis, requiring him or her to be present in the Republic, he or she shall be paid an annual salary of an aggregate of 12 months salary fixed and set out in Schedule 1.

[subreg (1) am SL 16 of 2022 reg 10, opn 13 July 2022]

(2) Where the employment of a Judge is on a circuit or for a specific cause or matter, he or she shall be paid a salary for the period he or she is in the Republic commencing from the date of arrival to the date of departure.

[subreg (2) am SL 16 of 2022 reg 10, opn 13 July 2022]

(3) For the purposes of subregulation (2), the salary of a Judge shall:

(a) be paid on a monthly basis or part thereof depending on the period of his or her presence in the Republic; and

(b) include Saturdays, Sundays and Public Holidays.

[subreg (3) am SL 16 of 2022 reg 10, opn 13 July 2022]

#### 9 Other allowances

(1) A Judge shall be paid a non-accountable subsistence allowance per month or part thereof for the period of his or her presence in the Republic as set out in item 1 of Schedule 2.

[subreg (1) am SL 16 of 2022 reg 11, opn 13 July 2022]

(2) The Chief Justice shall be provided with a reimbursable entertainment allowance as set out in item 2 of Schedule 2.

[subreg (2) am SL 16 of 2022 reg 11, opn 13 July 2022]

#### 10 Travel

(1) Subject to the availability of flight and a seat, a Judge shall be entitled to a business class return airfare from his or her place of residence to the Republic.

[subreg (1) am SL 16 of 2022 reg 13, opn 13 July 2022]

(2) Where a Judge is required to travel on official travel, he or she shall be entitled to a business class return airfare.

[subreg (2) am SL 16 of 2022 reg 13, opn 13 July 2022]

(3) The spouse of the Chief Justice may accompany him or her to the Republic or on official travel on a business class return airfare.

(4) Where a Judge travels on official travel, he or she:

(a) shall be paid a per diem as set out in Schedule 2; and

(b) is not eligible to be paid any salary unless he or she is employed on a full-time basis.

[subreg (4) am SL 16 of 2022 reg 13, opn 13 July 2022]

#### 11 Accommodation

A furnished paid for accommodation shall be provided to a Judge by the Republic.

[reg 11 am SL 16 of 2022 reg 12, opn 13 July 2022]

#### 13 Medical

A Judge is entitled without charge for any medical treatment available at the Republic of Nauru Hospital.

[reg 13 am SL 16 of 2022 reg 13, opn 13 July 2022]

#### 14 Leave

(1) Where a Judge is employed on a full-time basis, he or she shall be entitled to the following leave:

(a) recreation leave of 20 working days;

(b) sick leave of 10 working days;

(c) special leave of 3 working days; and

(d) any other leave contained in the Contract of Employment or as the Cabinet may approve.

[subreg (1) am SL 16 of 2022 reg 13, opn 13 July 2022]

(2) The Judge who is employed on a full time basis may take leave of absence from the Republic during a judicial vacation.

[subreg (2) am SL 16 of 2022 reg 13, opn 13 July 2022]

#### 15 Telecommunication

A Judge shall be provided with an official phone number and internet facility as set out in Schedule 3.

[reg 15 am SL 16 of 2022 reg 13, opn 13 July 2022]

#### 16 Allowances not taxable

The allowances and other benefits of a Judge are not taxable income.

[reg 16 am SL 16 of 2022 reg 13, opn 13 July 2022]

#### 17 Employment and services tax

The salary of a Judge shall be subject to the provisions of the Employment and Services Tax Act 2014.

[reg 17 am SL 16 of 2022 reg 13, opn 13 July 2022]

#### 18 Repeal

The Judicial Salaries Regulations 1980 are repealed.

(1) These Regulations shall not apply to an existing contract of employment of any Judge until the date of expiry of such contract, when it is deemed to have come to an end.

## SCHEDULE — SALARY OF CLERK

Statutory Salaries (Clerk of Parliament Conditions of Service and Salary) Regulations 2020

## Table of Amendments

The Statutory Salaries (Clerk of Parliament Conditions of Service and Salary) Regulations 2020 SL 35 were notified and commenced on 13 November 2020.

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 2 of the Statutory Salaries Act 1974:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Statutory Salaries (Clerk of Parliament Conditions of Service and Salary) Regulations 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Objective

The objective of these Regulations is to prescribe the conditions of service, salary and allowances of the Clerk of Parliament.

#### 4 Salary of the Clerk of Parliament

The salary of the Clerk is set out in the Schedule.

#### 5 Travel

(1) Where the Clerk is required to travel on official travel, he or she shall be entitled to a business class return airfare.

(2) Where the Clerk travels on official travel, he or she shall be paid a per diem at the rate provided for Heads of Departments in the Republic of Nauru travel allowance for official travel on behalf of the Republic.

#### 6 Transport

The Clerk shall be provided with transport for his or her official and personal use for the duration of his or her appointment.

#### 7 Leave

(1) The Clerk shall be entitled to the following leave:

(a) recreation leave of 20 working days;

(b) sick leave of 10 working days;

(c) special leave of 3 working days;

(d) long service and furlough leave; and

(e) any other leave as the Speaker may approve.

(2) The Clerk may take such leave as may be appropriate in consultation with and approval of the Speaker.

#### 8 Telecommunication

The Clerk shall be provided with an official phone, official phone number and internet facility as approved by the Speaker.

#### 9 Repeal

The Clerk of Parliament’s Salary Regulations 1979 are hereby repealed. reg 9LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU
