#### 1 Short title and commencement

This Act maybe cited as the Lands (Validating) Act 1951 and came into effect on 12 November 1951.

#### 2 Validation of certain agreement for lease

Notwithstanding anything contained in Section 3 of the Lands Ordinance 1921-1950, the Agreement dated the 8th day of August, 1949 made between certain Naoeros and the British Phosphate Commissioners for the lease to the British Phosphate Commissioners of an area of land at the Republic containing 2 acres 2 roods 25.12 perches and approved by Harold Hastings Reeve, purporting to act as Acting Administrator of the Island of Naoero, is hereby declared to be, and at all times, to have been, valid and effectual.
