# Counter Terrorism and Translational Organised Crime (Amendment) Act 2010

Republic of Nauru Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime (Amendment) Act 2010 ______________________________ Act No. 4 of 2010 ______________________________ Table of Provisions Republic of Nauru Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime (Amendment) Act 2010 ______________________________ Act No. 4 of 2010 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime Act 2004.

Certified on 18 November 2010 Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime (Amendment) Act 2010.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date the Speaker certifies that it has been passed by Parliament under Article 47.

#### 3 Act amended

The Schedule amends the Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime Act 2004.

Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime (Amendment) Act 2010 Schedule – Amendment of Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime Act 2004 Section 3

[1] Renumbering of section 1 Section 1 renumber as section 1A

[2] New section 1 Before section 1A insert 1 Short title This Act may be cited as the Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime Act 2004.

[3] Amendment of Schedule 1 Schedule 1, after item 13 insert Amendment to the 1980 Convention on the Physical Protection of Nuclear Material, adopted at Vienna on 8 July 2005

#### 15 Protocol to the 1988 Convention for the Suppression of Unlawful Acts

against the Safety of Maritime Navigation, adopted in London on 14 October 2005 Protocol to the 1988 Protocol for the Suppression of Unlawful Acts against the Safety of Fixed Platforms located on the Continental Shelf, done at London on 14 October 2005 I hereby certify that the above is a fair print of a Bill for an Act entitled Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime (Amendment) Act 2010 that has been passed by the Parliament of Nauru and is now presented to the Deputy Speaker, exercising the powers of the Speaker under Article 35(4), for his certificate under Article 47 of the Constitution.

Pursuant to Article 47 of the Constitution, I, Landon Deireragea, Deputy Speaker of Parliament, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by Article 35(4) of the Constitution, hereby certify that the Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime (Amendment) Act 2010 has been passed by the Parliament of Nauru.

Clerk of Parliament 18 November 2010 Deputy Speaker of Parliament 18 November 2010
