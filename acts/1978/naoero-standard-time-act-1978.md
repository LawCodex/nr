# Naoero Standard Time Act 1978

## Table of Amendments

The Naoero Standard Time Act 1978 No 13 was certified and commenced on 21 December 1978.

Amending Legislation Certified Date of Commencement Interpretation (Consequential Amendments) Act 2011 No 18 3 November 2011 3 November 2011 Revised Written Laws Act 2021 No 7 1 June 2021 1 June 2021 Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026

An Act to provide for time in the Republic to be 11 or 12 hours in advance of Greenwich Mean Time and connected matters.

Enacted by the Parliament of Naoero as follows:

[cl am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 1 Short title and commencement

This Act may be cited as the Naoero Standard Time Act 1978 and came into effect on 21 December 1978.

[s 1 am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 2 Interpretation

In this Act:

‘Greenwich Mean Time’ means the mean time of the meridian of Greenwich in England;

‘The meridian of Greenwich’ means the meridian of Greenwich by relation to the markers or posts marking or indicating zero degrees of longitude placed or erected at Greenwich in England by the Royal Observatory, England;

‘Naoero Alternate Time’ means 12 hours in advance of Greenwich Mean Time;

[def am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

‘Naoero Standard Time’ means 11 hours in advance of Greenwich Mean Time; and

[def am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

‘Present Time’ means 11½ hours in advance of Greenwich Mean Time.

#### 3 Time change

(1) The Cabinet may by notice in the Gazette prescribe that as from a date and time not earlier than 21 days from the date of publication of the issue of the Gazette in which such notice appears time in the Republic shall be Naoero Alternate Time or Naoero Standard Time.

[subs (1) am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

(2) A notice under subsection (1), shall be so drawn as to take effect at two of the clock ante meridiem on a Saturday as measured by the time, be it Present Time, Naoero Alternate Time or Naoero Standard Time, in force on the date on which the issue of the Gazette in which the notice appears, is published.

[subs (2) am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 4 Publicity

(1) The Cabinet shall require Radio Naoero to notify on each of the 7 days preceding the Saturday on which a notice under Section 3(1) takes effect and in such manner as the Cabinet may require, the fact that time in the Republic will change on the next following Saturday at 2 of the clock ante meridiem.

[subs (1) am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

(2) Radio Naoero shall comply with a request by the Cabinet under subsection

(1) and shall broadcast notification not less than twice a day in the English and Naoero languages, and in such other language as the Cabinet may require.

[subs (2) am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]
