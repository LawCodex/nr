# Public Investments Act 1978

## Table of Amendments

The Public Investments Act 1978 No 11 was certified and commenced on 20 December 1978.

Amending Legislation Certified Date of Commencement Revised Written Laws Act 2021 No 7 1 June 2021 1 June 2021 Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026

An Act to make Naoero Government Securities authorised investments for public bodies, statutory corporations, corporations and trustees and for associated purposes.

Enacted by the Parliament of Naoero as follows:

[cl am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 1 Short title and commencement

This Act may be cited as the Public Investments Act 1978 and came into effect on 20 December 1978.

#### 2 Interpretation

In this Act:

‘Naoero Government Securities’ means Naoero Government Stock and Treasury Bonds;

[def am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

‘Naoero Government Stock’ means stock to which the provisions of Part III of the Government Loans Act 1972 apply; and

[def am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

‘Treasury Bonds’ means bonds to which the provisions of Part IV of the Government Loans Act 1972 apply.

#### 3 Modification of existing laws

The existing laws mentioned in the first column of Schedule 2 are amended to the extent set out in the second column of such Schedule.

#### 4 Naoero Government Securities to be authorised investments

Any person may subscribe for, hold, acquire and dispose of Naoero Government Securities.
