# Criminal Justice (Amendment) Act 2020

______________________________ No. 16 of 2020 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Criminal Justice Act 1999.

Certified: 23rd October 2020 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Amendment) Act 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Criminal Justice Act 1999

The Criminal Justice Act 1999 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 2

Section 2 is amended by inserting the following in alphabetical order:

‘Minister’ means the Minister for Justice;

‘Secretary’ means the Secretary for Justice.
