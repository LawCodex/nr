# Employment and Services Tax (Amendment) Act 2020

EMPLOYMENT AND SERVICES TAX (AMENDMENT) ACT 2020 ______________________________ No. 8 of 2020 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Employment and Services Tax Act 2014.

Certified: 4th June 2020 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Employment and Services Tax (Amendment) Act 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification of the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Employment and Services Tax Act 2014

The Employment and Services Tax Act 2014 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 12

Section 12 is amended by deleting the word ‘non-resident’ where it appears in subsections (1) and (4).

#### 5 Amendment of Section 18

Section 18 is amended by deleting the word ‘non-resident’ where it appears.

#### 6 Amendment of Section 24

Section 24(2)(a) is amended by deleting the word ‘non-resident’ where is appears.

#### 7 Amendment of Section 26

Section 26 is amended by deleting the word ‘non-resident’ where it appears in subsections (2) and (3).
