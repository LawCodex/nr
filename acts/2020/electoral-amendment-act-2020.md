# Electoral (Amendment) Act 2020

______________________________ No. 2 of 2020 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Electoral Act 2016.

Certified: 28th January 2020 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Electoral (Amendment) Act 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Electoral Act 2016

The Electoral Act 2016 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 41

Section 41(3)(b) is amended by deleting ‘$150’ and substituting

‘$1,000’.
