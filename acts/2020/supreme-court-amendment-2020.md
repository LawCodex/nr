# Supreme Court (Amendment) 2020

SUPREME COURT (AMENDMENT) ACT 2020 ______________________________ No. 1 of 2020 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Supreme Court Act 2018.

Certified: 28th January 2020 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Supreme Court (Amendment) Act 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification of the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Supreme Court Act 2018

The Supreme Court Act 2018 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 10

Section 10 (1) is deleted and substituted as follows:

‘(1) Subject to subsection (2), the Chief Justice and the Judges of the Supreme Court continue to hold office until:

(a) resignation from that office;

(b) retirement;

(c) removal from that office; or

(d) attaining the age of 70 years.’
