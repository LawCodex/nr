# CUSTOMS (AMENDMENT) ACT 2020

______________________________ No. 31 of 2020 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Customs Act 2014.

Certified: 23rd October 2020 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Customs Act (Amendment) Act 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Customs Act 2014

The Customs Act 2014  is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 4

Section (4)(1) is amended  by deleting the words ‘Republic of Nauru Customs Office’ and substituting with the words ‘Nauru Customs Service’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 8

Section 8 is amended by deleting the words ‘Nauru Customs’ and substituting with the words ‘Nauru Customs Service’.

#### 6 Amendment of Section 315

Section 315 is amended by deleting the words ‘Controller of Customs’ and substituting with the words ‘Chief Collector of Customs’.

#### 7 General amendment as to references

Any reference to:

(a) ‘Republic of Nauru Customs’ in any written law shall be referred to as ‘Nauru Customs Service’;

(b) ‘Chief Collector’ in any written law shall be referred to as ‘Chief Collector of Customs’;

(c) ‘Nauru Customs’ in any written law shall be referred to as ‘Nauru Customs Service’.
