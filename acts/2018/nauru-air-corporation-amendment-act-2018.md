# Nauru Air Corporation (Amendment) Act 2018

NAURU AIR CORPORATION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2018 ______________________________ No.  6 of 2018 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Nauru Air Corporation Act 1995 Certified: 6th March 2018 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Nauru Air Corporation (Amendment) Act 2018.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Nauru Air Corporation Act 1995

The Nauru Air Corporation Act 1995 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of section 7

Section 7 is amended by:

(a) omitting and substituting subsection (1) as follows:

(1) Subject to this Act, the business of the Corporation shall be controlled by a Board of Directors constituted by 7 members appointed by the Cabinet and 2 ex officio members.

(b) inserting a new subsection (6) as follows:

(6) Of the 7 members appointed under subsection (1):

(a) one must have experience in aviation; and

(b) one must have experience in aviation and finance.

#### 5 Amendment of section 14

Section 14(3) is amended by omitting the word ‘five’ and substituting with the figure ‘7’.
