## PART 1 — PRELIMINARY

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Cenpac Corporation Act 2018.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker and came into effect on 10 May 2018.

#### 3 Definitions

In this Act:

‘Board’ has the same meaning as is defined in the Public Enterprises Act 2019;

‘Corporation’ means the Cenpac Corporation established under Section 4;

[def am Act 40 of 2025 s 4, opn 19 Sep 2025]

## PART 2 — CENPAC CORPORATION

#### 4 Establishment of Corporation

(1) There shall be established a corporation to be known as the Cenpac Corporation consisting of a Board, Chief Executive Officer and such number of staff as the Board in consultation with the Minister may from time to time determine.

(2) The Corporation is a body corporate with perpetual succession and shall have a common seal.

(3) The Corporation may:

(a) sue and be sued;

(b) enter into contracts and other legal obligations;

(c) acquire, hold, manage and dispose of real or personal properties; and

(d) perform all such powers, functions, duties and responsibilities of a corporation under the Corporations Act 1972.

(4) The affixing of the common seal of the Corporation shall be authenticated by the signature of the Chief Executive Officer.

#### 5 Functions of the Corporation

The functions of the Corporation as a commercial entity are to:

(a) establish, operate, manage and invest in communication services;

(b) establish, operate and manage a network for accessing the world wide web and international gateway for communication to and from the Republic;

(c) establish and operate as an internet service provider for wholesale and retail purposes;

(d) register, assign and allocate domain names under the Republic’s internet country code to –level – domain (ccTLD) “.nr”;

(e) establish and operate a commercial business associated with the communication and broadcasting sector or such other activities as the Corporation deems appropriate;

(f) make prudent and secured investments to ensure the viability of the Corporation;

(g) perform such other functions or exercise such other powers conferred on the Corporation under this Act;

(h) represent the Republic as a member of the East Micronesia Cable consortium agreement by the NFC Division;

(i) manage the international internet capacity supply through the East Micronesian Cable to the Republic by the NFC Division; and

(j) make recommendations and advise the Minister on matters connected with its functions.

[s 5 am Act 40 of 2025 s 5, opn 19 Sep 2025]

#### 6 Powers of the Corporation

Subject to this Act, the Corporation has the power to do all things necessary, convenient or appropriate to be done, whether in the Republic or elsewhere, for the fulfilment of its functions.

#### 7 Board of Directors

(1) The Corporation shall have a Board of directors appointed and holding office in accordance with the Public Enterprises Act 2019.

(2) The business, operations and affairs of the Corporation shall be managed by, or under the direction or supervision of, the Board.

[s 7 subst Act 11 of 2019 Sch 2 clause 1, opn 11 June 2019. Section 37 of the Public Enterprises Act 2019 applies.]

(3) The business, operations and affairs of the NFC Division of the Corporation shall be managed by or be administered under the direction and supervision of the Board.

## PART 5 — ASSETS AND LIABILITIES

#### 22 Assets and liabilities

(1) Cenpac Inc Corporation is a corporation incorporated under the Corporations Act 1972 and subject to this Act, shall continue its operations as a corporation under this Act.

(2) The Cenpac Inc Corporation is novated and reconstituted as a corporation under this Act with the following consequences:

(a) all funds previously in the custody, possession or control of Cenpac Inc Corporation shall vest in the Corporation;

(b) all property, assets, charges and interests of Cenpac Inc Corporation, whether within or outside the Republic, shall vest in the Corporation;

(c) all liability for the payment of loans and funds borrowed from creditors by Cenpac Inc Corporation, whether within or outside the Republic, shall be the debts of and be paid by the Corporation;

(d) all contracts and agreements with Cenpac Inc Corporation which are in force shall be deemed to have been novated to the Corporation established under this Act;

(e) all obligations of Cenpac Inc Corporation under any written law of the Republic is transferred to the Corporation by this enactment; and

(f) subject to subparagraph (c), the Corporation shall not be liable or responsible for any debt or liability of the Cenpac Inc Corporation other than such debt or liability duly approved by the Cabinet.

## PART 6 — NAOERO FIBRE CABLE DIVISION

[Pt 6 insrt Act 40 of 2025 s 8, opn 19 Sep 2025; am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 23 Naoero Fibre Cable Division reconstitution

(1) The Naoero Fibre Cable Corporation established under the Naoero Fibre Cable Corporation Act 2017 is reconstituted and established as the NFC Division of the Corporation under this Act.

[subs (1) am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

(2) The NFC Division as a Division is part of the body corporate of the Corporation with perpetual succession.

[s 23 am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 24 Chief Executive Officer for the Naoero Fibre Cable Division

(1) The Board after consultation with the Minister and upon approval of the Cabinet, shall appoint a person as the chief executive officer of the NFC Division.

(2) The chief executive officer of the NFC Division is responsible and accountable to the Board for the management of the business and affairs of the NFC Division.

(3) The Board shall employ the chief executive officer of the NFC Division under a contract of employment that provides for:

(a) a specified proportion of the chief executive officer’s remuneration to be payable, or a specified benefit to become due to the chief executive officer, only if:

(i) the chief executive officer achieves specified performance criteria including making the submarine cable accessible to the Republic as scheduled; and

(ii) the NFC Division achieves specified commercial outcomes; and

(b) at least an annual review of the chief executive officer’s performance and outcome which includes ensuring that internet services is accessible to the people at the most economical rate.

[s 24 am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 25 Staff

The NFC Division may employ persons to assist the Corporation in the performance of its functions and the exercise of its powers on terms and conditions as are determined by the Board on the recommendation of the chief executive officer of the NFC Division.

#### 26 Functions of the NFC Division

The functions of the Corporation which shall be administered and managed by the NFC Division are to:

(a) acquire, maintain, manage and provide telecommunication services in accordance with any prior agreements with donor agencies which are to be approved by the Board;

(b) continue to represent the Republic and the Corporation as a member of the East Micronesia Cable consortium agreement;

(c) continue to manage the submarine cable international internet capacity supply through the East Micronesian Cable to the Republic;

(d) undertake, maintain, operate and repair any works, system, facilities, apparatus or equipment required for any function mentioned in paragraphs

(a) and (c) in relation to the submarine cable;

(e) facilitate and assist the Corporation for an open access to the Naoero Cable Landing Station to duly licence telecommunications operators in the Republic to enable the Corporation to sell international internet capacity on a fair, non-discriminatory, transparent and equal price basis to duly licenced telecommunications operators in the Republic;

(f) establish the wholesale pricing for the bandwidth of the East Micronesia Fibre Cable and recommend the pricing to the Board; and

(g) continue to represent the Republic as a party to all the agreements and arrangements that has been signed by various entities including the donor Governments, for and on behalf of the Republic, in its reconstitution and establishment under this Act; and

(h) do anything that the Corporation determines to be conducive or incidental to the performance of the NFC Division’s functions, which were formerly performed by the Naoero Fibre Cable Corporation under the repealed Naoero Fibre Cable Corporation Act 2017.

[s 26 am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 27 Powers

The NFC Division has all the powers necessary for the performance of its functions under this Act.

#### 28 Revenue of the NFC Division

(1) The revenue of the NFC Division shall be:

(a) the income received from the wholesale sale of internet capacity of the submarine cable to retail service providers by itself or through other Divisions of the Corporation;

(b) funds provided by the Corporation from its other Divisions for the purposes of acquiring any capital or general operational expenditure; and

(c) any revenues appropriated by the Parliament for the specific purpose of the NFC Division.

(2) The Corporation shall keep an account of the NFC Division separate from other accounts which the Corporation operates for the purposes of carrying out its functions.

(3) An account kept by the NFC Division shall be an account of the Corporation separate from its other Division and business activities.

(4) All monies payable to and received by the NFC Division shall be paid into the NFC Division account with the Corporation, that has been approved for the purposes by the Cabinet.

(5) The NFC Division shall:

(a) submit annual budget proposals to the Board when it is so required to be done; and

(b) provide finance and accounts updates to the Board on a regular basis or as may be required by the Board. s 26LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

#### 29 Account record keeping

(1) The NFC Division of the Corporation shall keep written financial records that:

(a) correctly record and explain its transactions and financial position and performance;

(b) enables financial statements to be prepared and audited in accordance with this Act and the Public Enterprises Act 2019; and

(c) enables other reports required by this Act to be prepared.

(2) The records required under subsection (1):

(a) shall be kept for at least 7 years after the dates of the transactions to which they relate;

(b) shall be kept at the principal place of business of the NFC Division; and

(c) may be kept in electronic form if they are readily retrievable and convertible into hard copy form.

#### 30 Annual Reports

The NFC Division shall prepare an annual report for approval of the Board to be published annually as part of the annual report of the Corporation. s 30 CENPAC CORPORATION ACT 2018
