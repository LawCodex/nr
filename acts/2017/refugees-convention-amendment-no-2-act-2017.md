# Refugees Convention (Amendment) No.2 Act 2017

REFUGEES CONVENTION (AMENDMENT) No. 2 ACT 2017 ______________________________ No. 20 of 2017 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Refugees Convention Act 2012 Certified: 14th September 2017 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Refugees Convention (Amendment) No. 2 Act 2017.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of Refugees Convention Act 2012

The Refugees Convention Act 2012 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of section 3

Omit

‘Secretary’ means the Head of Department;

Substitute

‘Secretary’ means the Secretary for Multicultural Affairs;
