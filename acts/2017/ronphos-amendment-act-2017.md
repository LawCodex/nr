# RONPHOS (Amendment) Act 2017

______________________________ No. 22 of 2017 ______________________________ An Act to amend the RONPHOS Act 2005 Certified: 14th September 2017 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the RONPHOS (Amendment) Act 2017.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the RONPHOS Act 2005

The RONPHOS Act 2005 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of section 11D

Section 11D(3) is amended by omitting the numeral ‘5’ and substituting with the numeral ‘3’.

The clause will now read:

‘(3) A quorum of the Board consists of 3 directors’
