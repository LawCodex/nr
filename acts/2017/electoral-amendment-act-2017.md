# Electoral (Amendment) Act 2017

______________________________ No. 9 of 2017 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Electoral Act 2016 Certified: 5th May 2017 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Electoral (Amendment) Act 2017.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Electoral Act 2016

The Electoral Act 2016 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of section  84

Section 84(1) is amended by omitting the words ‘except a by-election’.

#### 5 Repeal of section 86

Section 86 is hereby repealed.

#### 6 Repeal of section 87

Section 87 is hereby repealed.
