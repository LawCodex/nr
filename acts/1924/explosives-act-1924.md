#### 1 Short title and commencement

This Act may be cited as the Explosives Act 1924 and came into effect on 1 June 1924.

[s 1 subst Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1 clause 57, opn 15 Apr 2011]

#### 2 Permit required to import, keep, and etc, explosives

From and after the coming into force of this Act, no person, firm, or company shall import into the Republic, keep, or store, or be in actual or constructive possession of any explosives of any nature whatsoever unless the written permission of the Minister has been first obtained.

[s 2 am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1 clauses 60, 61, opn 15 Apr 2011; Act 18 of 2011 s 3 and Sch clause 9, opn 3 Nov 2011]

#### 3 Storage of explosives

All explosives shall be stored or kept in such place or places, and in such quantities, as shall be approved by the Minister or officer deputed by him or her.

[s 3 am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1 clause 61, opn 15 Apr 2011]

#### 4 Explosives not to be used for fishing

From and after the coming into force of this Act, no person, firm, or corporation shall use or employ any explosives for the purpose of fishing.

[s 4 am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1 clause 60, opn 15 Apr 2011]

#### 5 Use of explosives for blasting purposes

(1) Whenever explosives are being used for blasting purposes, it shall be the duty of the competent person present, in actual charge of the work, to see that all persons within the possible danger area are duly warned by some system that is clearly and definitely understood by all concerned, and that all such persons are in safety before the fuses are lit, and that they are not permitted to leave the safety zone unless and until the person in charge has satisfied himself or herself that all charges have been exploded, or otherwise satisfactorily accounted for.

(2) Blasting operations within 150 yards of any road or path accessible to the public are prohibited, unless in addition to the precautions prescribed in subsection (1):

(a) the road or path is clear of traffic, and the approaches guarded by a red flag or other suitable signal arrangements as may be approved, in writing, by the Minister well in view of any person approaching the danger area; such flag to be hoisted at least 3 minutes and not more than 5 minutes prior to the shot being fired, and to be taken down immediately after the shot has been fired; and

(b) notice boards are erected at each approach to the danger area with printed notices legibly painted thereon, in English and Naoero, warning persons that they are not to pass beyond such notices whenever the red flag is flying.

6 Penalty The penalty for any breach or non-observance of this Act is a fine of not less than $10, and not more than $100 and imprisonment for a period not exceeding 6 months.
