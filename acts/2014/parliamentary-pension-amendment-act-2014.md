# Parliamentary Pension (Amendment) Act 2014

_________________________ Act No. 21 of 2014 _________________________ Contents

## SCHEDULE – AMENDMENT OF PARLIAMENTARY PENSION ACT 2008

[1] Amendment of section 8 ….……………………………………………………………………………....1 REPUBLIC OF NAURU Parliamentary Pension (Amendment) Act 2014 _________________________ Act No. 21 of 2014 _________________________ An Act to amend the Parliamentary Pension Act 2008 Certified 29 October 2014 Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Parliamentary Pension (Amendment) Act 2014.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date on which it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Act amended

The Schedule amends the Parliamentary Pension Act 2008.

## SCHEDULE – AMENDMENT OF PARLIAMENTARY PENSION ACT 2008

[1] Amendment of section 8 1.1 Amend section 8 (2) Omit

‘$150’ Substitute

‘$300’ New clause will now read:

(2) Subject to sub-section (3) and (4), the rate of the Parliamentary Pension shall be $300 per fortnight. 1.2  Amend section 8 (3) Omit

‘$1.50’ Substitute

‘$5.00’ New clause will now read:

‘In addition to the basic rate payable under subsection (2), a former Member of Parliament shall be entitled to $5.00 per fortnight for each full year (in excess of 9 years) of service as a Member of Parliament.’ 1.3 Amend section 8 (4) Omit

‘$1.50’ Substitute

‘$5.00’ New clause will now read:

‘In addition to payment provided for under subsections (2) and (3) hereof, a former Member of Parliament shall be entitled to $5.00 per fortnight for each full year of service as the Deputy Speaker. Speaker, Minister or President.’
