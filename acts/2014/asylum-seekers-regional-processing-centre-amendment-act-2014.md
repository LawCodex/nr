[13] Insert new section 19C 19C      Preservation of privacy and dignity

(1) An authorised officer who searches a person must, as far as is reasonably practicable in the circumstances, comply with this section.

(2) The authorised officer must inform the person to be searched of the following matters:

(7) A person must be allowed to dress as soon as a search is finished.

(8) If clothing is seized because of the search, the authorised officer must ensure the person searched is left with or given reasonably appropriate clothing.
