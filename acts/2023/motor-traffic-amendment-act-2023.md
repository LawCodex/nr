# Motor Traffic (Amendment) Act 2023

_____________________________ No.  8 of 2023 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Motor Traffic Act 2014.

Certified: [7 June 2023]

Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Motor Traffic (Amendment) Act 2023.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Motor Traffic Act 2014

The Motor Traffic Act 2014 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 5

Section 5(1) is amended by:

(a) in the definition of ‘authorised officer’, deleting ‘of Motor Vehicles’; and

(b) deleting the definition of ‘Registrar’ and substituting the following definition:

‘‘Registrar’ means the Registrar of Motor Vehicles and Licences, who shall, for the purposes of this Act and Regulations, be the Secretary for Transport;’.

#### 5 Deletion of Section 6

Section 6 is deleted.

#### 6 Amendment of Section 11

Section 11(4) is amended by deleting ‘approved garage’ and substituting

‘Registrar’.

#### 7 Amendment of Section 13

Section 13(1) is amended by deleting ‘approved garage receiving a registration application and conducting vehicle inspection’ and substituting ‘Registrar’.

#### 8 Amendment of Section 16

Section 16 is amended by:

(a) in subsection (1), deleting ‘(1) An approved garage conducting vehicle inspections and registering vehicles under this Act, shall keep a Register containing the following information about each motor vehicle it has registered the’ and substituting ‘The Registrar shall keep a register containing the following information in relation to each registered motor vehicle’; and

(b) deleting subsections (2) and (3).

#### 9 Amendment of Section 92

Section 92(3) is deleted and substituted as follows:

‘(3) A policy under subsection (1) shall not cover liability in excess of $40,000 for any claim made by or in respect of any or all passengers.’.
