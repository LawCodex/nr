# Agricultural Quarantine (Amendment) Act 2023

AGRICULTURAL QUARANTINE (AMENDMENT) ACT 2023 ______________________________ No.  7 of 2023 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Agricultural Quarantine Act 1999.

Certified: [7 June 2023]

Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Agricultural Quarantine (Amendment) Act 2023.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Agricultural Quarantine Act 1999

This Act amends the Agricultural Quarantine Act 1999.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 2

Section 2 of the Act is amended by inserting the definitions of the terms

‘fixed penalty’, ‘fixed penalty notice’ and ‘fixed penalty offence’ as follows:

‘‘fixed penalty’ means the penalty payable by a person who is issued a fixed penalty notice for the commission of a fixed penalty offence;

‘fixed penalty notice’ means an Agricultural Quarantine Fixed Penalty Notice;

‘fixed penalty offence’ means an offence prescribed as such by regulations;’

#### 5 Insertion of Section 31A

Section 31A is inserted after Section 31 as follows:

‘31A  Agricultural Quarantine Fixed Penalty Notices

(1) A Quarantine Officer may issue a fixed penalty notice to a person who:

(a) commits a fixed penalty offence; and

(b) is liable to pay a fixed penalty.

(2) The Cabinet may make regulations prescribing:

(a) fixed penalty offences for which fixed penalties shall be imposed;

(b) fixed penalties for corresponding fixed penalty offences;

(c) the fixed penalty notice which a Quarantine Officer may serve on a person for a fixed penalty offence;

(d) additional penalties to be imposed by the court on a person who has failed to pay the fixed penalty in accordance with a fixed penalty notice;

(e) the procedures for dealing with a fixed penalty notice; and

(f) any other matter which shall ensure the effective enforcement of a fixed penalty notice.’
