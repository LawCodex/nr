# Sports Act 2026

## Table of Amendments

The Sports Act 2026 No 3 was certified and commenced on 14 January 2026.

Amending Legislation Certified Date of Commencement

An Act to provide for the promotion and development of sports in the Republic and for related purposes.

Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

## PART 1 — PRELIMINARY

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Sports Act 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date of certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Objectives of this Act

The objectives of this Act are to:

(a) provide for the functions and powers of the Secretary in the development and promotion of sports in the Republic;

(b) establishment of a Register for Sports Bodies;

(c) provide for the application process for registration of a sport body;

(d) provide for process whereby sports bodies can apply for assistance from the Government;

(e) provide for the establishment of a Sports Disputes Tribunal, its functions and powers in relation to hearing and determining disputes between sports bodies and any misconduct; and

(f) such other matters in relation to regulating sports and promoting the development of sports in the Republic.

#### 4 Interpretation

‘athlete’ means a person who competes in any sport at the international or national level competition or any sports competition;

‘athlete support personnel’ means:

(a) any coach, trainer, manager, agent, team, staff, official, medical, paramedical personnel and includes persons who offers support to athletes during games; or

(b) any other person working with, treating or assisting an athlete participating in or preparing for a sports competition at the international or national level;

‘contact details’ includes residential address, phone number and email address;

‘contact person’ means the person appointed by a sport body to be the focus point of contact for the Department for any matter required under this Act;

‘Department’ means the Department responsible for Sports;

‘Minister’ means the Minister responsible for this Act;

‘official’ means a person who controls or assists with the control of the actual play of a sport and administers the rules and laws of the sport to ensure the proper conduct of a sport in a safe environment;

‘Registrar’ means Registrar for the Register of Sports Bodies as provided under Section 7;

‘Secretary’ means the Secretary responsible for Sports;

‘sports bodies’ or ‘sport body’ means a sport club or a sport federation eligible to be registered under this Act;

‘sport club’ means an association registered under the Registration of Associations Act 2020 which has no affiliations with international or regional sports bodies;

‘Sports Dispute Tribunal’ means the Sports Dispute Tribunal established under Section 23; and

‘sport federation’ means an association registered under the Registration of Associations Act 2020 and has affiliations with international or regional sports bodies. s 4LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

## PART 2 — ADMINISTRATION

#### 5 Functions of the Secretary

The functions of the Secretary are to:

(a) promote the development of any sport;

(b) coordinate with national, regional and international sports bodies and institutions to promote the development of sports;

(c) develop programmes and initiatives to promote the positive impacts of sports on education, in collaboration with the Department responsible for education and other relevant stakeholders;

(d) develop programmes and initiatives to promote the benefit of sports in maintaining health, in collaboration with the Department responsible for Health and other relevant stakeholders;

(e) promote principles of good governance in sports by issuing guidelines and putting in place appropriate measures to achieve this;

(f) work in collaboration with relevant stakeholders to ensure implementation of antidoping measures;

(g) provide policy advice to the Minister for the development of any sport including the promotion of potential economic benefits of sports;

(h) implement policies approved by the Cabinet relating to sports;

(i) ensures the athletes, athlete support personnel, official and sport body comply with policies, guidelines, sports rules or sport body’s constitution or article of association; and

(j) perform such other functions as may be prescribed or conferred under this Act.

#### 6 Powers of the Secretary

The Secretary has the power to do all things necessary or convenient to be done for the performance of his or her functions under this Act or any other written law.

## PART 3 — REGISTRATION AND REGISTER OF SPORTS BODIES

#### 7 Registrar of Sports Bodies

(1) There shall be a Registrar for the Register of Sports Bodies.

(2) The Registrar shall be the Secretary.

#### 8 Functions of the Registrar

The Registrar shall:

(a) keep and maintain the Register;

(b) perform the functions and duties conferred on him or her under this Act;

(c) do all things as are necessary to be done for the proper administration of this Act; and

(d) keep and maintain the records for applications and other documents required to be lodged or filed by sports bodies seeking registration or have been registered under the Act.

#### 9 Powers of the Registrar

The Registrar has the power to do all things necessary or convenient to be done for the performance of his or her functions under this Act or any other written law.

#### 10 Register of Sports bodies

(1) There shall be a Register of Sports Bodies.

(2) The Register shall contain:

(a) the name of the sport body;

(b) person nominated or authorised to register the sport body;

(c) the certificate of registration of the sport body as an association under the Registration of Associations Act 2020;

(d) the contact details of the contact person for the sport body; and

(e) such other matters as may be prescribed.

(3) Upon the payment of prescribed fees:

(a) the Register shall be made available for inspection by any person during business hours; and

(b) certified copies of the entries in the Register be provided.

(4) The particulars registered in the Register and the records kept and maintained by the Registrar shall be evidence of the record of the sport body kept and maintained by the Registrar.

(5) A copy of or extract from any document contained in the Register certified to be a true copy or extract under the hand and seal of the Registrar is admissible as evidence in any legal proceedings as if it were the original document or a part of the document as extracted.

(6) In any legal proceedings, a certificate issued under the hand and seal of the Registrar is prima facie evidence of the fact the requirements of this Act specified in the certificate:

(a) had or had not been complied with at a date or within a period specified in the certificate; or

(b) had been complied with, but at a date later than that specified in the certificate.

(7) The Registrar may refuse to accept any document and request that the document be amended, completed and relodged or a new document be lodged, if he or she is of the opinion that the document:

(a) contains matters contrary to the law;

(b) by reason of any omission or misdescription has not been duly completed;

(c) does not comply with requirements of this Act; or

(d) contains any error, alteration or erasure.

(8) Where the Registrar of Associations notifies the Secretary that a sport body has been de-registered under the Registration of Associations Act 2020, the Secretary shall also de-register such body from the Register of Sports Bodies.

(9) The Cabinet may make regulations in relation to the use, processing and disclosing of any information required under subsection (2).

#### 11 Application for registration

(1) A sport body may apply to the Registrar to be registered under this Act.

(2) An application for registration shall:

(a) be in the prescribed form;

(b) provide the prescribed information;

(c) provide copies of documents required for registration;

(d) be declared and signed by the person stated in subsection (3); and

(e) accompanied by the prescribed fee.

(3) The statement of particulars shall be declared and signed by an executive member of the sport body authorised by the sport body.

(4) The application for registration of a sport body shall be accompanied by a certificate in the prescribed form authorising the executive member of the sport body to apply for registration and for the purposes of subsection (3).

(5) The Registrar may require and the sport body shall provide such further information.

#### 12 Decision on the application for registration

(1) Upon receipt of an application under Section 11, the Registrar shall consider the application and within 14 days from the date the application is receive, it may:

(a) grant the application;

(b) require further information before granting or declining the application; or

(c) decline the application.

(2) The Registrar in determining an application shall consider the following:

(a) requirements provided under Section 11;

(b) requirements provided under the Registration of Associations Act 2020; and

(c) such information as requested from the applicant.

(3) The Registrar may grant the certificate of registration to a sport body, where he or she considers that the application satisfies the requirements under this Act. s 10LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

(4) Where a certificate of registration is granted under subsection (3), the Registrar shall:

(a) issue the certificate to the sport body; and

(b) enter the record of the issue of the certificate in the Register.

(5) Where the Registrar declines the application, he or she shall provide reasons for the decision.

(6) The Registrar may decline to grant a certificate of registration to a sport body on any one or more of the following grounds:

(a) where the sport body has not provided information required under the Act;

(b) where the information provided by the sport body is not complete, is false or is misleading; and

(c) for such other reason as determined by the Registrar.

(7) The Registrar shall as soon as practicable after a certificate has been issued to the sport body, include in the Register the following details:

(a) the name and address of the sport body;

(b) the certificate number; and

(c) the duration of the certificate.

#### 13 Duration of registration

The certificate of registration is valid for 12 months from the date the certificate is granted.

#### 14 Renewal of registration

Applications for the renewal of certificate of registration shall be made:

(a) at least 30 days prior to the expiry date of the licence;

(b) in the prescribed form; and

(c) on the payment of the prescribed fees.

#### 15 Suspension of registration

(1) Where a sport body contravenes a provision of the Act or fails to comply with conditions of a funding agreement approved under this Act, the Registrar shall:

(a) suspend the certificate of registration;

(b) serve a notice of suspension to the sport body; and

(c) allow the sport body to provide reasons why its certificate of registration should not be cancelled.

(2) Where a sport body:

(a) continues to fail to comply with this Act; or

(b) does not provide reasons why its certificate of registration should not be suspended, the Registrar shall serve a notice of the cancellation of the certificate of registration.

#### 16 Alteration or cancellation of a registration

The Registrar may alter or cancel a certificate of registration of a sport body where the Registrar is satisfied that the sport body:

(a) is not complying with this Act in a material manner; s 16 SPORTS ACT 2026

(b) fails to comply with conditions of a funding agreement approved under this Act; or

(c) requests the Registrar to alter or cancel its certificate of registration.

#### 17 Registration conditions

(1) A sport body shall comply with the following conditions:

(a) the provisions of the Act; and

(b) any such conditions as determined by the Registrar.

(2) Where a sport body fails to comply with any of the conditions under subsection (1), the Registrar may suspend or cancel the certificate of registration in addition to any penalty that may be imposed.

#### 18 Registrar to approve the restoration of a cancelled certificate

of registration

(1) The Registrar may restore the cancelled registration of a sport body upon:

(a) an application by the sport body in the prescribed form to the Registrar;

(b) payment of the prescribed fee; and

(c) approval of the Registrar.

(2) The Registrar shall require the sport body to comply with the requirements of this Act or any other written law, before the Registrar restores its registration in the Register.

#### 19 Surrender of certificate of registration

(1) Where a sport body ceases to continue, it shall notify the Registrar in writing and surrender the certificate of registration.

(2) Where a certificate is surrendered under subsection (1), the registration of the sport body shall be deemed to have lapsed and shall be cancelled by the Registrar.

#### 20 Record keeping requirements

(1) The Secretary shall keep a record of all matters required for the purposes of this Act.

(2) The records shall be kept and maintained in:

(a) electronic form or digital form; and

(b) manual physical form or in case of electronic or digital form, capable of being produced in printed form.

(3) The Secretary shall keep and maintain the records for a period of at least 7 years.

(4) The Secretary shall not disclose or share information with any third party except where it is required by a law enforcement agency, for any other official purpose, by the Republic or any proceedings.

(5) Any information provided to the Republic under this Act, shall not be disclosed to any other person without the prior consent or approval of the Secretary, except where the information is required as exhibits or evidence in court. s 16LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

## PART 4 — ASSISTANCE TO SPORTS BODIES

#### 21 Application by sports bodies for assistance

(1) A sport body registered under Part 3, may in accordance with the prescribed requirements apply to the Secretary for assistance.

(2) The Secretary shall in accordance with the prescribed procedure make a determination on the application.

#### 22 International assistance

(1) Subject to subsection (2), any assistance from an international body for a sports body shall be channelled through the Department in accordance with the relevant written laws and government policies.

(2) A sports body shall provide to the Secretary a report of the use of the international assistance.

(3) An assistance is exempted from the requirement under subsection (1) where such is provided by an international body:

(a) which has in its regulatory framework or rules the requirements that there shall be no Government interference or involvement in facilitating or receiving of such assistance; or

(b) where its international standards provides that the involvement of Government jeopardises the standing of the sports body with the international body.

## PART 5 — SPORTS DISPUTES TRIBUNAL

#### 23 Establishment of the Sports Disputes Tribunal

(1) The Sports Disputes Tribunal shall be established.

(2) The Sports Disputes Tribunal shall consist of 3 members.

(3) The members of the Sports Disputes Tribunal shall be appointed by the Minister in consultation with the Cabinet.

(4) The members of the Sports Disputes Tribunal shall be constituted by the following persons:

(a) a legal practitioner admitted to the Supreme Court of Nauru and has practiced for 5 years or more, who shall be the Chairperson;

(b) a person with 3 years’ experience in the management of a sport body or relevant field; and

(c) a representative of the sports bodies or community.

(5) The appointment of members of the Sports Disputes Tribunal shall be for a period of 3 years and a member is eligible to be re-appointed.

(6) A member may resign from office in writing to the Secretary.

(7) A member may be entitled to a reasonable allowance as may be determined by the Cabinet.

(8) The Secretary shall be the secretary to the Sports Disputes Tribunal.

#### 24 Functions of the Sports Disputes Tribunal

The functions of the Sports Disputes Tribunal are to:

(a) hear and determine disputes to be prescribed relating to sports or sports administration;

(b) hear and determine any misconduct by:

(i) a sport body;

(ii) an athlete; or

(iii) an athlete support personnel;

(c) hear and determine any complaints relating to sports or sports administration; and

(d) such other functions as may be prescribed by regulations.

#### 25 Powers of the Sports Disputes Tribunal

The Sports Disputes Tribunal has the power to do all things necessary or convenient to be done for the performance of its functions under this Act or any other written law.

#### 26 Practice and procedure

(1) The Sports Disputes Tribunal may establish the practice and procedure for investigation, hearing and determination of any complaints under this Act.

(2) The practice and procedure referred to in subsection (1) may be prescribed by the Cabinet.

#### 27 Disclosure of interest

(1) Where a member has any interest relating to a matter, the member shall disclose the details of his or her interest as soon as practicable after the member becomes aware that he or she has an interest.

(2) A disclosure under subsection (1), shall be recorded and the member shall after the disclosure, not take part in any deliberation or decision of the Sports Disputes Tribunal in respect of that matter.

#### 28 Appeal

A person who is aggrieved by a decision of the Sports Disputes Tribunal may appeal to the Minister within 21 days from the date of the decision of the Sports Disputes Tribunal. s 27LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

## PART 6 — MISCELLANEOUS

#### 29 Protection from liability

The Minister, Secretary, Registrar, members of the Sports Disputes Tribunal and staff of the Department are not liable for any loss or damage arising from or be subject to any criminal prosecution and civil claim, for the performance of a function or exercise of a power, in good faith under this Act or any other written law.

#### 30 Confidentiality

(1) The Minister, Secretary, Registrar, members of the Sports Disputes Tribunal and staff of the Department shall keep information he or she accesses in the course of performing his or her functions or exercise of his or her powers under this Act, confidential.

(2) Upon authorisation by the Secretary, information may be disclosed for the purpose of furthering the objectives of this Act, required by a law enforcement agency or required by an order of the court.

#### 31 Regulations

(1) The Cabinet may make regulations to prescribe all matters necessary or convenient to be prescribed to give effect to this Act.

(2) Without limiting subsection (1), the Cabinet may make regulations to provide for:

(a) procedures of the Sports Disputes Tribunal;

(b) fees payable under the Act;

(c) matters in relation to regulation or promotion of sports; and

(d) such other matters necessary and convenient for achieving the purposes of this Act.

#### 32 Savings provision

At the commencement of this Act any policies already in place are deemed to have been made under this Act and continue to be in force until such policies are revoked or replaced by new policies in relation to such matters.
