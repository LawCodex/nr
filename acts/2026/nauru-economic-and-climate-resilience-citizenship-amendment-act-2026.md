# Nauru Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship (Amendment) Act 2026

NAURU ECONOMIC AND CLIMATE RESILIENCE CITIZENSHIP (AMENDMENT) ACT 2026 _____________________________ No. 13 of 2026 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Nauru Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Act 2024 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 31 March 2026 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Nauru Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship (Amendment) Act 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Nauru Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Act

2024 The Nauru Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Act 2024 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 3

Section 3 is amended in the definition of ‘Minister’ by deleting ‘Justice and Border Control’ and substituting with ‘Nauru Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Program Office’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 5

Section 5(2) is deleted and substituted with:

‘(2) Where the Minister is satisfied that the requirements of the Act have not been met, he or she shall not grant citizenship to a person applying for citizenship under this Act.

(3) A person shall not be granted citizenship where he or she has been involved in any manner whatsoever on matters contained in Section 23(1)(b).’.

#### 6 Consequential amendments

(1) Regulation 16(5) and (6) of the Nauru Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Regulations 2024 is deleted and substituted as follows:

‘(5) For applications which were not endorsed by the Registrar, the Registrar shall submit this to the Minister to review the recommendation and non-endorsement and direct the Registrar in writing to notify the Program Office that the application has been refused.

(6) Where the Minister has made a decision under subregulation (5), the Registrar shall for the purpose of Section 5(2) of the Act, inform the Program Office that the Minister has rejected the application for citizenship.’.

(2) Regulation 16(9) is amended by deleting the ‘the Act’ where second occurring and substitute with ‘these Regulations’.
