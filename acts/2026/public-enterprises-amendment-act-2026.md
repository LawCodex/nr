# Public Enterprises (Amendment) Act 2026

PUBLIC ENTERPRISES (AMENDMENT) ACT 2026 _____________________________ No. 23 of 2026 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Public Enterprises Act 2019 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 9 June 2026 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Public Enterprises (Amendment) Act 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Public Enterprises Act 2019

The Public Enterprises Act 2019 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 14

Section 14 is amended by deleting subsection (2) and substituting with the following:

‘(2) No act or transaction creating any liability or guarantee shall be undertaken except in accordance with the process provided under the Public Debt Act 2026.’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 19

Section 19 is deleted and substituted with the following:

‘19 Responsible Minister shall approve certain activities

(1) A public enterprise shall obtain approval of the Responsible Minister before undertaking any of the following:

(a)  make an investment of an amount exceeding 30% of the value of the assets of the public enterprise as set out in its most recent audited financial statements;

(b)  incorporate a subsidiary;

(c)  be a party to an agreement to form a joint venture;

(d)  make an investment of any amount outside the Republic; or

(e)  anything that is prescribed by regulations as requiring the prior approval of the Responsible Minister under this Section.

(2) A public enterprise shall ensure compliance with the Public Debt Act 2026 before undertaking any of the following:

(a)  borrowing as defined under the Public Debt Act 2026; or

(b)  lending or on-lending.

(3)  The Responsible Minister shall consult with the Accountable Minister and the Cabinet before exercising the power to approve under subsection (1).’.

#### 6 Amendment of Section 28

Section 28(1)(k) is amended by deleting ‘Treasury Fund Protection Act 2004’ and substituting with ‘Treasury Fund Protection Act 2004, Public Debt Act 2026’.

#### 7 Amendment of Section 37

Section 37 is amended to insert a new subsection (2A) as follows:

‘(2A) Each Board shall have a secretary.’.

#### 8 Amendment of Section 46

Section 46 is deleted and substituted with the following:

‘46 Remuneration The remuneration, allowances and other benefits of directors and secretaries of public enterprises and its subsidiaries shall be prescribed by regulations.’.

#### 9 Amendment of Section 85

Section 85 is amended as follows:

(a) by deleting ‘and’ at the end of paragraph (e);

(b) by deleting paragraph (f) and substituting with:

‘(f)  prescribing remuneration, allowances and other benefits of directors and secretaries of public enterprises and its subsidiaries;

(g) to amend Schedule 1; and

(h)  providing for any other matters that are contemplated by, necessary for, or incidental to, giving full effect to this Act and its proper administration.’.
