# Constitution (Amendment) (No.2) Act 2026

CONSTITUTION (AMENDMENT) (NO.2) ACT 2026 _____________________________ No. 21 of 2026 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Constitution of Nauru 1968 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 12 May 2026 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Constitution (Amendment) (No.2) Act 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Constitution of Nauru 1968

The Constitution of Nauru 1968 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of the Constitution

The Constitution is amended by deleting all references to ‘Nauru’ and substituting with ‘Naoero’.

#### 5 Consequential amendments

A reference in any written law to ‘Nauru’ is deleted and substituted with ‘Naoero’.

#### 6 Insertion of Article 102

Article 102 is inserted as follows:

‘102    Transitional provision relating to amendment of reference to ‘Nauru’ to  be substituted with ‘Naoero’

(1) At the commencement of the Constitution (Amendment) (No.2) Act 2026, a reference in any:

(a) Agreement, Arrangement or Understanding in which the Government is a party, to the term ‘Nauru’ shall be deemed to be a reference to the word ‘Naoero’;

(b) contract in which the Government is a party whether through an elected official, a public official, a Head of Department, Chief Executive Officer or Board member of a corporation or an instrumentality of the Republic, to the term ‘Nauru’ shall be deemed to be a reference to the word ‘Naoero’; and

(c) registry or other record required by a written law to be established, kept and maintained, to the term ‘Nauru’ shall be deemed to be a reference to the word ‘Naoero’.

(2) No existing right, obligation, proceeding, decision, action, appointment or other matter undertaken under the Constitution prior to the commencement of the Constitution (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 2026 shall be invalidated by its enactment.’.
