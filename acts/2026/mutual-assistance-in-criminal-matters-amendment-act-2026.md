# Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters (Amendment) Act 2026

REPUBLIC OF NAOERO MUTUAL ASSISTANCE IN CRIMINAL MATTERS (AMENDMENT) ACT 2026 _____________________________ No. 29 of 2026 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters Act 2004 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 20 July 2026 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Naoero as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters (Amendment) Act 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters Act 2004

The Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters Act 2004 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 9

Section 9 is amended by deleting ‘serious offence’ and substituting with ‘criminal matter’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 49

The definition of ‘court’ is amended by inserting ‘Naoero’ before ‘court’.

#### 6 Amendment of Section 50

Section 50 is amended by inserting ‘Naoero’ before ‘court’.

#### 7 Amendment of Section 51

Section 51 is deleted and substituted as follows:

‘51 Requirements for testimony from a foreign country

(1) The testimony shall be taken before a court in the foreign country concerned:

(a) on oath or affirmation; or

(b) under any caution or admonition as would be accepted by courts in the foreign country concerned, for giving testimony in proceedings before such courts.

(2) The testimony may be taken in closed court of the foreign country concerned.’.

#### 8 Amendment of Section 53

Section 53 is amended as follows:

(a) by inserting ‘Naoero’ before ‘court’s’ in paragraph (a); and

(b) by inserting ‘Naoero’ before ‘court’ in paragraph (c).

#### 9 Amendment of Section 54

Section 54 is amended as follows:

(a) by inserting ‘Naoero’ before ‘court’ wherever it appears; and

(b) by inserting ‘Naoero’ before ‘court’s’ in subsection (1).

#### 10 Amendment of Section 56

Section 56 is amended by inserting ‘Naoero’ before ‘court’ wherever it appears.
