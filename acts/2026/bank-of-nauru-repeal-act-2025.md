# Bank of Nauru Repeal Act 2025

## Table of Amendments

The Bank of Nauru Repeal Act 2025 No 6 was certified and commenced on 29 January 2026.

Amending Legislation Certified Date of Commencement

An Act to repeal the Bank of Nauru Act 1976 and related purposes.

Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

## PART 1 — PRELIMINARY

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Bank of Nauru Repeal Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

## PART 2 — REPEAL AND TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### 3 Repeal of Bank of Nauru Act 1976

The Bank of Nauru Act 1976 is repealed.

#### 4 Extinguishment of Guarantees

Any guarantee of depositor obligations under Section 4 of the Bank of Nauru Act 1976 is extinguished as such the Republic is discharged from any such obligation, and no claim survives or may be made against the Republic under that Section after the commencement of this Act.

#### 5 Savings of completed transactions

The repeal of the Bank of Nauru Act 1976 does not affect the validity of any act lawfully done, or any right or liability lawfully discharged, under that Act before its repeal.

#### 6 Residual Matters

The Cabinet may, by regulation, provide for the resolution of any residual matter arising from the repeal of the Bank of Nauru Act 1976, but no regulation may revive or reopen the Bank or its obligations including matters contained in Section 4 of this Act.

#### 7 Consequential amendments

(1) The Banking (Application for Licence) Regulations 1977 is amended by deleting Regulation 2(3).

(2) The Payments Directorate Act 2008 is repealed and all assets, income sources and equipment of the Bank vested in the Republic under such Act shall not be affected by its repeal.
