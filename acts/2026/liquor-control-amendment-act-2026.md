# Liquor Control (Amendment) Act 2026

_____________________________ No. 16 of 2026 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Liquor Control Act 2017 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 16 April 2026 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Liquor Control (Amendment) Act 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Liquor Control Act 2017

The Liquor Control Act 2017 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 91

Section 91 is deleted and substituted with the following:

‘91 Closed days Any sale or supply of liquor or kava on any of the following days is prohibited:

(a) Good Friday;

(b) the Saturday following Good Friday;

(c) Easter Monday;

(d) the Tuesday following Easter Monday;

(e) Sunday; and

(f) any other day or portion of a day specified for the purposes of this Section by the Cabinet by notice in the Gazette.’.
