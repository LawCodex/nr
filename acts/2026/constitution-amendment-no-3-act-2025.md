# Constitution (Amendment) (No. 3) Act 2025

CONSTITUTION (AMENDMENT) (No. 3) ACT 2025 _____________________________ No.  8 of 2026 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Constitution of Nauru 1968 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 26 March 2026 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Constitution (Amendment) (No. 3) Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Constitution of Nauru 1968

The Constitution of Nauru 1968 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Insertion of Article 84A

Article 84A is inserted after Article 84 as follows:

‘84A Revision of the Constitution in certain circumstances

(1) The provisions of this Constitution may be reviewed by the Parliament, in accordance with this Article.

(2) The Parliament may review a proposed law which has not been approved under Article 84(3) of the Constitution, where such law is required to be made for the peace, order and good government.

(3) The Cabinet may make a resolution for the President to recall the proposed law under clause (2) of this Article, to be considered by the Parliament.

(4) The President may submit a resolution of the Cabinet in clause (3) in writing to the Speaker, proposing a review of a provision of the Constitution which falls within clause (2) of this Article, where:

(a) the two thirds majority was not reached in the referendum;

(b) at least 40% of the votes cast supported the proposed amendment; and

(c) the Cabinet is still desirous of pursuing the proposed amendment.

(5) Where the Parliament on a motion by the President allows such motion to be put for a special debate, the Parliament shall consider the proposed law.

(6) The proposed law shall be debated and passed by the Parliament unanimously.

(7) The proposed law shall not be submitted for a certificate of the Speaker under Article 47 unless it has been passed by the Parliament unanimously in accordance with this Article.

(8) The Speaker shall be required to vote for the purposes of any proposed law within clause (2).

(9) Where the proposed law is passed unanimously by the Parliament, the Clerk of the Parliament shall provide a certificate to the Speaker that the requirements of clause (7) of this Article has been complied with and the proposed law to which clause (2) applies, shall be certified by the Speaker under Article 47.

(10) The power of the Parliament to review under this Article is contained in the Seventh Schedule.

(11) For the purposes of this Article:

(a) a ‘review’ is a revision of a provision of the Constitution under this Article and not an amendment of the Constitution under Article 84; and

(b) ‘unanimous’ means the votes of all Members of Parliament including the Speaker.

(12) This Article shall not be amended except where the amendment is supported by at least two-thirds of the total number of members of Parliament and the requirements of Article 84(2)(a) is complied with.

(13) This Article may not be repealed except where the repeal is supported by at least two-thirds of the total number of members of Parliament and the requirements of Article 84(2)(a) is complied with.’.

#### 5 Insertion of Article 101

Article 101 is inserted after Article 100 as follows:

‘101 Transitional Provision relating to Article 84A

(1) The Parliament may exercise its power of review under Article 84A for a proposed law:

(a) which failed a referendum under Article 84(3);

(b) that was submitted for a referendum under Article 84 within 12 months prior to the commencement of the Constitution (Amendment) (No. 3) Act 2025; and

(c) becomes law immediately and effective upon the completion of the review process, duly enacted by the Parliament under Article 84A and certified by the Speaker.

(2) Notwithstanding provisions of any other written law or of the Constitution that are inconsistent with the effect of this Article, this Article shall prevail to the extent of such inconsistency.’.

#### 6 Insertion of the Seventh Schedule

The Seventh Schedule shall be inserted as follows:

‘Seventh Schedule (Article 84A) The Parliament shall have the power to review any provisions of the Constitution contained in the Fifth Schedule, except the following provisions of the Constitution, where a proposed law failed under Article 84(3):

(a) Part I;

(b) Part II;

(c) Articles 16 and 17;

(d) Articles 26 and 27;

(e) Articles 58, 59, 60, 62 and 65;

(f) Article 71; and

(g) Clause (1) of Article 72;

(h) Article 84;

(i) Clauses (1), (2), (3), (4) and (5) of Article 85; and

(j) Article 93.’.
