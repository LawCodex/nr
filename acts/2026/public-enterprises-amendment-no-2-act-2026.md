# Public Enterprises (Amendment) (No.2) Act 2026

REPUBLIC OF NAOERO PUBLIC ENTERPRISES (AMENDMENT) (NO.2) ACT 2026 _____________________________ No. 27 of 2026 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Public Enterprises Act 2019 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 20 July 2026 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Naoero as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Public Enterprises (Amendment) (No.2) Act 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Public Enterprises Act 2019

The Public Enterprises Act 2019 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 3

Section 3 is amended by inserting the following definition in its alphabetical order:

‘‘remuneration’ means payment such as salary or allowance, reward, recompense for services rendered, and any other benefits a person receives for carrying out duties and services in relation to his or her employment and appointment;’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 38

Section 38 is deleted and substituted with the following:

‘38 Eligibility for appointment as a director

(1) A person is eligible to be appointed as a director where he or she:

(a) is financially literate, that is able to read and understand financial statements, financial budget and determine financial risks;

(b) has the ability to understand and contribute to formulation of annual report, business plan and statement of corporate intent;

(c) has the experience in managing risk and ensuring compliance with administrative or lawful requirements;

(d) has adequate understanding of a director’s obligations under the Act and the public enterprise framework;

(d) has adequate interpersonal skills;

(e) has proven experience relevant to operations, scale and strategic context of the public enterprise to which they are to be appointed;

(f) has experience in executive or senior level management;

(g) has experience in governance, strategic analysis and committee roles;

(h) has experience in leadership or decision making role within a District or the community; and

(i) is not disqualified under subsection (2).

(2) A person is disqualified from being appointed as a director where the person:

(a) is under 18 years of age;

(b) is not a natural person;

(c) is an undischarged bankrupt in any jurisdiction;

(d) has been convicted in any jurisdiction of an offence of dishonesty and sentenced to imprisonment for not less than 12 months;

(e) is prohibited under a written law or by order of a court from being a director or promoter of, or being concerned or taking part in the management of, a corporation;

(f) is deemed mentally impaired under Section 4 of the Mental Health Act 1963;

(g) is a Minister in Government, a Member of Parliament or any other elected public official;

(h) is a public service employee in the department of the public service with policy or operational responsibility for the principal business of the public enterprise; or

(i) is a public service employee if his or her appointment would result in more than 2 directors, or more than half of total directors appointed at any time, of the public enterprise who are public service employees.

#### 6 Amendment of Section 46

Section 46 is amended by deleting ‘remuneration, allowances and other benefits of’ with ‘remuneration of Chairpersons,’.

#### 7 Amendment of Section 61

Section 61(3) is deleted and substituted with the following:

‘(3) A Board meeting shall be chaired by:

(a) the chairperson; or

(b) in the chairperson’s absence, a director appointed by the directors present.’.

#### 8 Transitional and savings provision

(1) At the commencement of this Act, a person who is appointed as a Deputy Chairperson for any Board of a public enterprise may continue with such appointment until his or her appointment is terminated.

(2) No person shall be appointed as a Deputy Chairperson after the commencement of this Act.
