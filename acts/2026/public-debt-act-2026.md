An Act to provide for the control and management of public debt in the Republic and for other related purposes.

## PART 1 — PRELIMINARY

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Public Debt Act 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Objectives of this Act

The objectives of this Act are to provide for the regulation and governance of public debt according to sound public finance and debt management principles, and debt transparency to ensure financial stability and accountability.

#### 4 Application

(1) This Act applies to any borrowings:

(a) by the Government through a Department or an instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise; and

(b) within or outside of the Republic.

(2) Where there is an inconsistency between any other written law and this Act with regard to the regulation of public debt, this Act shall prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.

(3) For the avoidance of doubt and in accordance with Section 14 of the Public Enterprises Act 2019, compliance by a public enterprise or instrumentality of the Republic with this Act shall not make the Republic liable in any capacity for any debt, obligation or liability incurred by that public enterprise or instrumentality of the Republic for any borrowing pursuant to this Act.

#### 5 Interpretation

In this Act:

‘borrowing’ means the:

(a) signing of direct credit or loan agreements with financial institutions, development partners, or other legal entities in line with applicable provisions;

(b) issuance of Treasury securities on domestic and international markets; and

(c) other debt instruments as determined and approved by the Minister;

‘contingent liability’ means:

(a) uncalled capital;

(b) guarantees and indemnities;

(c) legal proceedings and disputes; and

(d) unquantifiable contingent liabilities;

‘debt charges’ means interest payments, sinking fund charges, repayment or amortisation of debt and all expenditure in connexion with the raising of loans and securities, and the service and redemption of the debt;

‘Financial Instruction: Liabilities’ means the Instruction prescribed under Section 26;

‘Fiscal Agent’ refers to an agent which can be a central bank, domestic or foreign financial institution that undertakes financial operations including, but not limited to, issuance, registration, clearing and settlement of Treasury securities on behalf of Finance;

‘public debt’ means all financial liabilities created as a result of borrowing by the Government of Naoero and its instrumentalities pursuant to this Act;

[def am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

‘hedging transaction’ means a financial transaction undertaken for the purpose of reducing or offsetting risks arising from public debt, including risks related to interest rates, exchange rates or other market exposures, and includes the use of such mechanisms and instruments as are prescribed in the Financial Instruction:

Liabilities;

‘liability’ means a future payment, or financial claim, which is established when the Government is obliged, under specific circumstances, to provide funds or other resources to the creditor;

‘Minister’ means the Minister responsible for this Act;

‘Public Debt Strategy’ means a strategic plan designed to operationalise high level objectives for debt management, taking into account the cost and risk associated with the public debt portfolio and the financing of the Government’s borrowing requirements over the medium term;

‘public enterprise’ has the meaning given under the Public Enterprises Act 2019;

‘Secretary’ means the Secretary for Finance;

‘soft loans’ means loans that are provided on concessional terms; and

‘Treasury securities’ includes Treasury bills, Treasury bonds, and Government promissory notes as prescribed in the Financial Instruction: Liabilities. s 5LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

## PART 2 — AUTHORISATION, DELEGATION OF POWERS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### 6 Authority to borrow

(1) The procedure for approving the borrowing of money on behalf of the Government through a Department or an instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise, shall be undertaken in accordance with this Section.

(2) The Minister shall seek approval of the Cabinet to table a motion in Parliament, to approve the borrowing of money on behalf of the Government through a Department or an instrumentality of the Republic or a public enterprise.

(3) Where a public enterprise is incorporated under the Corporations Act 1972 and operates through a Board of directors, the Board shall submit any proposal for borrowing to the Minister, who shall submit to the Cabinet in accordance with subsection (2).

(4) The instruments for borrowing may be by:

(a) concluding loan agreements;

(b) issuing Treasury securities;

(c) entering into credit agreements;

(d) pledging to provide Government loan guarantees; or

(e) any other instrument determined by the Minister.

(5) The Minister may borrow money on behalf of the Government through a Department or an instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise, subject to meeting the requirements of subsection (2).

(6) The Minister shall table in Parliament a statement of the Government’s borrowing plan for the next ensuing financial year together with an appropriation law pursuant to Article 59 of the Constitution.

(7) In accordance with Article 58 of the Constitution, and the Treasury Fund Protection Act 2004, all revenues and monies raised or received by or on behalf of the Republic including loans, soft loans, grants and any other form of financial receipt, shall be paid into the Treasury Fund or in the case of public enterprises to their respective established accounts and shall be properly recorded by the Minister.

(8) All debt charges for which the Republic is liable as a result of borrowing, shall be charged on the Treasury Fund.

#### 7 Delegation of Powers of the Minister

(1) Subject to subsection (2), the Minister’s power to borrow money on behalf of the Government through a Department or an instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise under Section 6(5) shall not be delegated.

(2) The Minister may appoint an agent to execute an instrument on his or her behalf in relation to the Minister’s powers to borrow money.

#### 8 Management of public debt

(1) The Secretary shall manage all operations related to public debt, including but not limited to:

(a) borrowing of money;

(b) management of public debt;

(c) lending and on-lending; and

(d) provision of Government guarantees.

(2) The Minister may appoint a Fiscal Agent to manage transactions related to Treasury securities under a Fiscal Agency Agreement signed by the Minister, to undertake the roles and responsibilities as may be prescribed in the Financial Instruction: Liabilities. s 8LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

## PART 3 — PUBLIC DEBT MANAGEMENT

#### 9 Obligation of the Minister and Secretary in relation to public

debt management

(1) The Minister and the Secretary shall manage public debt:

(a) in a manner that ensures Government financing needs and payment obligations are met at the lowest possible cost over the medium to long term; and

(b) to ensure that risks in the public debt portfolio remain at acceptable levels.

(2) For the purpose of subsection (1)(b), the acceptable level of risks shall be provided under the Public Debt Strategy established under Section 10.

#### 10 Establishment of Public Debt Strategy

(1) The Secretary shall establish an annual Public Debt Strategy covering the current and the 2 next financial years consecutively.

(2) The Public Debt Strategy shall be submitted to the Cabinet for approval.

(3) The Minister shall table the Public Debt Strategy in Parliament, after it is approved by the Cabinet.

(4) The Strategy shall prescribe:

(a) guidelines and specified targets for the composition of the Government’s debt portfolio;

(b) guidelines and specified targets for new debt issuance as set out in the annual borrowing plan;

(c) policies and guidelines for lending, on-lending, and the provision of loan guarantees; and

(d) any additional matters prescribed in the Financial Instruction:

Liabilities.

(5) The Secretary shall monitor the public debt portfolio and report to the Minister on compliance with the risk limits set out in the Public Debt Strategy.

## PART 4 — BORROWING BY THE GOVERNMENT

#### 11 Borrowing purposes

The borrowing pursuant to Section 6 shall be for the following purposes:

(a) finance Government Budget deficits;

(b) finance projects and investments;

(c) maintain a credit balance in the Treasury Account at a level determined by the Minister;

(d) refinance outstanding debt or repay a loan prior to its date of repayment;

(e) replenish the international foreign exchange reserves held by the Government where necessary;

(f) honor obligations under outstanding Government loan guarantees;

(g) provide such Government loans or credits to Government Departments, instrumentalities of the Republic and public enterprises, and any other entities as may be approved by Parliament;

(h) immediately protect, mitigate or eliminate effects caused by a natural or environmental disaster or any other national emergency; or

(i) finance any other purpose as stipulated in the appropriation law approved by Parliament.

#### 12 Liability Management Operations

(1) The Minister, with the consent of the lender in regard to loans for which the Government or the Republic may be liable or agrees to pay and upon such terms and conditions, shall have the right to:

(a) repay a loan prior to its date of repayment;

(b) renegotiate the terms and conditions of the loan; or

(c) convert debt or consolidate debt.

(2) The Minister, in regard to Treasury securities, shall have the right to:

(a) repurchase a Treasury security prior to the maturity date;

(b) repurchase a Treasury security and reissue another Treasury security prior to the maturity date; or

(c) convert a Treasury security from one form to another.

(3) The Minister may undertake hedging transactions to address the risks associated with public debt transactions.

(4) The Minister shall not undertake a hedging transaction unless Cabinet and Parliament have granted prior approval.

## PART 5 — LENDING AND ON-LENDING

#### 13 Requirements of lending and on-lending

(1) A Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise shall not undertake lending or on-lending except in accordance with this Section.

(2) A Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise shall submit to the Secretary a lending or on-lending request that provides:

(a) the details of the loan required; and

(b) a technical plan and a financial plan for the relevant investment project, program or initiative.

(3) The Secretary shall assess and recommend to the Minister the approval of all lending and on-lending.

(4) No lending or on-lending shall be undertaken unless it has been approved by the Parliament.

(5) The procedure to be followed including the information to be provided by a Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise for lending or on-lending shall be prescribed in the Financial Instruction: Liabilities.

(6) The Secretary shall assess and monitor the lending or on-lending and advise the Minister of associated risks.

(7) Notwithstanding subsection (4) and Section 6(2), the Cabinet may, upon the recommendation of the Minister, approve an Emergency Short-Term Liquidity Advance provided by the Government, or provided by a public enterprise to another public enterprise, without prior Parliamentary approval, subject to the following restrictions:

(a) such advance is intended for the Government or another public enterprise;

(b) the principal amount of such advance shall not exceed the amount of AUD 500,000 per public enterprise;

(c) the advance shall be fully repaid within 90 days from the date of the drawdown; and

(d) the advance shall strictly comply with the urgency criteria, reporting requirements, and operational procedures prescribed in the Financial Instruction: Liabilities.

#### 14 Terms and Conditions for lending and on-lending

All other terms and conditions including the interest and other related fees for lending and on-lending shall be prescribed in the Financial Instruction:

Liabilities.

## PART 6 — GOVERNMENT LOAN GUARANTEES

#### 15 Requirement for Government Loan Guarantees

(1) A Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise shall not provide a loan guarantee except in accordance with this Section.

(2) A Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise shall submit to the Secretary a loan guarantee request that provides:

(a) the details of the loan to be guaranteed; and

(b) a technical plan and a financial plan for the relevant investment project, program or initiative.

(3) The Secretary shall assess and recommend to the Minister the approval of the loan guarantee.

(4) No loan guarantee shall be made by a Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise unless it has been approved by the Parliament.

(5) The procedure to be followed including the information to be provided by a Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise for a loan guarantee shall be prescribed in the Financial Instruction: Liabilities.

(6) The Secretary shall assess and monitor the loan guarantee and advise the Minister of associated risks.

#### 16 Terms and Conditions for loan guarantees

All other terms and conditions for the provision of loan guarantees, including the charging of guarantee fees, shall be set out in the Financial Instruction:

Liabilities.

## PART 7 — BORROWING BY GOVERNMENT DEPARTMENT, INSTRUMENTALITY OF THE REPUBLIC OR PUBLIC ENTERPRISE

17 Submission of borrowing request by Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise

(1) A Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise shall not seek to borrow money except in accordance with this Section.

(2) A Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise shall submit to the Secretary a borrowing request that provides:

(a) the details of the proposed borrowing; and

(b) a statement that identifies the impact of the borrowing on its balance sheet, profit and loss, and capacity to service the borrowing obligations.

(3) The Secretary shall assess the request and prepare a fiscal risk report, for submission to the Minister together with a recommendation to approve or decline the borrowing proposal, and the report shall set out the Government’s gross financial exposure and, where feasible, an estimate of the likely fiscal cost of default by the agency or enterprise.

(4) The Minister shall submit the borrowing proposal to Parliament for approval.

(5) Upon approval by Parliament, the Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise may proceed with the borrowing.

(6) The Secretary shall assess and monitor the borrowing and advise the Minister of associated risks.

#### 18 Recording, monitoring and reporting of Government

Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise borrowings

(1) Upon the signing of a borrowing agreement by a Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise, the Secretary shall record the borrowing details provided by the Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise.

(2) The Secretary shall monitor the outstanding amount of all borrowings by the Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise, including the disbursed amounts and repayment profiles.

(3) A Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise shall, within 3 months after the end of each financial year, submit to the Secretary a report for the period ending on 30 June that:

(a) sets out all new borrowings undertaken during the financial year; and

(b) states the total amount of borrowings outstanding at the end of the financial year.

(4) The Secretary shall include details of the total borrowings by a Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise in the Strategy prepared under Part 3.

## PART 8 — CASH AND LIQUIDITY MANAGEMENT

#### 19 Cash Management

(1) The Secretary shall track and manage the cash balances in all Government accounts, and such balances shall be classified as:

(a) total donor funds;

(b) total available funds;

(c) treasury available funds;

(d) the cash buffer requirement.

(2) In cash management, the Secretary shall ensure that the Government has sufficient funds available in the appropriate accounts at the time required, to meet all financial obligations in the most cost-effective manner.

(3) The Secretary is required to observe the objectives of cash management which are to:

(a) ensure that adequate cash is available to pay for expenditures when they fall due;

(b) borrow only when necessary and to minimise Government borrowing costs;

(c) maximise returns on idle cash; and

(d) manage risks by investing temporary cash surpluses productively and securely.

(4) The Secretary shall determine the amount of surplus funds available for investment and any such investment shall require the agreement and approval of the Minister.

(5) The Secretary shall maintain a list of approved investments, which shall be updated annually and approved by the Minister, and the approved list shall be prescribed in the Financial Instruction: Liabilities.

#### 20 Liquidity Management

(1) The Government shall maintain a minimum cash liquidity buffer to be approved by the Minister.

(2) The Financial Instruction: Liabilities shall prescribe how the cash liquidity buffer is to be maintained.

## PART 9 — ACCOUNTING, BUDGETING, DEBT SERVICING AND REPORTING

#### 21 Accounting

(1) The Secretary shall record all public debt, lending, on-lending and loan guarantees, and all financial transactions related to public debt, lending, on-lending and Government loan guarantees, in the Government accounts in accordance with the Budget classifications and the chart of accounts.

(2) The accounting records for all financial transactions related to public debt, lending, on-lending and Government loan guarantees shall be prescribed in the Financial Instruction: Liabilities.

(3) The Secretary shall apply the financial reporting standards for maintaining the accounts for public debt as may be prescribed by the Minister by Notice published in the Gazette.

#### 22 Budgeting and Debt Servicing

(1) The Secretary shall require Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise to provide forecasts of debt-service payments and disbursements in accordance with the Budget instructions issued by the Minister.

(2) The Secretary shall formulate a forecast of the cost of servicing public debt for inclusion in the Budget, but the absence of, or any deficiency in, a Budget allocation shall not impede, in accordance with the applicable financial regulations, the timely payment of principal, interest and all borrowing expenses in respect of funds borrowed by the Government.

#### 23 Annual Reporting

(1) The Secretary shall, no later than 6 months after the end of each financial year, submit to the Minister an annual report on public debt and debt-related activities, loan guarantees, lending and on-lending, and a list of contingent liabilities for submission to Parliament.

(2) The Minister shall upon approval of the Cabinet table the Report in Parliament.

(3) The annual report shall include:

(a) the objective of the Strategy and its rationale;

(b) information on the contribution of the Strategy and debt-management activities to achieving the debt-management objectives;

(c) the terms and conditions of all new borrowing contracted during the financial year;

(d) a list of all outstanding borrowing by Government Departments, instrumentalities of the Republic and public enterprises;

(e) a list of all outstanding lending and on-lending and a list of all loan guarantees issued by the Government; and

(f) a list of contingent liabilities, including:

(i) uncalled capital;

(ii) guarantees and indemnities;

(iii) legal proceedings and disputes; and

(iv) unquantifiable contingent liabilities. s 23LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

## PART 10 — MISCELLANEOUS

#### 24 Offence for entering into an agreement, arrangement or

understanding without Cabinet approval

(1) Subject to the requirements of this Act, a person, shall not enter into an agreement, arrangement or understanding on behalf of any Government Department, instrumentality of the Republic or public enterprise which has any implication of the matters governed by this Act, without the prior approval of the Cabinet and Parliament as required under this Act.

(2) A person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and shall be liable upon conviction:

(a) for an individual to a fine not exceeding $10,000 or an imprisonment term not exceeding 3 years, or both; or

(b) for a body corporate to a fine not exceeding $50,000.

#### 25 Record keeping

(1) The Secretary shall keep a record of all matters relating to the management of public debt, under this Act.

(2) The records shall be kept and maintained in:

(a) electronic or digital form capable of being produced in printed form; and

(b) printed form.

#### 26 Financial Instruction: Liabilities

(1) The Cabinet may prescribe the Financial Instruction: Liabilities to prescribe matters required as necessary for the implementation of this Act.

(2) The Secretary shall maintain and update, at least once every 2 years, the Financial Instruction: Liabilities.

(3) The Financial Instruction: Liabilities shall prescribe the methodology and procedures for the management of all borrowings under this Act including:

(a) public debt;

(b) lending and on-lending;

(c) the provision of loan guarantees;

(d) any other financial liabilities of the Government that are not the result of borrowing; and

(e) any other related matter deemed necessary by the Secretary.

#### 27 Offences and penalties

(1) A person shall not:

(a) initiate negotiations for borrowing without the prior consent of the Minister;

(b) enter into a borrowing agreement without the prior consent of the Minister, Cabinet and Parliament respectively;

(c) borrow for a purpose other than a purpose under Part 4;

(d) fail to pay the moneys raised from a borrowing in accordance with this Act; or

(e) fail to submit information to the Secretary or Minister respectively, in accordance with this Act.

(2) A person in contravention of subsection (1) commits an offence and shall be liable upon conviction:

(a) for an individual to a fine not exceeding $10,000 or an imprisonment term not exceeding 3 years, or both; or

(b) for a body corporate to a fine not exceeding $50,000.

(3) A person who does or purports to undertake any activity in contravention of any provision of this Act other than subsection (2), commits an offence and shall be liable upon conviction:

(a) for an individual to a fine not exceeding $10,000 or an imprisonment term not exceeding 3 years, or both; or

(b) for a body corporate to a fine not exceeding $50,000.

#### 28 Repeal

The Government Loans Act 1972 is repealed.

#### 29 Savings and Transitional Provisions

(1) Any loan raised, and any stock or bond issued, under the Government Loans Act 1972 shall continue in force as if raised or issued under this Act, and all rights and obligations relating to those loans, stock and bonds shall continue unaffected by the repeal.

(2) All assets, liabilities, records and accounts of the Government Loans Fund established under Section 5 of the Government Loans Act 1972, shall at the commencement of this Act, be deemed to be assets, liabilities, records and accounts of the corresponding fund established under this Act.

(3) Any order, notice, authorisation or direction made under the Government Loans Act 1972, including an order made under Section 3 authorising the raising of a loan, shall continue in force until amended or revoked under this Act.

(4) Any registry, Stock Ledger, entry, inscription, transfer, transmission or certificate created or maintained under the repealed Act shall continue to have full force and effect as if created or maintained under this Act.

(5) The repeal of the Government Loans Act 1972, does not affect any right, entitlement, liability or obligation accrued or incurred under that Act before its repeal.

(6) Any proceedings, claim or enforcement action relating to stock, bonds, loans or offences committed under the Government Loans Act 1972 may be commenced or continued as if the Government Loans Act 1972 remained in force. s 27LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO
