# Tobacco Control (Amendment) Act 2026

_____________________________ No.  20 of 2026 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Tobacco Control Act 2009 and for other related purposes.

Certified: []

Table of Provisions

#### 5 Consequential amendments to the Tobacco Control (Fixed Penalty Notices) Regulations 2023 .. 3

Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows: 1 Short title This Act may be cited as the Tobacco Control (Amendment) Act 2026. 2 Commencement This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker. 3 Amendment of the Tobacco Control Act 2009 The Tobacco Control Act 2009 is amended by the provisions of this Act. 4 Insertion of new Section 18A After Section 18 insert a new Section 18A as follows:

‘18A Closed days

(1) The Cabinet may by notice in the Gazette prohibit the import, distribution and sale of tobacco product, cigar, e-cigarette and adulterated smokeless tobacco product on a specific day or portion of a day.

(2) A person shall not import, distribute or sell tobacco product, cigar, e- cigarette or adulterated smokeless tobacco product on the specified day in a notice issued by the Cabinet under subsection (1).

(3) A person who contravenes subsection (2) commits an offence and is liable upon conviction:

(a) in the case of an individual who is a licensee:

(i) a fine not exceeding $2,000 or a term of imprisonment not exceeding 12 months or both; or

(b) in the case of an individual who is not a licensee:

(i) a fine not exceeding $2,000 or a term of imprisonment not exceeding 12 months or both; and

(ii) forfeiture of any remaining tobacco product, cigar, e-cigarette or adulterated smokeless tobacco product; or

(c) in the case of a body corporate, a fine not exceeding $50,000.

(4) In this Section:

‘adulterated smokeless tobacco product’ means any smokeless tobacco product containing additives or substances harmful to health which includes, snuff, chewing tobacco, and branded products such as Copen whether marketed under that name or any substantially similar rebranding or any similar product;

‘cigar’ means a roll of cut tobacco for smoking enclosed in a tobacco leaf or the leaf of another plant; and

‘e-cigarette’ has the meaning given to it under Section 12B of this Act.’. 5 Consequential amendments to the Tobacco Control (Fixed Penalty Notices) Regulations 2023 Schedule 1 is amended by inserting the following item:
