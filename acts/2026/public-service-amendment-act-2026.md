# Public Service (Amendment) Act 2026

REPUBLIC OF NAOERO PUBLIC SERVICE (AMENDMENT) ACT 2026 _____________________________ No. 28 of 2026 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Public Service Act 2016 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 20 July 2026 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Naoero as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Public Service (Amendment) Act 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Public Service Act 2016

The Public Service Act 2016 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 4

Section 4(1) is amended by inserting the following definition in its alphabetical order:

‘ ‘unattached employee’ means a public service employee who is designated by the Chief Secretary as an unattached employee under Section 37A; ’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 10

Section 10 is amended as follows:

(a) for paragraph (b), delete ‘and’;

(b) for paragraph (c), delete the fullstop ‘.’ and substitute with ‘; and’; and

(c) insert new paragraph (d) as follows:

‘(d) unattached employees.’.

#### 6 Insertion of new Division 5 under Part 5

A new Division 5 is inserted after Section 37 as follows:

‘DIVISION 5 – UNATTACHED EMPLOYEES 37A Unattached employee

(1) The Chief Secretary may designate a public service employee as an unattached employee where such person’s public service position is:

(a) abolished under Section 16; or

(b) changed in classification under Section 16 and such public service employee was not appointed to the reclassified position.

(2) The Chief Secretary shall provide the terms and conditions for the employment of an unattached employee.

(3) An unattached employee shall continue to be entitled to the salary, leave entitlement and other benefits he or she was entitled to under his or her substantive public service position before it was abolished or changed in classification.

(4) A designation under this Section shall be for a period of 3 months.

(5) Subject to subsection (6), the Chief Secretary shall appoint or transfer an unattached employee to a public service position.

(6) Where an unattached employee does not agree to an appointment under subsection (5), such employee shall resign in accordance with Section 83(1).

(7) An unattached employee that accepts the appointment under subsection (5), shall upon commencement of his or her employment in the public service position no longer be deemed an unattached employee.’.

#### 7 Amendment of Section 89

Section 89(1) is deleted and substituted with:

‘(1) Where a permanent employee is not designated as an unattached employee under Section 37A, this Section applies to such permanent employee whose public service position is abolished under Section 16(1)(b).’.
