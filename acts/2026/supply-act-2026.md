# Supply Act 2026

## Table of Amendments

The Supply Act 2026 No 25 was certified on 9 June 2026 and commenced on 1 July 2026.

Amending Legislation Certified Date of Commencement

An Act to provide for the withdrawal and expenditure of funds from the Treasury Fund to meet the expenditure of the Republic, necessary to carry on the services of the Republic after the commencement of the Financial Year 2026–2027.

Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Supply Act 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on 1 July 2026.

#### 3 Expiry of the Act

This Act expires on any of the following dates, which arrives earlier:

(a) 30 September 2026; or

(b) the commencement of the appropriation law for the financial year 2026–2027.

#### 4 Withdrawal of funds from the Treasury Fund

An amount not exceeding $91,786,171 may be withdrawn from the treasury Fund and expended as authorised in the 3rd column of the Schedule.
