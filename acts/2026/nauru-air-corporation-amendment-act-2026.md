# Nauru Air Corporation (Amendment) Act 2026

NAURU AIR CORPORATION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2026 _____________________________ No. 4 of 2026 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Nauru Air Corporation Act 1995 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 14 January 2026 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Nauru Air Corporation (Amendment) Act 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Nauru Air Corporation Act 1995

The Nauru Air Corporation Act 1995 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 4

Section 4 is amended by inserting the following definition after the definition for

‘Minister’:

‘ ‘Nauru-Australia Treaty’ means the Treaty signed between the Government of Nauru and the Government of Australia at the Parliament House in Canberra, Australia on 9 December 2024; ’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 7

Section 7(1) is amended by inserting ‘, one of which shall be the Director General appointed for the purposes of the Nauru-Australia Treaty’ after ‘2019’.
