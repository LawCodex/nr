# Civil Aviation (Amendment) Act 2026

_____________________________ No.  12 of 2026 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Civil Aviation Act 2011 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 26 March 2026 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Amendment) Act 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Civil Aviation Act 2011

The Civil Aviation Act 2011 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 6

Section 6 is amended as follows:

(a) by inserting the following new definitions in its correct alphabetical order:

‘‘e-cigarette’ has the meaning given to it under the Tobacco Control Act 2009;

‘tobacco product’ has the meaning given to it under the Tobacco Control Act 2009; and’; and

(b) by deleting the definition of ‘smoke’ and substituting it with the following:

‘‘smoke’ or ‘smoking’ means:

(a) puffing or inhaling and expelling the smoke of any tobacco product or using, holding of or control over any ignited tobacco product; or

(b) use, activate or inhaling from an e-cigarette;’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 87

Section 87 is amended by substituting with the following: '87 Designated officers The Chief Commissioner may designate a person to be a designated officer for an investigation.’.

#### 6 General amendment to the Act

All references to the term ‘authorised officer’ throughout the Act are deleted and substituted with ‘designated officer’.
