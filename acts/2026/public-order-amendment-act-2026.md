# Public Order (Amendment) Act 2026

_____________________________ No. 14 of 2026 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Public Order Act 2025 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 31 March 2026 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Public Order (Amendment) Act 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Public Order Act 2025

The Public Order Act 2025 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 4

Section 4 is amended by inserting the following definition in its alphabetical order:

‘‘exempt activity’ means an activity that is prescribed by regulations for the purposes of Section 15;

‘Minister’ means the Minister responsible for this Act;’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 9

Section 9(5) is amended by deleting ‘subsection (1)’ and substitute with ‘a Noise Abatement Direction issued under subsection (3),’.

#### 6 Amendment of Section 15

Section 15 is deleted and substituted as follows:

‘15 Exemption to noise nuisance

(1)  Notwithstanding Sections 8 and 9, an exempt activity prescribed by regulations shall not constitute a noise nuisance for the purposes of this Part.

(2)  A person who carries out an exempt activity shall not be liable for an offence under Sections 8 and 9.

(3)  A person shall not undertake an exempt activity where the person does not have an exempt activity licence.

(4)  The Commissioner of Police with the approval of the Minister, may issue an exempt activity licence to a person for an exempt activity and such licence shall state the duration for which the exempt activity may be undertaken.

(5) A person who contravenes subsection (3) commits an offence and shall be liable upon conviction to a fine of:

(a) $1,000 or an imprisonment term not exceeding 18 months, or both for a first offence; and

(b) more than $1,000 but not exceeding $5,000 or an imprisonment term not exceeding 3 years, for a subsequent offence.

#### 16 Regulations

(1)  The Cabinet may make regulations prescribing all matters necessary or convenient to be prescribed to give effect to this Act.

(2)     Without limiting subsection (1), the Cabinet may make regulations to provide for matters relating to:

(a)     noise abatement direction, including the form to be used for such directions; and

(b)     fixed penalties including:

(i) offences for which fixed penalties shall be imposed;

(ii) penalties for each corresponding prescribed fixed penalty offences;

(iii) fixed penalty notice which may be served to an offender for an offence for which a fixed penalty is prescribed;

(iv) a time frame for the payment of prescribed fixed penalty;

(v) procedure for the prosecution of and additional penalties to be imposed by the court on offenders defaulting to pay the prescribed penalty in accordance with the fixed penalty notice;

(vi) procedures for dealing with fixed penalty notice;

(vii) powers of police officers to enforce such fixed penalty notice; and

(viii) any other matters in relation to the effective enforcement of the fixed penalty notice.’.
