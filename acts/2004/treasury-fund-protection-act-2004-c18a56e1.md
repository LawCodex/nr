An Act to ensure compliance with the Constitution in financial matters and protect against the unauthorised withdrawal of monies from the Treasury Fund.

Enacted by the Parliament of Naoero as follows:

[cl am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 1 Short title

This Act may the cited as the Treasury Fund Protection Act 2004.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act came into effect on 21 May 2004 upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Withdrawal of monies from the Treasury Fund without

Parliamentary approval A person shall not make, direct or permit a withdrawal of any money from the Treasury Fund, or any other Fund established by Parliament or the Constitution, unless the withdrawal has been authorised by an Act of Parliament.

Penalty: $10,000 or imprisonment for 3 years or both.

[s 3 am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[150], opn 15 Apr 2011]

#### 4 Cabinet not to authorise withdrawal after 30 September in any

year

(1) The Cabinet may, in respect of a financial year commencing 1 July, recommend to Parliament a proposed law authorising the withdrawal of monies from the Treasury Fund for the purpose of meeting expenditure necessary to carry on the services of the Republic after the commencement of that financial year until the expiration of 3 months or the coming into operation of the appropriation law, whichever is the earlier.

(2) Where:

(a) a proposed law recommended in accordance with subsection (1) has not come into effect by 1 July of that financial year; and

(b) the appropriation law has not come into operation on or before 1 July of that financial year, the Cabinet may authorise the withdrawal of monies in accordance with the proposed law referred to in paragraph (a) but the amount of monies so withdrawn shall not exceed one-quarter of the amount withdrawn under the authority of the appropriation law or laws in respect of the preceding financial year.

(3) The authorisation of the Cabinet under subsection (2) cannot be exercised after 30 September in any year.

(4) An authorisation given by the Cabinet contrary to this Section is a breach of subsection (1) and every member of the Cabinet shall be liable as a principal offender.

#### 5 Cabinet not to give orders or directions contrary to the

Constitution Where the Cabinet gives any order for the withdrawal of monies from the Treasury Fund or any other Fund established by law without the authorisation of a law made by Parliament, each member of the Cabinet is liable for the offence of conspiracy to subvert the Constitution.

Penalty: $50,000 or imprisonment for 5 years or both

#### 6 Public officers not to advise Cabinet or other persons to

subvert the Constitution

(1) Any public officer who provides advice and comfort to the Cabinet or any Minister that any action he or she may take or authorise that is in breach of this Act or of the Constitution is not so in breach or informs the Cabinet or Minister of ways and means to so subvert the Constitution or breach this Act without appearing so to do, commits an offence.

Penalty: $10,000 or imprisonment for 3 years or both.

(2) A public officer who is found guilty of an offence under this Act shall cease to be a member of the public service and may not be so employed, or employed in an instrumentality of the Republic for a period of 5 years.

(3) It is a defence to a prosecution under this Act that the accused satisfies the court that the advice so given was based on a belief that was genuinely held and was reasonably so held.

[subs (3) am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[150], opn 15 Apr 2011]

(4) This Section has effect notwithstanding anything contained to the contrary in the Public Service Act 2016 or any other written law in the Republic.

#### 7 Revenues and monies raised or received by the Republic to be

paid into the Treasury Fund and recorded

(1) All revenues and monies raised or received by the Republic in accordance with Article 58 of the Constitution including, loans, soft loans, grants, etc, shall strictly be paid into the Treasury Fund and properly recorded. If the President, Minister or the Cabinet directs otherwise or if any public servant including a consultant, on his or her own volition or on the instructions of the political functionaries stated above, does anything contrary to the provisions of this Section, he or she commits an offence.

Penalty: $10,000 or imprisonment for 3 years or both.

[subs (1) am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[150], opn 15 Apr 2011]

(2) A statement of all revenues and other monies paid into the Treasury Fund shall be caused to be prepared and laid before Parliament by the Minister within one month after the end of each financial year notwithstanding the fact that the detailed and audited yearly statement of all accounts relating to public monies shall be laid separately in Parliament by the Minister.

(3) This Section has effect notwithstanding anything contained to the contrary in any other written law in the Republic.

#### 8 Cabinet not to authorise the mortgage of property of the

Republic or instrumentalities or give guarantees

(1) No mortgage or other charge over property of the Republic or of an s 5LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO instrumentality of the Republic has any force or effect at law or equity unless the mortgage or charge and the purpose thereof have been approved by Parliament.

[subs (1) am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[150], opn 15 Apr 2011]

(2) No guarantee given by the Cabinet on behalf of the Republic has any force or effect or binds the Republic, unless and until the guarantee and its purpose, have been approved by Parliament.

[subs (2) am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[150], opn 15 Apr 2011]

(2A) The provisions of subsections (1) and (2) hereof shall apply to any mortgage or other charge over property of the Republic or of an instrumentality of the Republic and to any guarantee given by the Cabinet on behalf of the Republic whether such mortgage, charge or guarantee was made or given before or after the commencement of this Act.

[subs (2A) insrt Act 17 of 2008, opn 18 Dec 2008]

(3) This Section has effect notwithstanding anything to the contrary in any other written law.

[subs (3) am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[150], opn 15 Apr 2011]

(4) A person including the President and a Minister, who gives or purports to give any mortgage or charge or guarantee in contravention of this Section or that is not made or given subject to its being approved by the Parliament within a period of 90 days commits an offence.

Penalty: $10,000 or imprisonment for 3 years or both.
