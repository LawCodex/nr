# Criminal Procedure (Amendment) Act 2015

CRIMINAL PROCEDURE (AMENDMENT) ACT 2015 ______________________________ No. 12 of 2015 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Criminal Procedure Act 1972 Certified: 12 May 2015 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Criminal Procedure (Amendment) Act 2015.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Act Amended

The Schedule amends the Criminal Procedure Act 1972.

## SCHEDULE - AMENDMENT OF CRIMINAL PROCEDURE ACT 1972

[1] Insert new section 7A

#### 7A Sentence for habitual sexual offenders

Any person who has, on at least 2 previous occasions been convicted of any sexual offence (whether of the same description of offence or not) shall be declared a habitual sexual offenders and shall be sentenced to life imprisonment without eligibility for parole.
