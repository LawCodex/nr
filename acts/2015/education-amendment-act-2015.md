# Education (Amendment) Act 2015

______________________________ No. 11 of 2015 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Education Act 2011 Certified: 12 May 2015 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Education (Amendment) Act 2015.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Act Amended

The Schedule amends the Education Act 2011.

## SCHEDULE - AMENDMENT OF EDUCATION ACT 2011

[1] Amendment of section 109 1.1 Amend subsection (2) Omit Penalty:  $100’ Substitute Penalty:  $10,000 fine or imprisonment for 1 year or both 1.2 Amend subsection (3) Omit Penalty:  $200’ Substitute Penalty:  $20,000 fine or imprisonment for 2 years of both 1.3 Amend subsection (4) Omit Penalty:  $200.

Substitute Penalty:  $20,000 fine or imprisonment for 2 years or both New clause will now read: 109 Offences on school premises

(1)  In this section:

‘school premises’ means land (including a building or structure on the land) occupied by a school.

(2)  A person must not trespass on school premises.

Penalty:  $10,000 fine or imprisonment for 1 year or both.

(3)  A person who is not a student of the school commits an offence if the person behaves in an offensive or disorderly way on school premises.

Penalty:  $20,000 fine or imprisonment for 2 years or both.

(4)  A person commits an offence if the person:

(a)  is on school premises; and

(b)  is directed to leave the premises by a police officer, the principal of a school or a person authorised by the principal; and

(c)  fails to leave the premises in accordance with the direction.

Penalty:  $20,000 fine or imprisonment for 2 years or both.

(5)  An offence against this section is a strict liability offence.
