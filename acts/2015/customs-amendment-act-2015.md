# Customs (Amendment) Act 2015

______________________________ No. 17 of 2015 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Customs Act 2014 Certified: 14 August 2015 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Customs (Amendment) Act 2015.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of section 2

Omit the following definition:

‘forfeited goods’ means goods that are forfeited to the Republic under section 283’ Substitute with the following:

‘forfeited goods’ means goods that are forfeited to the Republic under section 268 and section 283’
