An Act to provide for the salaries and allowances of the President, Speaker, Ministers, Deputy Speaker, Deputy Ministers and Members of Parliament.

Enacted by the Parliament of Naoero as follows:

[Long title subst Act 25 of 2019 s 5, opn 28 Aug 2019; am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day of certification and came into effect on 17 July 2008.

#### 3 Repeal

Subject to Section 4, the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 1988 is hereby repealed.

[s 3 am Act 11 of 2008, opn 26 Nov 2008]

#### 4 Savings and effective date

[s 4 rep Act 15 of 2011 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 1 July 2011]

#### 5 Salaries and allowances

The salaries and allowances of the President, Speaker, Ministers, Deputy Speaker, Deputy Ministers and Members of Parliament shall be as set out in the Schedules.

[s 5 subst Act 25 of 2019 s 6, opn 28 Aug 2019]

#### 5A Allowances for Deputy Ministers

The Parliament shall fix such allowances as may be appropriate in consideration of the services that the Deputy Ministers shall perform in the exercise of their powers, duties and functions as prescribed under the Deputy Ministers Act 2019.

[s 5A insrt Act 25 of 2019 s 7 opn 28 Aug 2019]

#### 6 Charge on Treasury Fund

The amounts to which a person shall be entitled under this Act shall be paid from the Treasury Fund which shall be charged accordingly.

#### 7 Review of salaries and allowances

The salaries and allowances of the President, Speaker, Ministers, Deputy Speaker, Deputy Ministers and Members of Parliament may only be varied by the Parliament by an amendment to:

(a) Schedule 1, which prescribes the salaries; and

(b) Schedule 2, which prescribes the allowances.

[s 7 subst Act 25 of 2019 s 8, opn 28 Aug 2019]

#### 7A Withholding of salaries and allowances

(1) The payment of salaries and allowances of the President, Speaker, Ministers, Deputy Speaker, Deputy Ministers and Members of Parliament, may be withheld if such person is suspended from the House.

[subs (1) subst Act 25 of 2019 s 9, opn 28 Aug 2019]

(2) The withholding of salaries and allowances may only be done through a simple majority vote of Members of Parliament, excluding any Member who has been temporarily suspended.

(3) Once a Member’s suspension is lifted, the suspension of payment of salaries and allowances that the Member is entitled to is also lifted.

(4) The House may also, through a simple majority vote, elect to pay to the Member any salaries and allowances withheld during the Member’s period of suspension.

[s 7A insrt Act 22 of 2014 Sch[1], opn 29 Oct 2014]

#### 8 Regulations

The Cabinet may make regulations, not inconsistent with this Act, prescribing matters required or permitted by this Act to be prescribed.

#### 9 Transitional and savings provision in relation to the
