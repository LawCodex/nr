# Parliamentary Pension (Amendment) No.2 Act 2016

PARLIAMENTARY PENSION (AMENDMENT) No. 2 ACT 2016 ______________________________ No. 61 of 2016 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Parliamentary Pension Act 2008 Certified: 23rd December 2016 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Parliamentary Pension (Amendment) No. 2 Act 2016.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of Parliamentary Pension Act 2008

The Parliamentary Pension Act 2008 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of section 8

Section 8 is amended by inserting a new subsection (6) as follows:

(6) A former Member of Parliament who is convicted of a criminal offence for which the prescribed minimum penalty is not less than 2 years imprisonment is not entitled to a Parliamentary Pension under subsection (1).
