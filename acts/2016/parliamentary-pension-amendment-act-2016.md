# Parliamentary Pension (Amendment) Act 2016

PARLIAMENTARY PENSION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2016 ______________________________ No. 40 of 2016 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Parliamentary Pension Act 2008 Certified: 19th July 2016 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Parliamentary Pension (Amendment) Act 2016.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of Parliamentary Pension Act 2008

The Parliamentary Pension Act 2008 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of section 8

(1) Section 8 is amended by inserting a new subsection 4(A) as follows: 4(A) In addition to payments provided for under the preceding subsections, a former Member of Parliament shall be entitled to a payment of $5000 per annum for each year of service if he or she has served as a Member of Parliament for 30 continuous years.
