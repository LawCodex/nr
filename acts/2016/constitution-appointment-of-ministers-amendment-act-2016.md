# Constitution (Appointment of Ministers) (Amendment) Act 2016

CONSTITUTION (APPOINTMENT OF MINISTERS) (AMENDMENT) ACT 2016 ______________________________ No. 54 of 2016 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Constitution of Nauru Certified: 4th November 2016 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Constitution (Appointment of Ministers) (Amendment) Act 2016.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of Constitution

The Constitution is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Article 19

#### 19 Appointment of Ministers

Article 19 of the Constitution is amended by:

(1) Omitting the words “four and five” where they appear in sub-clause (1) and substituting it with the words “five and six”;

(2) Omitting the word “four” where it appears in sub-clause

(2) and substituting it with the word “five”.

(3) Omitting the words “four and five” where it appears in sub- clause (3) and substituting it with the words “five and six”.
