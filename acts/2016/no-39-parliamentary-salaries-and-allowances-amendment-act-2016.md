# No.39 Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act 2016

PARLIAMENTARY SALARIES AND ALLOWANCES (AMENDMENT) ACT 2016 ______________________________ No. 39 of 2016 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008 Certified: 19th July 2016 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act 2016.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008

The Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of section 5

Section 5 is amended by inserting the words ‘Assistant Ministers’ after the words ‘Ministers of Cabinet’ and before the words ‘and the President’.

#### 5 Amendment of section 7

Section 7 is amended by inserting the words ‘Assistant Ministers’ after the words ‘Ministers of Cabinet’ and before the words ‘and the President’.

#### 6 Amendment of section 7A

Section 7A(1) is amended by inserting the words ‘Assistant Ministers’ after the words ‘Ministers of Cabinet’ and before the words ‘and the President’.
