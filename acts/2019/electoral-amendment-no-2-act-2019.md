# Electoral (Amendment) No.2 Act 2019

ELECTORAL (AMENDMENT) NO. 2 ACT 2019 ______________________________ No. 21 of 2019 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Electoral Act 2016 Certified: 12th July 2019 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Electoral (Amendment) No. 2 Act 2019.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Electoral Act 2016

The Electoral Act 2016 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of section 78

Section 78(7) is amended by deleting the figure ‘2’ and substituting ‘4’.

The clause will now read:

‘(7) The Electoral Commissioner must not grant to a voter more than 4 proxy voting authorisations.’

#### 5 Amendment of section 85

Section 85(4) is amended by deleting ‘section 87’ where it appears immediately after the word ‘under’ and substituting ‘section 85A’.

#### 6 Amendment to section 85A

Section 85A (4) is deleted and substituted as follows:

“(4)  If two or more candidates receive an equal value of votes and one or more of the candidates must be excluded, the Electoral Commissioner must determine by lot a candidate or candidates to be excluded, using a random method of selection such as:

(a)  tossing a coin; or

(b)  drawing the names out of a container in such manner that the Electoral Commissioner cannot see which names he or she is drawing.”
