# Legal Practitioners (Amendment) Act 2019

LEGAL PRACTITIONERS (AMENDMENT) ACT 2019 ______________________________ No. 28 of 2019 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Legal Practitioners Act 2019 Certified: 28th November 2019 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Legal Practitioners (Amendment) Act 2019.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification of the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Legal Practitioners Act 2019

The Legal Practitioners Act 2019 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 4

Section 4 is amended by deleting and substituting the meaning for

‘President’ as follows:

‘President’ means the President of the Law Society who shall be a legal practitioner duly admitted to practice law in the Republic;

#### 5 Amendment of Section 20

Section 20(1) is deleted and substituted as follows:

The clause now reads:

‘(1)  A practitioner shall not commence practice under his or her own name or a business name:

(a) without any supervision unless he or she has practiced for a minimum period of 3 years after admission to the Supreme Court; and

(b) has practiced under the supervision of a practitioner having practiced for not less than 3 years in the Republic or elsewhere.’
