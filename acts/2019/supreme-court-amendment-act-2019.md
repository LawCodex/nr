# SUPREME COURT (AMENDMENT) ACT 2019

______________________________ No. 4 of 2019 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Supreme Court Act 2018 Certified: 22nd March 2019 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Supreme Court (Amendment) Act 2019.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Supreme Court Act 2018

The Supreme Court Act 2018 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of section 38

Section 38(2) is deleted and substituted as follows:

‘(2) Where the District Court has acquitted an accused person in any criminal cause or matter:

(a) the Director of Public Prosecutions; or

(b) with his or her sanction in writing, the person who prosecuted the criminal cause or matter before the District Court, - may appeal the judgment, decision or order of acquittal to the Supreme Court.’
