# Nauru Utilities Corporation (Amendment) Act 2019

NAURU UTILITIES CORPORATION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2019 ______________________________ No. 3 of 2019 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Nauru Utilities Corporation Act 2011 Certified: 21st March 2019 Table of Contents Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Nauru Utilities Corporation (Amendment) Act 2019.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences upon certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Nauru Utilities Corporation Act 2011

The Nauru Utilities Corporation Act 2011 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of section 7

Section 7 is amended as follows:

(a) in subsection (1) by deleting and substituting the word ‘two’ where it appears with the word ‘three’;

(b) deleting and substituting subsection (3) as follows:

‘(3) Of the three ex officio members appointed under subsection (1):

(a) one must be a senior officer currently employed within the Corporation;

(b) one must be a senior officer currently employed within the Department of Finance; and

(c) one must be the Secretary of the Department of Commerce Industry and Environment or his or her nominee.’
