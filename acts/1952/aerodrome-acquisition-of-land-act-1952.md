An Act to provide for the acquisition of land for the purposes of an Aerodrome.

BE it ordained by the Acting Administrator of the Island of Nauru, in pursuance of the powers conferred by Article I of the Agreement between His Majesty’s Government in London, His Majesty’s Government of the Commonwealth of Australia and His Majesty’s Government of the Dominion of New Zealand, and adopted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title and commencement

This Act may be cited as the Aerodrome (Acquisition of Land) Act 1952 and came into effect on 7 October 1952.

[s 1 subst Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1 clause [10], opn 15 Apr 2011]

#### 2 Definitions

[s 2 rep Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1 clause [11], opn 15 Apr 2011]

#### 3 Vesting of land

The land described in the Schedules to this Act is vested in the Republic as an estate in fee simple absolute, free from encumbrances.

[s 3 subst Act 9 of 1968 s 3, opn 16 Oct 1968]

#### 4 Notice to vest land of Administrator

[s 4 rep Act 9 of 1968 s 3, opn 16 Oct 1968]

#### 5 Notice of acquisition

[s 5 rep Act 9 of 1968 s 3, opn 16 Oct 1968]

#### 6 Compensation for land

(1) The Republic shall pay to the owners of land acquired under this Act compensation in the form of an amount payable annually in advance in perpetuity.

[subs (1) am Act 9 of 1968 s 4, opn 16 Oct 1968]

(2) The annual amount payable in accordance with this Section is the amount which would be payable under clause 4 of the Third Schedule to the Lands Act 1976 (or, under an order made under Section 16 of the Lands Act 1976) if the land were leased for use as an aerodrome.

[subs (2) am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1 clause [12], opn 15 Apr 2011]

#### 12 Succession to compensation

The right of a person to compensation under this Act shall devolve and may be transferred in the same manner and to the same extent, if any, as if that right were the ownership of the land in respect of which that right arose.
