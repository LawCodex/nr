An Act to adopt certain laws of the Commonwealth of Australia, Queensland and the former Territory of Papua as laws of the Republic.

[Long title subst Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[80], opn 15 Apr 2011]

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Laws Repeal and Adopting Act 1922.

#### 10 Application of certain Commonwealth laws

Subject to this Act, the Acts of Parliament of the Commonwealth of Australia specified in Schedule 1 shall, so far as they are applicable, apply to the Republic.

[s 10 renum Ord 25 of 1967 s 5 and Sch 3, opn 29 Dec 1967; am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[84], opn 15 Apr 2011] 11 [Repealed]

[s 11 rep Act 11 of 1971 s 7, opn 21 Feb 1972]

#### 12 Adopting Statutes of Queensland

Those portions of the Acts and Statutes of the State of Queensland specified in Schedule 2 and any amendments thereto that were in force in the said State on 1 July 1921, and those portions of every regulation or rule made under the provisions of any of the said Acts or Statutes that were in force at the date aforesaid are hereby adopted as laws of the Republic, so far as the same are applicable to the circumstances of Naoero, and are not repugnant to, or inconsistent with the provisions of any Act, Ordinance, law, regulation, rule, order, or proclamation having the force of law, that has been, or may hereafter be expressed to extend to, or applied to, or made or promulgated in the Republic.

[s 12 subst Ord 2 of 1927, opn 19 Mar 1927renum Ord 25 of 1967 s 5 and Sch 3, opn 29 Dec 1967; am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[84], opn 15 Apr 2011; Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026] 13 [Repealed]

[s 13 rep Act 11 of 1971 s 7, opn 21 Feb 1972]

#### 14 Adopting Ordinances of Papua

Those portions of the Ordinances of the former Territory of Papua specified in Schedule 3 that were in force in that Territory on 23 September 1922, and those portions of every regulation or rule made under the provisions of any of the Ordinances that were in force in that Territory on 23 September 1922 are adopted as Ordinances, regulations, and rules of the Republic so far as the same are applicable to the circumstances of the Republic, and are not repugnant to, or inconsistent with the provisions of any Act, Ordinance, law, regulation, rule, order, or proclamation having the force of law, that has been or may hereafter be expressed to extend to, or applied to or made or promulgated in the Republic.

[s 14 renum Ord 25 of 1967 s 5 and Sch 3, opn 29 Dec 1967; am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[84], opn 15 Apr 2011] 15 [Repealed]

[s 15 rep Act 11 of 1971 s 7, opn 21 Feb 1972]

#### 16 Adopted law to be construed to meet circumstances of the

Republic All or any references to authorities, persons, places, subjects, matters, or things in any Act, Statute, law, regulation, or rule hereby adopted shall be taken as referring to corresponding or analogous authorities, persons, places, subjects, matters, or things in the Republic, and for the purpose of facilitating the application of any of the aforesaid Acts, Statutes, laws, regulations, or rules hereby adopted, it shall be lawful for any court or magistrate to construe the same with such verbal alteration not affecting the substance as may be necessary to render the same applicable to the matter before the court or magistrate.
