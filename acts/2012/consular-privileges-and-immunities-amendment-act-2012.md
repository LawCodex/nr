#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Consular Privileges and Immunities (Amendment) Act 2012.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification.

#### 3 Act amended

Schedule 1 amends the Consular Privileges and Immunities Act 1976.
