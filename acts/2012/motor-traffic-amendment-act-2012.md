#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Motor Traffic (Amendment) Act 2012.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on a date fixed by the Minister by Gazette notice.

#### 3 Act amended

The Schedule amends the Motor Traffic Act 1937.

(a) any matter relating to road safety; and

(a) be of general application or vary in their application according to prescribed factors; and
