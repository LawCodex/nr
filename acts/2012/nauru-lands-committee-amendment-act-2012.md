# Nauru Lands Committee (Amendment) Act 2012

Nauru Lands Committee (Amendment) Act 2012 ______________________________ Act No. 9 of 2012 ______________________________ Table of Provisions As certified on 10 October 2012 REPUBLIC OF NAURU Nauru Lands Committee (Amendment) Act 2012 ______________________________ Act No. 9 of 2012 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Nauru Lands Committee Act 1956 Certified on 10 October 2012 Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Nauru Lands Committee (Amendment) Act 2012.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day it receives the certificate of the Speaker under Article 47.

#### 3 Amendment of Nauru Lands Committee Act 1956

The Schedule amends the Nauru Lands Committee Act 1956.

As certified on 10 October 2012

[2] Amendment of section 2 Section 2 insert (in alphabetical order)

‘Curator’ means the Curator of Intestate Estates mentioned in section 6 of the Succession, Probate and Administration Act 1976;

‘personal estate’ means the personal property to which a deceased person was entitled at the time of the person’s death, and any share or interest in such property, that is:

(a) subject to the law of Nauru; and

(b) not real estate;

## PART 3 – TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

[9] New sections 9 and 10 After section 8 insert

#### 9 Validation of decisions of Curator

A decision of the Curator made before the commencement of the Nauru Lands Committee (Amendment) Act 2012 that purports to determine the distribution of the personal estate of a deceased Nauruan is taken to have been made by the Committee.

#### 10 Application of section 7(1)(b)

Section 7(1)(b) applies to any decision made or taken to have been made by the Committee, whether before or after the commencement of the Nauru Lands Committee (Amendment) Act 2012.
