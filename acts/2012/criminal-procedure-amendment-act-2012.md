# Criminal Procedure (Amendment) Act 2012

______________________________ Act No. 15 of 2012 ______________________________ Table of Provisions As certified on 6 November 2012 REPUBLIC OF NAURU Criminal Procedure (Amendment) Act 2012 ______________________________ Act No. 15 of 2012 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Criminal Procedure Act 1972 Certified on 6 November 2012 Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Criminal Procedure (Amendment) Act 2012.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification.

#### 3 Act amended

The Schedule amends the Criminal Procedure Act 1972.
