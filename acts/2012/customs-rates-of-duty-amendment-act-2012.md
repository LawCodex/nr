# Customs (Rates of Duty) (Amendment) Act 2012

Customs (Rates of Duty) (Amendment) Act 2012 ______________________________ Act No. 2 of 2012 ______________________________ Table of Provisions REPUBLIC OF NAURU Customs (Rates of Duty) (Amendment) Act 2012 ______________________________ Act No. 2 of 2012 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Customs (Rates of Duty) Act 2010 Certified on 23 February 2012 Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Customs (Rates of Duty) (Amendment) Act 2012.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences 7 days after certification.

#### 3 Amendment of Customs (Rates of Duty) Act 2010

The Schedule amends the Customs (Rates of Duty) Act 2010.
