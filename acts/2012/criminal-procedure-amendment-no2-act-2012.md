[2] Amendment of section 61 (Power to dispense with personal attendance of As certified on 21 December 2012 REPUBLIC OF NAURU Criminal Procedure (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2012 ______________________________ Act No. 25 of 2012 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Criminal Procedure Act 1972 Certified on 21 December 2012 Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Criminal Procedure (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2012.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification.

#### 3 Act amended

The Schedule amends the Criminal Procedure Act 1972.
