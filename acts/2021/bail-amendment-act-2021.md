# Bail (Amendment) Act 2021

______________________________ No. 5 of 2021 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Bail Act 2018.

Certified: 31st March 2021 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Bail (Amendment) Act 2021.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Bail Act 2018

The Bail Act 2018 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 4B

Section 4B(1)(a)(iii) is amended by deleting the words ‘assaulting a police officer in the execution of the police officer’s duties’ where they appear and substituting with the words ‘causing harm to a police officer’.
