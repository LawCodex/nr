# Supreme Court (Amendment) Act 2021

______________________________ No. 9 of 2021 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Supreme Court Act 2018.

Certified: 1st June 2021 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Supreme Court (Amendment) Act 2021.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Supreme Court Act 2018

The Supreme Court Act 2018 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 109

Section 109 is deleted and substituted as follows: 109 Court sitting days

(1) The Supreme Court shall sit on all working days.

(2) The Supreme Court may sit on a Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday.
