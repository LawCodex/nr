# District Court (Amendment) Act 2021

______________________________ No. 8 of 2021 ______________________________ An Act to amend the District Court Act 2018.

Certified: 1st June 2021 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the District Court (Amendment) Act 2021.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the District Court Act 2018

The District Court Act 2018 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 63

Section 63 is deleted and substituted as follows: 63  Court sitting days

(1) The District Court shall sit on all working days.

(2) The District Court may sit on a Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday.
