An Act to provide for the appointment, salary, conditions of service, duties, powers and functions of the Auditor General and for the auditing of public accounts and for other purposes.

[Long title am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

Enacted by the Parliament of Naoero as follows:

#### 1 Short title and commencement

This Act may be cited as the Audit Act 1973 and came into effect on 9 November 1973.

#### 2 Interpretation

In this Act:

‘accounting officer’ includes every person who is charged with the duty of collecting, receiving or accounting for, or who in fact collects, receives or accounts for, any public monies, or who is charged with the duty of disbursing, or who does in fact disburse, any public monies, and every person who is charged with the receipt, custody or disposal of, or the accounting for, public stores or who in fact receives, holds, disposes of or accounts for public stores;

‘Auditor General’ means the public officer for the time being holding the office of Auditor General by virtue of his or her appointment under the provisions of Section 3 of this Act; and

[def am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

‘controlling officer’ means, in respect of public monies collected, received or disbursed or public stores and other assets of the Republic received, held or disposed of by or on account of any department of the public service, the public service head of that department, save that, where provision is made by any written law relating to the control and management of the public finances of the Republic for the appointment of persons to be responsible for the said public monies and public stores and any person other than such public service head of the department has been appointed by or under that written law to be so responsible, it means the person so appointed;

‘public monies’ means all revenue, loan, trust and other monies and all stamps, bonds, debentures and other securities whatsoever raised, received or held, whether temporarily or otherwise, by or on account of the Republic;

‘public stores’ means chattels of whatsoever nature the property of, or in the possession of or under the control of, the Republic;

‘Secretary for Finance’ means the head of the Department of Finance;

#### 3 Auditor General

(1) The Auditor General shall be appointed by the Chief Secretary:

Provided that no appointment shall be made except with the approval of the Cabinet.

[subs (1) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(2) Subject to Article 65(3) of the Constitution, the annual salary of the Auditor General shall be such amount as the Cabinet fixes by regulations.

[subs (2) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(3) The provisions of the law and regulations relating to the public service shall apply to the Auditor General, except where such provisions are in conflict with the provisions of the Constitution, this Act or any other written law for the time being in force.

[subs (3) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

[s 3 heading am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

#### 4 Absence, etc, of the Auditor General

(1) Where the Auditor General is unable, by reason of illness, absence from the Republic or any other cause, to perform his or her duties and functions, the Chief Secretary may appoint any person to perform those duties and functions until such time as the Auditor General is again able to perform them.

[subs (1) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(2) Where the office of Auditor General is vacant, the Chief Secretary shall appoint a person to perform the duties and functions of the post until such time as an Auditor General is appointed:

Provided that an appointment made under this subsection shall not exceed 12 months and no further such appointment shall be made upon the expiration of the period of that appointment.

[subs (2) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(3) Every person appointed under the provisions of this Section to perform the duties and functions of the Auditor General shall, for so long as the appointment continues, have and may exercise all the powers and authority of, and shall perform the duties imposed upon the Auditor General by this Act and any other written law for the time being in force.

[subs (3) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(4) No appointment shall be made under this Section without the prior approval of the Cabinet.

[s 4 heading am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

#### 5 Staff of Audit Office

(1) The staff required to assist the Auditor General in the performance of his or her duties shall consist of such number of public officers as the President may determine and appointed by the Chief Secretary.

[subs (1) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(2) Anything which under the authority of this Act is directed to be done by the Auditor General, other than the certifying and reporting of accounts for Parliament, may be done by any staff authorised by him or her.

[subs (2) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

#### 6 Duties of the Auditor General

(1) Subject to subsection (2), the Auditor General shall, on behalf of Parliament, examine, inquire into and audit the accounts of all accounting officers in Naoero and in the offices of the Republic outside Naoero.

[subs (1) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015; Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026] s 3LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

(2) The Auditor General may, in respect of accounts maintained at any office of the Republic outside Naoero, authorise any person publicly carrying on the profession of accountant in the country in which that office is situated or a public officer to inspect, examine or audit the books and accounts maintained at that office; such person or public officer shall have and may exercise so much of the powers and authority of the Auditor General as the Auditor General may by writing under his or her hand delegate to him or her and shall report on his or her inspection, examination or audit of those books and accounts to the Auditor General in such manner as the Auditor General may direct.

[subs (2) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015; Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

(3) The Auditor General shall satisfy himself or herself:

(a) that all reasonable precautions have been taken to safeguard the collection of public monies and that the laws, directions or instructions have been duly observed; and

(b) that all money appropriated or otherwise disbursed has been expended and applied for the purpose for which the grants made by Parliament were intended to provide and that the expenditure conforms to the authority which governs it.

[subs (3) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(4) No authority shall be given to any public officer under subsection (2) except with the consent of the Minister responsible for the department of the public service in which that officer is employed.

(5) The Auditor General may, subject to such expenditure being authorised by an appropriation law or by a law made under Article 61 of the Constitution, pay from the Treasury Fund to any person, other than a public officer, authorised by him or her under subsection (2) to inspect, examine or audit any books and accounts his or her reasonable expenses of such inspection, examination or audit and such fees as may from time to time be authorised in writing by the Speaker.

[subs (5) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

[s 6 heading am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

#### 7 Accounts of statutory bodies

(1) Where by or under the provisions of any written law, the Auditor General is required or authorised to inspect, examine or audit the books and accounts of any body corporate established by such written law, in the exercise of his or her duties, the Auditor General shall have, in relation to any such body corporate and its members, officers and employees, the same discretion and powers mutatis mutandis as are conferred upon him or her by Sections 6 and 9 in relation to public monies and public stores.

[subs (1) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(2) The Auditor General may authorise any person publicly carrying on the profession of accountant in the country where the books and accounts are kept or any public officer to inspect, examine or audit the books and accounts of any body corporate which by or under any written law he or she may be required or authorised to inspect, examine or audit and such person or officer shall report to the Auditor General in such manner as the Auditor General may direct.

[subs (2) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015] s 7 AUDIT ACT 1973

(3) No authority shall be given to any public officer under subsection (2) except with the concurrence of the Minister responsible for the department of the public service in which that officer is employed.

(4) The Auditor General shall report on his or her inspection, examination or audit of the books and accounts referred to in this Section and shall, unless any written law requires otherwise, submit his or her report to the Minister responsible for the Act by which the body corporate is established, who shall cause it to be tabled in Parliament as soon as reasonably possible:

Provided that such Minister shall obtain the observations of the body corporate on any matter to which attention has been called by the Auditor General in his or her report and such observations shall be furnished to Parliament with the said report.

[subs (4) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(5) The Auditor General may, subject to such expenditure being authorised by an appropriation law or by a law made under Article 61 of the Constitution, pay from the Treasury Fund to any person authorised under subsection (2) to inspect, examine or audit any books and accounts his or her reasonable expenses of such inspection, examination or audit and such fees as may from time to time be authorised in writing by the Speaker:

Provided that, unless otherwise provided by any written law, all monies so paid shall be reimbursed to the Treasury Fund by the body corporate whose books and accounts such person was authorised to inspect, examine or audit.

[subs (5) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

#### 8 Notification of irregularities to the Minister

Where at any time it appears to the Auditor General that any irregularities have occurred in the expenditure, receipt, custody, issue, sale, transfer or delivery of any public monies or public stores, or in the accounting for the same, he or she shall immediately bring the matter to the notice of the Minister.

[s 8 am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

#### 9 Powers of the Auditor General

(1) In the exercise of his or her duties under this Act, the Auditor General may:

(a) call upon any public officer for any explanation and information he or she may require in order to enable him or her to discharge his or her duties;

(b) authorise in writing any public officer on his or her behalf to conduct any inquiry, examination or audit and such officer shall report to the Auditor General:

Provided that any such authority shall be subject to the concurrence of the Minister responsible for the department of the public service in which that officer is employed;

(c) without payment of any fee cause search to be made in and extracts to be taken from any book, document or record in any public office;

(d) examine upon oath, declaration or affirmation, which oath, declaration or affirmation the Auditor General is empowered to administer, all persons whom he or she may think fit to examine respecting the expenditure, receipt, custody, issue, sale, transfer or delivery of any public monies or public stores, and respecting all other matters and s 7LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO things whatsoever which he or she considers necessary for the due performance and exercise of the duties and powers vested in him or her; and

(e) commence before the Supreme Court a case in writing as to any question regarding the interpretation of any written law concerning the powers of the Auditor General or the discharge of his or her duties, and a Judge of the Supreme Court shall give a written opinion on such case.

[subs (1) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(2) In the exercise of his or her duties under this Act the Auditor General or any person duly authorised by him or her shall have access to all records, books, vouchers, documents, cash, stamps, securities, stores and other property of the Republic in the possession of any officer.

[subs (2) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(3) Any person examined pursuant to the provisions of subsection (1)(d) who gives a false answer to any question put to him or her or makes a false statement on any matter knowing that answer or statement to be false or not knowing or believing it to be true, shall be deemed to be guilty of perjury and shall be liable to be prosecuted and punished accordingly.

[s 9 heading am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

#### 10 Yearly statement of accounts

(1) The Minister responsible for the public accounts of the Republic shall, within a period of 3 months after the end of each financial year or such longer period as Parliament may by resolution appoint, certify under his or her hand, subject to such explanation and qualification as he or she may think necessary, and transmit to the Auditor General the following statements and accounts:

(a) a statement of the Treasury Fund showing the estimates of receipts and expenditure under the several heads and subheads, including any supplementary estimates, approved in respect of that financial year and the amounts actually received and expenditure incurred under those heads and subheads;

(b) statements of the Government Loans Fund and of the Trust Fund showing receipts and expenditure in each case;

(c) a statement of the assets and liabilities of the Republic at the end of the financial year, the manner in which those assets are invested or held and the general heads in respect of which those liabilities are outstanding; and

(d) such other statements as Parliament may, from time to time, by resolution require or the Minister may consider necessary.

[subs (1) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(2) A controlling officer shall, if so required by the Minister responsible for the public accounts of the Republic, to the extent that he or she is responsible for the control of the public monies to which any part of the statement referred to in subsection (1) relates, certify under his or her hand, subject to such explanation or qualification as he or she may think necessary, the correctness of such part of such statement and the propriety of the charges shown in the statement.

(3) Every public officer who receives monies as official trustee, public trustee or Curator of Intestate Estates or in a like capacity or is responsible for the s 10 AUDIT ACT 1973 administration of any fund established by any written law shall, within a period of 1 month after the end of each financial year, sign and transmit to the Secretary for Finance a statement relating to such monies or funds setting out the income and expenditure during that financial year and the assets and liabilities at the end thereof, and the Secretary for Finance shall transmit such statement to the Auditor General together with the statements and accounts referred to in subsection (1).

[subs (3) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

#### 11 Annual report of Auditor General

(1) The Auditor General shall prepare and transmit to the Minister within a period of 5 months after the close of each financial year, or such longer period as Parliament may by resolution appoint, a report upon his or her examination and audit of all accounts relating to public monies and public stores, together with certified copies of the statements and accounts referred to in Section 10.

[subs (1) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(2) The Minister shall table in Parliament within 2 weeks of receiving the report of the Auditor General or, if Parliament is not sitting on any of the last 4 days of that period, on the first day of the next sitting of Parliament thereafter, a copy of the report together with a copy of the statements and accounts referred to in Section 10.

[subs (2) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(3) The Auditor General may at any time, if it appears to him or her to be desirable, transmit a special report to the Minister for presentation in like manner to Parliament.

[subs (3) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(4) A special report under subsection (3) may be made on any matter incidental to his or her powers and duties under this Act

#### 2 Salary of Auditor General

(1) Where the Auditor General is a person who was not appointed under the Public Service Act 2016 his or her salary shall be whichever amount is the larger of:

(a) the amount set out in respect of salary in Part 1 of the Schedule to these Regulations; and

(b) the amount which is the sum of that amount and of every amount by which the basic wage of Naoero citizens is increased after the date on which these Regulations are made.

[subreg (1) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015; Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

(2) Where the office of Auditor General is held by a person who was appointed under the Public Service Act 2016 his or her salary shall be the sum specified in Part 2 of the Schedule to these Regulations.

[subreg (2) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(3) In addition to his or her salary the Auditor General shall be entitled to be paid and receive travel and subsistence allowances on the scale for the time being payable to public service heads of department.

[subreg (3) am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

(4) The salaries provided for by subregulations (1) and (2) shall have effect from the date specified in the second column of Parts 1 and 2 respectively of the Schedule to these Regulations.

[reg 2 heading am Act 10 of 2015 s 3 and Sch[1], opn 12 May 2015]

#### 3 Repeal

The Director of Audit (Salary) Regulations 1977 and the Director of Audit

(Salary) Regulations 1979 are hereby repealed.
