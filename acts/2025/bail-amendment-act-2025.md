# Bail (Amendment) Act 2025

_____________________________ No.  10  of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Bail Act 2018 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 9 April 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Bail (Amendment) Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Bail Act 2018

The Bail Act 2018 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 4B

Section 4B is amended by deleting subsection (6).
