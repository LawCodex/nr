# Electoral (Amendment) Act (No.2) 2025

ELECTORAL (AMENDMENT) ACT (NO. 2) 2025 _____________________________ No.  23 of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Electoral Act 2016 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 15 May 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Electoral (Amendment) Act (No. 2) 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Electoral Act 2016

The Electoral Act 2016 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 41

Section 41(3)(b) is amended by deleting ‘$1,000’ and substituting with ‘$500’.
