# Correctional Service (Amendment) Act 2025

CORRECTIONAL SERVICE (AMENDMENT) ACT 2025 _____________________________ No.  9  of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Correctional Service Act 2009 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 9 April 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Correctional Service (Amendment) Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Correctional Service Act 2009

The Correctional Service Act 2009 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 12

Section 12(2) is deleted and substituted as follows:

‘(2) Any hearing and determination for disciplinary offences against officers shall be conducted by the Chief Secretary in accordance with the rules of natural justice and with any procedures prescribed by regulations under the Public Service Act 2016.’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 59

Section 59 is amended by inserting subsection (3) as follows:

‘(3) All disciplinary proceedings that have commenced and have not been completed at the commencement of the Correctional Services (Amendment) Act 2025, shall be referred by the Chief Correctional Officer to the Chief Secretary for the hearing and determination under the Public Service Act 2016.’.
