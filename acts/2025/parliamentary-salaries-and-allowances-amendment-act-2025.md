# Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act 2025

PARLIAMENTARY SALARIES AND ALLOWANCES (AMENDMENT) ACT 2025 _____________________________ No.  4 of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 11 March 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008

The Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Schedule 1

Schedule 1 is deleted and substituted as follows:

‘SCHEDULE 1

[Section 5]

SALARIES Office Salary (per fortnight) Salary (per year) President  $ 4,232.11 $ 110,035.00 Minister assisting the President $ 3,846.17 $ 100,000.00 Minister $ 3,468.05 $ 90,169.00 Deputy Minister $ 3,269.23 $ 85,000.00 Speaker $ 3,846.17 $ 100,000.00 Deputy Speaker $ 3,188.42 $ 82,899.00 Member of Parliament $2,884.60 $ 75,000.00 ’

#### 5 Insertion of new Section 9

After Section 8, a new Section 9 is inserted as follows:

‘9 Transitional and savings provision in relation to the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act 2025 Any salary increment paid before the commencement of the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act 2025 is validated and deemed to have been made pursuant to the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act 2025.’.
