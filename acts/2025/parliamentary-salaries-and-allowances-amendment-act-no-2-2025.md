# Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act (No.2) 2025

PARLIAMENTARY SALARIES AND ALLOWANCES (AMENDMENT) ACT (NO.2) 2025 _____________________________ No.  13  of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 9 April 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act (No.2) 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008

The Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 9

Section 9 is amended by inserting the words “and the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act (No.2) 2025” after the words “Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act 2025” wherever occurring.

#### 5 Amendment of Schedule 1

Item 5 of Schedule 1 is deleted and substituted as follows:

Speaker $ 3,892.07 $ 101,193.90
