# Anti-Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions (Amendment) Act 2025

ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING AND TARGETED FINANCIAL SANCTIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT 2025 _____________________________ No.  17 of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Anti-Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions Act 2023 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 13 May 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Anti-Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions (Amendment) Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Anti-Money Laundering and Targeted Financial

Sanctions Act 2023 The Anti-Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions Act 2023 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 128

Section 128(2) is amended by:

(a) deleting ‘or’ at the end of paragraph (h);

(b) deleting the fullstop at the end of paragraph (i) and substituting with ‘; or’; and

(c) inserting the following new paragraph (j) after paragraph (i):

‘(j) determinations which may be granted by the Minister in consultation with the Cabinet, exempting a reporting entity or class of reporting entities from the requirements of regulations made under this Part, where the Cabinet is satisfied that the reporting entity the subject of a determination, is adequately regulated under anti-money laundering or combatting financing of terrorism laws and supervised by authorities of another country; or’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 131

Section 131 is amended by:

(a) renumbering it as subsection (1); and

(b) inserting a new subsection (2) as follows:

‘(2) Without limiting subsection (1), regulations may prescribe determinations which may be granted by the Minister in consultation with the Cabinet, exempting a reporting entity or class of reporting entities from the requirements of Regulations prescribed under this Section, where the Cabinet is satisfied that the reporting entity the subject of a determination, is adequately regulated under anti-money laundering or combatting financing of terrorism laws and supervised by authorities of another country.’.

#### 6 Consequential amendments

(1) The Anti-Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions (Financing of Terrorism and Proliferation Financing) Regulations 2023 are amended by:

(a) inserting a new Regulation 72 as follows:

‘72 Minister in consultation with the Cabinet may grant exemption

(1) Subject to subregulation (2), the Minister in consultation with the Cabinet may make a determination exempting a reporting entity from a requirement under these Regulations.

(2) A determination shall not be made unless, the Cabinet is satisfied that a reporting entity, is adequately regulated under anti-money laundering or targeted financial sanctions laws and supervised by authorities of another country.’ ; and

(b) renumbering the current Regulation 72 as Regulation 73.

(2) The Anti-Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions (Parallel Financial Investigations) Regulations 2024 are amended by inserting a new Regulation 14 as follows:

‘14 Minister in consultation with the Cabinet may grant exemption

(1) Subject to subregulation (2), the Minister in consultation with the Cabinet may make a determination exempting a reporting entity from a requirement under these Regulations.

(2) A determination shall not be made unless, the Cabinet is satisfied that a reporting entity, is adequately regulated under anti-money laundering or combatting financing of terrorism laws and supervised by authorities of another country.’.
