# Crimes (Amendment) Act 2025

_____________________________ No.  28 of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Crimes Act 2016 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 19 June 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Crimes (Amendment) Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Crimes Act 2016

The Crimes Act 2016 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 41A

Section 41A is amended by deleting ‘Part 4 or Part 7’ and substituting with ‘Part 4, Part 5, Part 6, Part 7 or Part 9’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 209

Section 209(1) is amended in the definition of ‘firearm’ by:

(a) deleting the word ‘or’ at the end of paragraph (v); and

(b) deleting paragraph (b)(vi) and substituting with:

‘(vi)   an explosive or a device capable of making an explosive or similar sound which is capable of being used to endanger a person’s safety; or

(vii)  an imitation or replica of anything mentioned in paragraph (a) or this paragraph; but’.
