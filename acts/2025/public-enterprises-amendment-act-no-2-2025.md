# Public Enterprises (Amendment) Act (No. 2) 2025

PUBLIC ENTERPRISES (AMENDMENT) ACT (No. 2) 2025 _____________________________ No.  44 of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Public Enterprises Act 2019 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 24 October 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Public Enterprises (Amendment) Act (No. 2) 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Public Enterprises Act 2019

The Public Enterprises Act 2019 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 37

Section 37(2)(b) is amended by deleting ‘7’ and substituting with ‘9’.
