# Survey Act 2025

## Table of Amendments

The Survey Act 2025 No 5 was certified and commenced on 11 March 2025.

Amending Legislation Certified Date of Commencement

An Act to provide for the registration of surveyors, to regulate the practice of surveying and for other related purposes.

Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

## PART 1 — PRELIMINARY

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Survey Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Objectives

The objectives of this Act are to:

(a) provide for the registration of surveyors and field officers;

(b) provide for a Register of Surveyors and field officers and maintenance of surveyors’ practicing certificate;

(c) provide for instruments of survey, equipment, mapping system accuracy and drawings of plans for surveys;

(d) provide public to have accessibility to registered survey plans; and

(e) provide for enforcement and prohibition on removal of survey marks.

#### 4 Interpretation

In this Act:

‘appropriate accuracy’, in relation to a survey, means the greatest accuracy that may reasonably be attained in making the survey;

‘cadastral survey’ means the determination of the form, contour, position, area, shape, height, depth or nature of any part of the earth or of any natural or artificial features, and the position, length, and direction of boundary lines on, below or above any part of the earth for the purposes of making legal plans defining boundaries and registering land or documents with appropriate authorities;

‘Civil Aviation Authority’ means the Authority established under Section 11 of the Civil Aviation Authority 2011;

‘Department’ means the department responsible for Land Management;

‘Director’ means the Director of Lands and Survey;

‘existing monument’, in relation to land, means a monument, the position of which was fixed in the course of surveying other land;

‘field officer’ means a person who is appointed as a field officer under Section 18;

‘geodetic system’ means a system that enables positions on the surface of the Earth to be determined by reference to a mathematical model that describes the size and shape of the earth;

‘Global Navigation Satellite System’ means in relation to land survey using Global Positioning System instruments to conduct surveys;

‘licence’ means a licence issued to a field officer under Section 19;

‘metric system of measurement’ has the same meaning given to it under the Metric Conversion Act 1973;

‘Minister’ means the Minister responsible for Land Management;

‘monument’ means a brass tablet, iron or wooden post, mound, pit or trench, or any other thing used by a surveyor to mark a boundary, corner or line;

‘official plan of survey’ means a plan of survey recorded and kept by the Department;

‘portion’ means a demarcated area of land surveyed in a District and identified on an official plan of survey by a number allocated and certified by the Director;

‘regulations’ means regulations made under this Act;

‘Secretary’ means the Secretary responsible for Land Management;

‘station’ means a survey control station or a monument and includes an active or passive reference station or a sign denoting a point of reference or elevation;

‘survey control system’ means a system used to determine the position of points, features and boundaries in cadastral surveys, other surveys and land information systems;

‘survey plan’ means a graphical representation of one or more cadastral surveys;

‘surveyor’ means a person who is registered as a surveyor under Section 13;

‘tenure system’ means a system that provides for the creation and transfer of interests in land; and

‘total station’ means theodolites survey instruments used for land survey and includes drones, global positioning systems, levelling instruments, measuring tapes and prisms. s 4LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

## PART 2 — ADMINISTRATION

#### 5 Functions and responsibilities of the Secretary

The functions and responsibilities of the Secretary shall be to:

(a) register surveyors;

(b) register field officers;

(c) maintain a register each of surveyors and field officers;

(d) issue practicing certificates for surveyors;

(e) issue licences to field officers;

(f) keep and maintain a register of all approved instruments to be used for the survey;

(g) facilitate the receipt of complaints in relation to surveyors or field officers; and

(h) do all other things as may be necessary or convenient to achieve the objective of this Act.

#### 6 Powers of the Secretary

The Secretary shall have the power to do all things necessary or convenient in carrying out his or her functions and for the purposes of achievement of the objectives of this Act.

#### 7 Director of Surveys

(1) There shall be a Director of Lands and Surveys.

(2) The Director shall be appointed by the Chief Secretary on the recommendation of the Secretary.

(3) The Director shall be a registered surveyor under this Act.

(4) The Director reports to the Secretary on all such matters that is necessary under this Act.

#### 8 Functions of the Director

(1) The functions of the Director for the purposes of this Act shall be to:

(a) maintain a national geodetic system;

(b) maintain a national survey control system;

(c) establish and maintain a coordinate system;

(d) administer, coordinate and extend geodetic control networks and traverses, precise levelling or other precision instruments forming the national survey control system;

(e) maintain permanent reference marks governing or providing subsidiary controls for any survey of land;

(f) provide survey, survey computation, mapping, cartography, land information and other related services;

(g) investigate the status of and title to any land required for reservation or allocation for public purposes;

(h) approve and certify plans and surveys in accordance with this Act and any other applicable written law;

(i) coordinate the establishment, maintenance and preservation of the land survey system for the purpose of determining the positions of boundaries that govern the extent of interests in land;

(j) coordinate the establishment and maintenance of a geographical positioning system for the Republic;

(k) coordinate the establishment and maintenance of a mapping system for the Republic;

(l) coordinate the development and maintenance of a land-related information systems network for the Republic;

(m) maintain the network of survey control markers and controlled photographic diapositives that are the physical elements of the geographical positioning system;

(n) register and maintain certified survey data;

(o) set standards and issue guidelines for the structure, storage and provision of survey data;

(p) provide advice to the Minister, Secretary and other person responsible under any written law for a tenure system on matters relating to the spatial definition of interests under the system; and

(q) perform such other functions which the Minister or the Secretary may from time to time assign to the Director.

(2) In exercising his or her functions, the Director shall have regard to:

(a) the risks of inaccuracies in cadastral surveys to the Republic, landowners or any other parties who may have any interest in a land;

(b) the efficiency and effectiveness of measures to manage risks to the accuracy of cadastral surveys;

(c) the efficiency with which the costs and benefits of those measures will be allocated among the Republic, surveyors, current and future owners of land and other parties; and

(d) the use of cadastral survey data for a purpose other than a cadastral survey.

#### 9 Powers of the Director

The Director has the power to do all things necessary or convenient to be done for the performance of his or her functions under this Act or any other written law. s 8LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

## PART 3 — SURVEYORS AND FIELD OFFICERS

### DIVISION 1 — SURVEYORS

#### 10 Register of Surveyors

(1) There shall be a Register of Surveyors, which shall be kept and maintained by the Secretary.

(2) The Register of Surveyors shall contain:

(a) the name and address of a surveyor who is registered and holds a current practising certificate under this Act; and

(b) any other prescribed particulars.

(3) Upon the payment of prescribed fees:

(a) the Register shall be made available for inspection by any person during business hours; and

(b) certified copies of the entries in the Register be provided.

(4) The Secretary may cause the Register of Surveyors or any part of it to be published in the Gazette.

(5) The fee charged under subsection (3) shall be paid into the Treasury Fund.

#### 11 Application to register as surveyor

(1) A person is eligible to be registered as a surveyor if he or she:

(a) holds the prescribed qualifications; and

(b) is registered as a surveyor in a prescribed country.

(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1), a Nauruan citizen is eligible to be registered as a surveyor, where he or she:

(a) has 10 years of experience as a field officer;

(b) has been engaged or assisted a surveyor in surveying for at least 10 years;

(c) has demonstrated ability to undertake independent cadastral and topographic survey;

(d) is familiar with the use, interpretation and analysis of any data or information of the total station; and

(e) has the knowledge and capacity to use, interpret and analyse information using the Liscad program.

(3) An application for registration as a surveyor shall be:

(a) made in the prescribed form;

(b) accompanied by prescribed particulars;

(c) accompanied by evidence of payment of the prescribed fee; and

(d) made to the Secretary.

#### 12 Decision on application for registration as surveyor

(1) Upon receipt of an application under Section 12, the Secretary shall consider the application and within 30 days from date the application is made, he or she with the recommendation of the Director may:

(a) grant the application;

(b) require further information before granting or declining the application; and

(c) decline the application.

(2) Where the Secretary declines the application under subsection (1)(c), he or she shall provide the reasons for the decision.

#### 13 Certificate of registration

(1) Where the application is granted under Section 12(1), the Secretary shall issue a Certificate of registration.

(2) The granting of the Certificate may be subject to special or general conditions as the Secretary considers necessary.

#### 14 Certificate of annual practice

(1) The Secretary shall issue a practicing certificate to a registered surveyor, for him or her to practice as a surveyor.

(2) The practising certificate is valid for 12 months.

(3) The fee for the annual practicing certificate shall be prescribed.

#### 15 Renewal of practising certificate

(1) A registered surveyor shall apply for renewal of his or her practising certificate before the expiration of the practising certificate.

(2) An application for renewal shall be:

(a) made in the prescribed form;

(b) accompanied by prescribed particulars;

(c) accompanied by evidence of payment of the prescribed fee; and

(d) made to the Secretary.

(3) Upon receipt of an application for renewal under subsection (1), the Secretary with the recommendation of the Director shall consider the application for renewal and upon being satisfied that a surveyor has been practising in accordance with this Act and regulations, the Secretary shall renew the practising certificate.

(4) A practising certificate may only be renewed for a period of 12 months from the date of expiry of the previous practising certificate.

(5) A practising certificate issued under subsection (3) may be subject to special or general conditions as the Secretary considers necessary.

#### 16 Surveyor to notify change

(1) A surveyor who changes his name, address or any other prescribed particulars shall, within 21 days from the date of the change, notify the Secretary in writing of the change.

(2) Upon receipt of the notification, the Secretary shall update the Register of Surveyors.

#### 17 Amendment of the Register of Surveyors

(1) Where the Secretary is notified by a surveyor of a change to his or her name, or that the name of a surveyor is incorrectly stated in the Register of Surveyors, the Secretary shall correct any entry in the Register of Surveyors relating to the surveyor.

(2) The Secretary may, where he or she considers appropriate, on the written s 12LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU application of a surveyor for the removal of his or her name, remove the name of the surveyor from the Register of Surveyors.

### DIVISION 2 — FIELD OFFICERS

#### 18 Appointment of field officer

The Secretary may, in consultation with the Director, appoint a person as a field officer where the Secretary is satisfied that the person:

(a) is employed by a surveyor or working as an assistant with the surveyor;

(b) has assisted the Director or any other surveyor with conducting a cadastral survey; and

(c) is capable of conducting a cadastral survey on his or her own under the supervision of a surveyor.

#### 19 Licence

(1) A person appointed as a field officer under Section 18 shall be issued a licence.

(2) A licence issued under subsection (1) may be subject to special or general conditions.

(3) A licence is valid for 12 months.

#### 20 Cancellation of licence

(1) The Secretary may cancel the licence of a field officer, where the Secretary is satisfied that the field officer:

(a) contravenes a provision of this Act or regulations;

(b) falsifies a survey or a survey plan; or

(c) is convicted of a criminal offence.

(2) Where a field officer ceases to be employed by the Department, the appointment and licence of the field officer are deemed to have been revoked.

#### 21 Renewal of licence

(1) Where the Secretary is satisfied that a field officer has been practising in accordance with this Act and regulations, the Secretary shall renew the licence of a field officer.

(2) A licence shall only be renewed for a period of 12 months from the date of expiry of the previous licence.

#### 22 General duties of surveyors and field officers

In conducting a survey, a surveyor and a field officer shall:

(a) comply with this Act and regulations; and

(b) have regard to the risks of inaccuracies in cadastral surveys to the Republic and owners or interest in land. s 22 SURVEY ACT 2025

## PART 4 — SURVEYS

#### 23 Survey

For the purposes of this Act, ‘survey’ means a survey of land made or to be made:

(a) for the purposes of a written law relating to the alienation, leasing, occupation or subdivision of land, or relating to the titles to land, in the Republic;

(b) by the Republic;

(c) at the request of the landowner, lessee or of a person interested in becoming the landowner;

(d) in relation to the resumption or compulsory acquisition of land in the Republic pursuant to a written law;

(e) re-survey of old marks at an interval of every 10 years, for confirmation of boundaries due to tectonic movements;

(f) for the purpose of establishing the location of:

(i) a public road;

(ii) a common road;

(iii) a public reserve;

(iv) high water mark boundaries;

(v) public easements;

(vi) the boundary of any land; or

(g) any other surveys that may be necessary by virtue of any written law, directions from the Cabinet, Minister or Secretary.

#### 24 Who may conduct a survey

(1) A survey shall only be conducted by a surveyor or a field officer.

(2) A survey plan shall be a correct delineation of the boundaries of the land surveyed and marked or pegged on the ground.

(3) Every survey plan shall be certified by the Director.

#### 25 Instrument to be used in making survey

In conducting a survey:

(a) angles may be measured by means of a total station in accurate adjustment or the Global Navigation Satellite System; and

(b) distance may be measured by means of a total station and Global Navigation Satellite System, a steel or an invar band approved by the Director.

#### 26 Measurement of line

In conducting a survey, the most direct method that is reasonably practicable for measuring a line shall be used.

#### 27 Measurements to be in the metric system of measurement

(1) All measurements in the survey plan shall be in the metric system of measurement.

(2) Where necessary, a surveyor or field officer may state the measurement in the imperial system after recording the metric system of measurement.

#### 28 Boundaries to be marked

(1) In conducting a survey, boundaries shall be marked at every angle and where necessary, at a point on the boundary line in accordance with this Act and regulations.

(2) Where the boundary of a portion crosses a tract of inaccessible land, the boundary shall be surveyed and marked on either side of and up to, the accessible part.

(3) A mark shall be established at the terminal point which shall, when practicable, be connected by a traverse survey detouring the obstacle or by triangulation.

(4) Where it is not possible to place the boundary mark on the actual boundary due to the nature of the ground or any other cause, the mark shall be placed as near as possible to the boundary and reference from the mark to the unmarked boundary shall be shown.

(5) Where there are no original survey marks, the surveyor or field officer shall indicate that the boundaries and corners have been reinstated and correctly measured in relation to adjacent boundaries, fences and ditches or any other evidence of original occupation that may be found.

#### 29 Survey data

(1) The Director shall be responsible for registering and maintaining approved certified survey data.

(2) All registered surveyors and field officers shall register their survey data with the Director.

(3) All survey data shall be verified and certified by the Director prior to being published.

(4) The survey data registered and maintained under subsection (1) shall be the true representation of the data collected from a land survey.

(5) The Director at his or her discretion may publish survey data in the Gazette within 14 working days from the date it is registered.

(6) If there is a conflict between the survey data produced by surveyors within the Department and independent surveyors, the Director shall have the final decision on the correct survey data in accordance to this Act and regulation.

#### 30 Re-survey of land

Where land is being re-surveyed, the boundaries of the land as marked on the ground shall be deemed to be the true boundaries of the land notwithstanding that those boundaries do not correspond with the boundaries of the land as shown on a relevant plan of the land.

#### 31 Fixing boundary when monument missing

In conducting a survey, where an existing monument relevant to establishing the position of a boundary of the land is not located after diligent search, the surveyor or field officer making the survey shall, after investigating fully the position of the boundary, determine the position of the boundary by reference to: s 27LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

(a) the boundaries of the adjacent parcels of land;

(b) the old registered survey marks; and

(c) such other matters relevant to determining the position of the boundary as the surveyor or field officer discovers during the search.

#### 32 Datum line of azimuth

(1) A surveyor or field officer shall not adopt a line as the datum line of azimuth of a survey unless the position of the monuments that establish the position of the line has been determined.

(2) For the purposes of a line adopted as the datum line of azimuth of a survey, a surveyor or field officer shall:

(a) where the bearing of the azimuth of the line is shown on an official plan of survey, accept the bearing as the correct bearing of the azimuth for the purposes of the survey; or

(b) in any other case, determine the bearing of the azimuth by astronomical observations.

#### 33 Accuracy of survey

(1) The accuracy of the measurement of each line which forms part of a surround of land being surveyed shall be checked by calculating, to two decimal places, the length of the line by the method known as the closure of the latitudes and departures.

(2) Land is deemed to have been surveyed with appropriate accuracy where the ratio of the sum of the differences of the latitudes and departures of the perimeter to the sum of the latitudes and departures of the perimeter does not exceed the ratio of one to six thousand.

#### 34 Field notes

(1) A surveyor or field officer shall make and preserve exact and regular field notes that conform to professional standards whether written or electronic forms of all surveys made by the surveyor.

(2) The field notes shall:

(a) contain a record of all observations and measurements made by the surveyor or field officer;

(b) contain the marks found or placed by the surveyor or field officer, including diagrams;

(c) contain a record of the make, model and serial number of all survey equipment used for the survey, including the calibration factor and date of calibration;

(d) be neatly and clearly recorded in black pencil or erasable pen so that a surveyor or draftsperson may draw a correct plan of the survey;

(e) ensure that all bearings entered in the field notes are expressed in degrees, minutes and seconds of arc calculated clockwise from zero to 360 degrees;

(f) on the first page, show the title of the survey, the district, portion or such other reference which may adequately identify the land surveyed, the date of commencement of the survey and the signature of the surveyor or field officer;

(g) on each page, to be initialled by the surveyor or field officer; s 34 SURVEY ACT 2025

(h) specify the nature and position of each monument found during the survey and if the surveyor or field officer was unable, after diligent search, to locate an existing monument:

(i) full particulars of the investigations carried out by the surveyor or field officer in attempting to find the monument;

(ii) the point that the surveyor or field officer has determined to be the position of the monument; and

(iii) all measurements taken by the surveyor or field officer in relation to the point, assuming that point is the location of the monument; and

(i) specify the position of the datum line and the origin of the azimuth adopted for the purposes of the survey.

(3) The particulars of each structure found, bearing taken and measurement and observation made for the purpose of a survey shall be recorded in the field notes immediately after the structure was found, bearing was taken or measurement or observation was made, as the case may be.

(4) A surveyor shall ensure that the field notes are properly indexed and recorded and shall permit access to field notes, records or indexes or provide copies of them to any person concerned.

#### 35 Calculation of area

The area of each portion of land shall be calculated mathematically or through the Liscad program.

#### 36 Survey Plan

(1) Where a surveyor or field officer prepares a plan representing a survey, he or she shall mark the datum line of azimuth of the survey by distinguishing letters placed at the ends of the line and indicate the nature of the marks which define that line on the plan.

(2) A plan representing a survey prepared under subsection (1) shall bear the printed name and signature of the surveyor or field officer conducting the survey.

#### 37 Records of survey plans

(1) The Director shall keep and maintain records of survey plans.

(2) A survey plan shall be kept and maintained in physical and electronic form so as to enable the Director to provide such information in accordance with this Act.

#### 38 Access to survey plans

The Director shall upon the payment of prescribed fees:

(a) permit a person to access and inspection a survey plan during business hours; and

(b) ensure that a certified copy of a survey plan be provided.

#### 39 Plan of survey to be certified

A plan of a survey shall be certified by a surveyor or field officer in the prescribed form, to certify that a survey made by the surveyor or field officer was made in accordance with this Act. s 34LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

#### 40 Survey Plan to be deposited

(1) A surveyor or field officer conducting a survey shall deposit with the Director within 3 months from the date the survey is completed:

(a) a plan representing the survey;

(b) the field notes relating to the survey; and

(c) a signed declaration attesting that all the required checks under the declaration have been completed.

(2) Where the Director is satisfied that a survey is made in accordance with this Act and regulations and that the plan of the survey deposited with him or her represents the survey accurately, he or she shall certify the plan and provide a copy of the plan to the Nauru Lands Committee.

#### 41 Inspection of survey

(1) Where a survey plan has been prepared and has not been deposited with the Director, the Director may inspect the survey or order an inspection to be made by a surveyor appointed by the Director.

(2) The Director or a surveyor appointed under subsection (1) shall:

(a) inspect any survey for which an application has been made and any survey for which the Director believes there is sufficient reason to do so; and

(b) consider any evidence that may have been presented to the surveyor who carried out the survey.

(3) Where the Director conducts an inspection under subsection (2), the Director shall prepare a report and submit such report to the Secretary.

(4) Where a surveyor conducts an inspection under subsection (2), the surveyor shall prepare a report and submit it to the Director, together with any record that the Director may request.

(5) Where there is an error in the survey plan, the Director may, in writing, require the surveyor or field officer responsible for the error to undertake or arrange to be undertaken, the work necessary to correct the error within such time as the Director considers reasonable.

#### 42 Geographical positioning and mapping system

The Director may, where he or she deems necessary, coordinate the provision of a global positioning system or a mapping system.

#### 43 Survey control

(1) A survey control shall only be established by a surveyor in accordance with the specifications and instructions of the Director.

(2) Where the Director confirms the coordinate position of a survey control marker, the survey control marker shall become an integral part of the global positioning system.

#### 44 Recognition of survey plan

A court may give judicial notice of a survey plan prepared by a surveyor or field officer and approved by the Director. s 44 SURVEY ACT 2025

## PART 5 — OFFENCES

#### 45 Conducting survey without registration or licence

(1) A person shall not conduct a survey or prepare a survey plan unless he or she is:

(a) a registered surveyor; or

(b) a licensed field officer.

(2) A person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and on conviction is liable to a fine not exceeding $2,000 or a term of imprisonment not exceeding 12 months or to both.

#### 46 Incorrect certificate

(1) A person shall not certify a plan that purports to represent a survey, unless:

(a) that person is a surveyor or a field officer;

(b) if that person is a surveyor, the survey was made by him or her or under his or her personal supervision or if that person is a field officer, the survey was made by him or her;

(c) the survey was made with appropriate accuracy;

(d) the survey was made in accordance with the provisions of this Act;

(e) the survey is truly represented by the plan; and

(f) the survey was completed on the date stated certified to be the date when the survey was completed.

(a) assaults, interferes with, obstructs or hinders the Director, a surveyor or field officer in the performance of his or her duties or the exercise of any powers prescribed under this Act;

(b) induces or incites any other person to hinder or obstruct the Director, a surveyor or field officer acting in accordance with this Act;

#### 48 Interference with monument or survey mark

(1) A person shall not take, destroy or alter the position of or markings on, a monument or survey mark which has been placed under the authority of this Act.

(2) A person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and on conviction is liable to a fine not exceeding $50,000 or a term of imprisonment not exceeding 10 years or to both.

(3) Where a person is convicted for contravening this Section, the court may order that the person pay the costs of repairing, replacing or restoring the survey mark to its original position, including survey costs. s 48LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

## PART 6 — MISCELLANEOUS

#### 49 Entering land for survey

(1) For the purposes of conducting or inspecting a survey, the Director or a surveyor or field officer authorised by the Director may, using reasonable care, pass over or through the land of any person to:

(a) measure along and ascertain the bearings of any line or boundary;

(b) affix or set up survey control stations, survey pegs, marks or poles;

(c) alter, repair, move or remove any survey control stations, survey pegs, marks or poles; and

(d) do all things necessary for such survey or inspection.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), the Director, surveyor or field officer shall, as far as practicable, give reasonable notice to the proprietor or occupier of the land of their intention to enter the land.

(3) The surveyor or field officer may be liable for any damage the surveyor or the field officer may cause to any land in conducting a survey.

#### 50 Use of drones

(1) For the purposes of this Act, a surveyor or field officer may use a drone without the approval required under the Drones Act 2018, except where prior approval of Civil Aviation Authority is required for aviation purposes in writing.

(2) For the purposes of this Act, the Secretary shall not be required to obtain any permit or approval to import a drone as required under the Drones Act 2018.

#### 51 Evidential value of copy of record, document, plan, book or

paper A copy of any record, document, plan, book or paper belonging to or deposited with the Director and attested under the signature of the Secretary, the Director or any person authorised in writing by the Minister shall be admitted in evidence in a case in which the original record, document, plan, book or paper is admissible as evidence.

#### 52 Protection from liability

The Minister, Secretary, Director, Department, surveyor, a field officer or an employee of the Department shall not be subject to any civil or criminal liability, action, claim or demand for any act done or omitted to be done in good faith in the performance of a function or exercise of a power under this Act or any written law.

#### 53 Disability or death of a surveyor or field officer

(1) Where a surveyor or field officer becomes physically or mentally impaired to the extent that he or she cannot undertake a survey, the surveyor’s or field officer’s field notes, records and indexes shall be delivered to the Director for the purposes of keeping and maintaining the records.

(2) On the death of surveyor or field officer, the surveyor’s or field officer’s field notes, records and indexes shall be delivered to the Director for the purposes of keeping and maintaining the records.

#### 54 Record keeping obligations

(1) The Secretary or Director shall keep and maintain records of any deliberation, decision, inquiry or any information the Secretary or Director receives in the performance of his or her functions or exercise of powers under this Act.

(2) A record that is required to be kept under this Act shall be kept and maintained in physical and electronic form so as to enable the Secretary or Director to provide such information in accordance with this Act.

#### 55 Regulations

(1) The Cabinet may make regulations to prescribe matters that are required or permitted by this Act to be prescribed or are necessary or convenient to be prescribed for carrying out or giving effect to this Act and generally for achieving the purposes of this Act.

(2) Without limiting subsection (1), the Cabinet may make regulations to prescribe:

(a) survey and plan rules;

(b) symbols and acronyms commonly used in surveys or specifically provided for the Republic;

(c) any matter or thing relating to the establishment and maintenance of survey marks and boundary monuments;

(d) any matter or thing relating to the accuracy of surveys;

(e) the form of the certification of a survey;

(f) fixing the unit of measure to be used on general plans and diagrams;

(g) the form, system and maintenance of the Register of Surveys;

(h) the collection and recording of any information in respect of land; and

(i) any form or fee for the purposes of the Act. s 53LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

## PART 7 — REPEAL AND SAVINGS

#### 56 Repeal

The Survey Act 1961 and Survey Marks Act 1925 are repealed.

#### 57 Savings and transitional provision

(1) A survey map or plan of survey prepared and registered with the Department under the repealed Survey Act 1961 or Survey Marks Act 1925 is deemed to have been made under this Act and remains valid.

(2) Any application, investigation and legal proceedings commenced under the repealed Survey Act 1961 or Survey Marks Act 1925 which are not determined or otherwise dealt with at the commencement of this Act shall continue and may be deemed by the court to have been commenced under the equivalent provisions of this Act.

(3) Any act, appointment, decision, regulation, order, declaration, action, status or capacity that existed under the repealed Survey Act 1961 or Survey Marks Act 1925 continues in force so as to carry out, complete such act or decision, order, declaration, action or unless such status or capacity is revoked under the provisions of this Act.

#### 3 Definitions

‘Auto Level’ or ‘automatic level’ means a device that uses an internal compensator to self-level the line of sight, improving speed and accuracy in levelling tasks;

‘barometer’ means an instrument that measures atmospheric pressure, used in surveying to estimate elevation changes or forecast weather conditions;

‘Digital Level’ means an advanced levelling instrument that reads bar-coded staffs electronically to provide precise elevation data and to ensure minimal human error;

‘dumpy Level’ means a traditional optical levelling instrument with a fixed telescope used to establish horizontal planes and measure elevation differences;

‘Electronic Distance Meter’ or ‘EDM’ means a device that measures the distance between two points using electromagnetic waves, typically light or radio waves, with high precision and speed;

‘GIS’ refers to Geographic Information System;

‘GNSS’ refers to Global Navigation Satellite System;

‘GNSS Receiver comprising base and rover units’ means a high-precision GNSS receiver used in Real-Time Kinematic or static mode to determine precise location;

‘GPS’ refers to Global Positioning System;

‘handheld GPS Units’ means a portable Global Positioning System device that is used for navigation and date collection in the field, often equipped with mapping software and satellite connectivity;

‘Laser Level’ means a levelling tool that projects a visible laser beam to establish a straight horizontal or vertical reference line;

‘Laser rangefinder’ means a device that uses a laser beam to measure the distance to a target by calculating the time it takes for the beam to return;

‘mobile GIS Device’ means smartphones, tablets or rugged handhelds equipped with GIS software and GPS, used for collecting, editing and analysing geospatial data in the field;

‘prism’ means a reflective target used with total station to measure distance;

‘prism pole’ means a graduated pole that holds the prism at a known height for accurate positioning;

‘Real time Kinematic (RTK) GPS’ means a high-precision Global Positioning technique that uses carrier-phase measurements and real-time corrections from a base station to achieve centimeter-level accuracy;

‘Reflector less total station’ means a total station capable of measuring distances without a prism, using laser or infrared technology to reflect off surfaces directly;

‘surveyor’s compass’ means a magnetic compass mounted on a base that is used to measure magnetic bearings;

‘Tachometer’ means an instrument used in tacheometric surveying to measure horizontal distances indirectly by observing angles and stadia intervals through a theodolite or total station; and

‘Theodolite’ means an instrument with a rotating telescope that measures horizontal and vertical angles. reg 3LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

## PART 2 — QUALIFICATION OF SURVERYORS

#### 4 Qualifications of a Surveyor

In addition to the requirement provided under Section 11 of the Act, a person shall have the following qualifications to be eligible to be registered as a surveyor:

(a) a minimum of a graduate diploma or bachelor degree in surveying from a tertiary institution approved by the Secretary; or

(b) qualifications, or qualifications and experience, accredited as being equivalent to the qualifications referred to in paragraph (a) by an institution of surveyors recognised by the Secretary.

## PART 3 — EQUIPMENTS, TOOLS AND SOFTWARE

#### 5 Measuring Equipment

In addition to equipment already provided under the Act, in conducting a survey, a surveyor or field officer shall use the following equipment to measure distance and angles:

(a) total station;

(b) theodolite;

(c) tachometer;

(d) Electronic Distance Meter (EDM);

(e) steel tape and measuring tape;

(f) chain (gunter’s chain or engineer’s chain);

(g) surveyor’s compass;

(h) prism & prism pole;

(i) reflector less total station;

(j) laser rangefinder;

(k) tripod for mounting total stations, theodolites, levels and GNSS receivers;

(l) tribrachs and adapters for precise mounting and centering of equipment and instruments; and

(m) such other equipment or instrument as approved by the Secretary.

#### 6 Leveling Equipment

In conducting a survey, a surveyor or field officer shall use the following equipment to ensure precise levelling:

(a) dumpy level;

(b) auto level;

(c) digital level;

(d) laser level;

(e) barometer; and

(f) such other levelling equipment as approved by the Secretary.

#### 7 GPS and GNSS Equipment

A surveyor or field officer may use the following equipment for geospatial positioning:

(a) GNSS Receiver comprising of the base and rover;

(b) handheld GamePad Digital Units;

(c) Real-time Kinematic (RTK) GPS;

(d) Drones; and

(e) such other equipment as approved by the Secretary.

#### 8 Data Collection and Recording Devices

A surveyor or field officer shall use the following devices and tools for data collection:

(a) field controller or data logger;

(b) survey software (for instance Trimble Access and Leica Captivate);

(c) mobile GIS Device;

(d) surveyor’s field book;

(e) data collector (rugged tablet or handheld device); and

(f) such other device or tool as approve by the Secretary.

#### 9 Marking and Setting out tools

In addition to tools provided under Section 49 of the Act, a surveyor or field officer in conducting a survey shall use the following to mark points on the ground or to assist with making the markings:

(a) survey pegs, stakes, or pins;

(b) chalk, spray paint;

(c) plumb bob;

(d) hammer or mallet;

(e) bush knife, chain saw, or brush cutter; and

(f) such other tools as approve by the Secretary.

#### 10 Aerial Survey Tools

(1) A surveyor or field officer shall use drones for aerial photography and photogrammetry.

(2) A surveyor or field officer shall use drone GPS for accurate positioning.

#### 11 Mapping and Processing Software

For the purposes of the Act and these Regulations, a person shall use the following software programs for mapping and processing of data:

(a) Computer-Aided Design software for instance AutoCAD or LISCAD;

(b) GIS software for instance, ArcGIS or QGIS;

(c) survey processing software for instance Trimble Business Center and Leica Infinity; and

(d) such other software or program approved by the Secretary.

#### 12 Safety and Support Equipment

The following tools and equipment shall be used by surveyors, field officers and any other person involved in the conduct of the survey to ensure fieldwork safety, assist with measurements and protection of equipment:

(a) safety gear, that is vests, boots and helmets;

(b) two-way radios or walkie-talkies;

(c) weather-proof cases & bags;

(d) levelling rod;

(e) surveying rod or levelling rod;

(f) range poles and staffs; and

(g) such other equipment or tool as approved by the Secretary. reg 8LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

## PART 4 — FORMS AND FEES

#### 13 Application to register as a surveyor

(1) For the purposes of Section 11 of the Act, an application form to register as a surveyor is in Form 1 of Schedule 1.

(2) The person applying to be registered as a surveyor shall pay the prescribed fee and provide the following information:

(a) personal and contact details of the applicant;

(b) Certificates for qualifications;

(c) years of experience as a surveyor (if applicable);

(d) evidence to demonstrate ability to undertake independent cadastral and topographic survey of the applicant;

(e) evidence to demonstrate familiarity with the use, interpretation and analysis of any data or information of the total station of the applicant;

(f) evidence to demonstrate knowledge and capacity to use, interpret and analyse information using Liscad program of the applicant;

(g) copy of identification, either bio page of passport or drivers licence or birth certificate of the applicant; and

(h) copy of police clearance certificate of the applicant.

(3) A person shall not provide false or misleading information in the application.

(4) A person who contravenes this provision, commits an offence and shall be liable upon conviction to an imprisonment term of 5 years or his or her application being declined.

#### 14 Form of Certificate of annual practice

For the purpose of Sections 13 and 14 of the Act, the Secretary shall issue a certificate of annual practice in Form 2 of Schedule 1.

#### 15 Renewal form

(1) For the purpose of Section 15 of the Act, a person shall make an application for renewal of certificate of annual practice at least 1 month prior to the expiry date of the licence in Form 3 of Schedule 1.

(2) A person shall not provide false or misleading information in the application.

(3) A person who contravenes this provision, commits an offence and shall be liable upon conviction to an imprisonment term of 5 years or his or her application being declined.

#### 16 Suspension and cancellation of registration as a surveyor

(1) The Secretary may suspend or cancel a surveyor’s certificate of annual practice where the surveyor:

(a) contravenes a provision of this Act and these Regulations;

(b) falsifies a survey or a survey plan;

(c) is convicted of a criminal offence; or

(d) has not paid the prescribed fee.

(2) Where the Secretary has suspended or cancelled the certificate of annual practice of a surveyor, the Secretary shall advise the surveyor in writing of the Secretary’s decision and the reasons for such decision.

#### 17 Form of field officer licence

For the purpose of Section 19 of the Act, the Secretary shall issue a licence in Form 4 of Schedule 1 and any condition prescribed under the Act and these Regulations.

#### 18 Drone survey

(1) A person may apply to the Secretary for his or her land to be surveyed using a drone.

(2) An application under subregulation (1) shall be:

(a) in Form 5 of Schedule 1; and

(b) accompanied by the prescribed fee in Schedule 2.

(3) An application made under this regulation shall provide the following information:

(a) personal and contact details of the applicant;

(b) description and location of land to be surveyed; and

(c) statement of reason for request.

#### 19 Fees

(1) The prescribed fees to be paid for the purposes of the Act and these Regulations are set out in the Schedule 2.
