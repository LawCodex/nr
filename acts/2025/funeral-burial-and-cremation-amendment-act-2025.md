# Funeral Burial and Cremation (Amendment) Act 2025

FUNERAL BURIAL AND CREMATION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2025 _____________________________ No.  27 of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Funeral Burial and Cremation Act 2017 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 19 June 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Funeral Burial and Cremation (Amendment) Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Funeral Burial and Cremation Act 2017

The Funeral Burial and Cremation Act 2017 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 18

Section 18 is amended:

(a) in subsection (1) by deleting ‘No’ and substituting with ‘Subject to subsection

(2), no’;

(b) by deleting subsection (2) and substituting with:

‘(2) A person may apply to the President and the President may grant permission for a person to bury any human remains, at the place of the deceased person’s residence or other place.’; and

(c) by inserting a new subsection (3) as follows:

‘(3) A person who contravenes subsection (1), commits an offence and upon conviction is liable to a fine not exceeding $5,000 or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding 12 months or to both.’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 53

Section  53 is amended:

(a) in subsection (1) by deleting ‘No’ and substituting with ‘Subject to subsection

(2), no’;

(b) by deleting subsection (2) and substituting with:

‘(2) A person may apply to the President and the President may grant permission for a person to bury any human remains, ashes or other remains from a cremation at the place of the deceased person’s residence or other place.’; and

(c) by inserting a new subsection (3) as follows:

‘(3) A person who contravenes subsection (1), commits an offence and upon conviction is liable to a fine not exceeding $10,000 or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding 12 months or to both.’.
