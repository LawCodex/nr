# Liquor Control (Amendment) Act 2025

_____________________________ No.  16  of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Liquor Control Act 2017 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 9 April 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Liquor Control (Amendment) Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Liquor Control Act 2017

The Liquor Control Act 2017 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Insertion of Part 7A

Part 7A is inserted after Part 7 as follows:

‘PART 7A – KAVA 41A Definitions

‘kava’ means liquid or powder made, obtained or extracted from the plant of the species piper methysticum; and

‘kava bar’ means a place where kava is sold or served to customers ready to be consumed. 41B Kava bar Licence

(1) A person shall apply to the Board for a licence to operate a kava bar.

(2) The Board may grant a kava bar licence to an applicant in the prescribed form and upon payment of the prescribed fee.

(3) The Board may impose conditions on a kava bar licence. 41C Person prohibited from operating kava bar without kava bar licence

(1) A person shall not operate a kava bar except where he or she has a kava bar licence.

(2) A person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and shall be liable upon conviction to a fine not exceeding $2,000 or an imprisonment term not exceeding 6 months, or both. 41D Kava bar licence to be displayed in kava bar

(1) A holder of a kava bar licence shall display such licence in his or her kava bar.

(2) A person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and shall be liable upon conviction to a fine not exceeding $1,000. 41E Kava bar to close at 12 midnight or other prescribed time

(1) The holder of a kava bar licence shall close the kava bar at 12 o’clock midnight or any other prescribed time and require all the customers to leave the premises.

(2) The holder of a kava bar licence who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and shall be liable upon conviction to a fine not exceeding $1,000.

(3) Where a licenced kava bar fails to comply with this requirement when directed by a licence inspector, authorised or police officer, the Board may consider cancelling the licence. 41F Transitional provision

(1) A person operating a kava bar before the commencement of the Liquor (Amendment) Act 2025 shall obtain a licence within 1 month of the commencement of the Liquor (Amendment) Act 2025.

(2) A person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence under Section 41C.’.
