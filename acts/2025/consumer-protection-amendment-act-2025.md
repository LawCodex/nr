# Consumer Protection (Amendment) Act 2025

CONSUMER PROTECTION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2025 _____________________________ No. 19 of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Consumer Protection Act 2024 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 13 May 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Consumer Protection (Amendment) Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Consumer Protection Act 2024

The Consumer Protection Act 2024 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Insertion of Section 74

A new Section 74 is inserted as follows:

‘74     Exemption

(1) The Minister in consultation with the Cabinet may make a determination:

(a) exempting from any or all of the provisions of this Act:

(i) a person; or

(ii) an activity or class of activities; or

(b) modifying, the application of this Act to:

(i) a person; or

(ii) an activity or class of activities.

(2) A determination under subsection (1) shall not be made unless the Cabinet is satisfied that the person or activity covered by the determination is regulated under anti-competition legislation and supervised by relevant authorities of another country.’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 74

Section 74 is renumbered as Section 75.

#### 6 Amendment of Section 75

Section 75 is renumbered as Section 76.
