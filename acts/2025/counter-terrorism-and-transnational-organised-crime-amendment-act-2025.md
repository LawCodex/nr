# Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime (Amendment) Act 2025

COUNTER TERRORISM AND TRANSNATIONAL ORGANISED CRIME (AMENDMENT) ACT 2025 _____________________________ No. 18 of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime Act 2004 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 13 May 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime (Amendment) Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime

Act 2004 The Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime Act 2004 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Insertion of Section 81

Section 81 is inserted after Section 80 as follows:

‘81     Exemption

(1) The Minister in consultation with the Cabinet may make a determination:

(a) exempting from any or all of the provisions of this Act:

(i) a person; or

(ii) an activity or class of activities; or

(b) modifying, the application of this Act to:

(i) a person; or

(ii) an activity or class of activities.

(2) A determination under subsection (1) shall not be made unless the Cabinet is satisfied that the person or activity covered by the determination is adequately regulated under counter terrorism and transnational organised crime legislation and supervised by relevant authorities of another country.’.
