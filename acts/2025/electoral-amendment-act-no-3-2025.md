# Electoral (Amendment) Act (No. 3) 2025

_____________________________ No.  27 of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Electoral Act 2016 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 25 July 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Electoral (Amendment) Bill (No. 3) 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Electoral Act 2016

The Electoral Act 2016 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 41

Section 41(3)(b) is amended by deleting ‘$500’ and substituting with ‘$250, to be paid after a decision for transfer has been approved under Section 42’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 42

Section 42(1) is amended:

(a) by inserting ‘the Roll which the Cabinet has considered and approved as follows’ after ‘entered is’; and

(b) in paragraph (e) by deleting ‘the Cabinet has considered and granted that’.

#### 6 Amendment of Section 43

Section 43 is amended by inserting the following new subsection (4):

‘(4)  Where a person applies to register as a voter by virtue of being a new citizen of Nauru, he or she shall provide a copy of:

(a) his or her Certificate of Citizenship granted under the Naoero Citizenship Act 2017; and

(b) the gazette Notice published under Section 7(6)(b) of the Naoero Citizenship Act 2017.’

#### 7 Amendment of Section 46

Section 46 is amended by deleting ‘5’ and substituting with ‘7’.

#### 8 Amendment of Section 66

Section 66(1) is amended by deleting ‘7.00am’ and substituting with

‘9.00am’.

#### 9 Amendment of Section 70

Section 70(3) is amended by deleting ‘lock’ and substituting with ‘seal’.

#### 10 Amendment of Section 120

Section 120 is amended by inserting:

(a) in the Section heading ‘during early voting or’ after ‘Campaigning material’; and

(b) in the provision, ‘during early voting under Section 78A’ after ‘who’.

#### 11 Amendment of Section 122

Section 122 is amended by inserting:

(a) in the Section heading ‘during early voting or’ after ‘Campaigning’; and

(b) in the provision, ‘during early voting under Section 78A or’ after ‘if the person’.

#### 12 Amendment of Section 136

Section 136 is amended by deleting ‘Cabinet’ and substituting with ‘Electoral Commission’.

#### 13 Regulations in force at the commencement of this Act

A regulation in force before the commencement of this Act shall be deemed to have been made by the Electoral Commission.
