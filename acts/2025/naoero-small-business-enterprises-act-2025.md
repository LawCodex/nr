## PART 1 — PRELIMINARY

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Naoero Small Business Enterprises Act 2025.

[s 1 am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Objectives of this Act

The objectives of this Act are to:

(a) establish a Small Business Enterprise Framework;

(b) establish the National Coordinating Committee for Small Business Enterprises;

(c) establish the Small Business Enterprises Fund;

(d) encourage Naoero citizens to engage in Small Business Enterprise for development of business skills and for alternative or supplementary source of income; and

(e) create business activities to provide employment to Naoero citizens in Small Business Enterprises.

[s 3 am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 4 Interpretation

In this Act:

‘Committee’ means the National Coordinating Committee for Small Business Enterprises established under Part 2;

‘Department’ means the Department responsible for Commerce and Foreign Investment;

‘Minister’, means the Minister responsible for this Act;

‘Secretary’ means the Secretary for the Department responsible for this Act; and

‘small business enterprise’ means a separate and distinct business entity, including associations and non-governmental organisations, managed by one owner or more of a small business enterprise under the Schedule.

## PART 2 — NATIONAL COORDINATING COMMITTEE FOR SMALL BUSINESS ENTERPRISES

#### 5 Establishment of the National Coordinating Committee for

Small Business Enterprises

(1) There shall be a National Coordinating Committee for Small Business Enterprises.

(2) The Committee shall comprise of:

(a) the Secretary who shall be the Chairperson;

(b) a representative of the Chamber of Commerce appointed by the Minister in consultation with the Chamber of Commerce;

(c) a representative from the land owners, appointed by the Minister with the approval of the Cabinet; and

(d) a Naoero citizen small business enterprises owner, appointed by the Minister with the approval of the Cabinet.

[subs (2) am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

(3) The Secretary shall appoint an employee of the Department to be the secretary of the Committee.

#### 6 Functions of the Committee

(1) The Committee shall:

(a) initiate and articulate policies for small business enterprises which meets the requirements of the objectives of this Act;

(b) serve as a vanguard agency and focal point for poverty alleviation and eradication, technology acquisition and adaptation, job creation and sustainable livelihood;

(c) promote and facilitate development programmes, instruments and support services to accelerate development, modernisation, networking and linkage of small business enterprises;

(d) mobilise internal and external resources, including technical assistance for small scale industries, their support institutions, and non-governmental organisation;

(e) oversee, co-ordinate and monitor development in small business enterprises;

(f) provide industrial extension services to small business enterprises, fabricators of machinery and beneficiaries of micro-credit loans;

(g) design, package and promote small scale commercial projects;

(h) establish liaison between Naoero Chamber of Commerce, local business community and proprietors of small business enterprises;

(i) link small business enterprises to sources of finance, technology, technical skill development and management;

(j) provide necessary assistance to small business enterprises in the marketing of their products;

(k) promote sub-contracting, clustering and networking relationship;

(l) encourage and promote strategic technical and business arrangements within small scale commercial enterprises;

(m) establish and co-ordinate the institutional development and activities of businesses in the Republic;

(n) collaborate with the agricultural research and mechanisation centres and agro-industrial schemes in the Republic or the Pacific region;

(o) liaise with external agencies for support and development of small business enterprises in the Republic;

(p) list commercial activities or products that small business enterprises have substantial internal capacities to conduct, produce or provide as services;

(q) recommend to the Government, in consultation with other relevant agencies and organisations, on applicable tariff regimes and other financial incentives for promoting the development of small business enterprises;

(r) monitor the implementation of and compliance with Government directives, incentives and facilities for development in small business enterprises in order to promote government policies in or outside the Republic;

(s) carry out such other activities connected with or incidental to the other functions of the Committee in order to promote Government policies in or outside the Republic;

(t) provide and facilitate technical and managerial training to small business enterprises;

(u) establish the eligibility criteria for accessing the Small Business Enterprises Fund; and

(v) any other prescribed function.

[subs (1) am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

The Committee has the power to do all things necessary or convenient to be done for the performance of its functions under this Act or any other written law. s 6LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

## PART 3 — SMALL BUSINESS ENTERPRISES FUND

#### 8 Establishment and purpose

(1) The Small Business Enterprises Fund is established for the purpose of facilitating the funding of small businesses by enabling:

(a) the payment of amounts under subsection (2) into the Fund; and

(b) the withdrawal of amounts under subsection (2) from the Fund.

(2) The Fund consists of the following:

(a) any amount appropriated for the Fund from the Treasury Fund;

(b) any amount received by the Republic by way of donor grant or aid; and

(c) any other Funds paid into the account for meeting the objectives of this Act.

#### 9 Withdrawal from the Fund

A withdrawal from the Fund may only be made where the requirements determined by the Committee are met.

#### 10 Eligibility for receiving assistance from the Fund

(1) A small business enterprise may be eligible to receive assistance from the Fund where such enterprise meets the following requirements:

(a) it is a corporation with 70% held or controlled by a Naoero citizen;

(b) it is a partnership where at least 70% of the partners are Naoero citizens;

(c) it is a sole trading business of a Naoero citizen;

(d) the nature of the proposed or existing small business enterprise which qualifies for funding under the Schedule;

(e) the gross projected income or the existing income of a small business enterprise shall not exceed $75,000 per annum;

(f) shall provide a business plan clearly demonstrating the principal objectives, outcomes and sustainability of the business; and

(g) any such other requirements which the Committee or the Cabinet approves.

[subs (1) am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

(2) The procedure for making and consideration of applications for small business enterprise assistance shall be as prescribed.

(3) The Committee may decline to provide any remaining or additional funds to a person who has been selected to receive assistance as a small business enterprise, where he or she fails to comply with the requirements of Section 11.

#### 11 Requirement to account for funds

A person who is granted assistance by provision of funds to commence or carry on a small business enterprise shall:

(a) expend the funds consistent with the purpose for which it was sought;

(b) allow an authorised person to have access to the records of the expenditure if required by the Committee;

(c) provide a 6-monthly report on the:

(i) utilisation of the funds; and

(ii) progress of the small business enterprise; and

(d) seek from the Department financial or business advice if he or she so requires in the conduct of the business to ensure the business succeeds.

#### 12 Secretary access records of expenditure

(1) The Secretary or a person authorised by the Secretary may request access to the records of a small business enterprise.

(2) The proprietor of a small business enterprise shall grant access where it is requested under subsection (1).

(3) A proprietor who contravenes subsection (2) may be subject to a decision of the Committee declining further assistance under Section 10(3). s 11LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

## PART 4 — MISCELLANEOUS

#### 13 Offence provision

(1) A person shall not in making an application under this Act provide false or misleading information.

(2) A person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and upon conviction is liable:

(a) in the case of an individual to a fine not exceeding $1,000 or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding 6 months or both; or

(b) in the case of a body corporate, to a fine not exceeding $5,000.

(3) Where an offence has been committed by a body corporate, a person who at the time of the commission of the offence was a director, manager, secretary, officer or partner of a body corporate or was purporting to act in such capacity, commits an offence and is liable under subsection (2)(a) where the following is deemed to have occurred:

(a) the act or omission constituting the offence took place with the actual knowledge or reckless disregard of the director, manager, secretary, officer or partner; and

(b) the director, manager, secretary, officer or partner, by act or omission, gave his or her authority, permission or consent to the act or omission constituting the offence.

#### 14 Regulations

(1) The Cabinet may make regulations prescribing all matters necessary or convenient to be prescribed to give effect to this Act.

(2) Without limiting subsection (1), the Cabinet may make regulations to:

(a) amend the Schedule; and

(b) provide for the procedure for making and consideration of applications.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Application process

(1) A small business enterprise eligible under Section 10 may apply to the Committee for funding under the Act.

(2) An application for funding shall:

(a) be in the Form under the Schedule;

(b) provide the required information specified under Regulation 4 and in the Form; and

(c) such other information as requested by the Committee.

#### 4 Required information

The following information is required to be provided when an applicant submits his or her application:

(a) applicant’s name, address, occupation and contact details;

(b) tax identification number from the Naoero Revenue Office;

(c) type of business (individual, partnership, corporation or trust);

(d) detailed nature of the business activity to be undertaken by the small business enterprise;

(e) number of years in business (if applicable);

(f) number of persons to be employed (if applicable);

(g) amount of the funding requested;

(h) statement of the purpose for requesting the funds and details of the use of such funds;

(i) copies of birth certificate, drivers licence or bio page of passport of the applicant or owners of the small business enterprise;

(j) copy of the business proposal or plan;

(k) business licence (if already issued); and

(l) proof of ownership of the business.

[reg 4 am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 5 Consideration of the application

(1) The Committee shall consider each application and inform the applicant of its decision within 14 days of the date of the receipt of the application.

(2) In determining an application under this regulation, the Committee shall consider the requirements under Section 10 of the Act and the following matters:

(a) that the applicant demonstrates the need of funding to establish or support the business; and

(b) information required under regulation 4 and in the Form.

(3) Where the Committee is satisfied that the application complies with the requirements of the Act and these Regulations, it shall grant funding to the applicant.

(4) Where the Committee is not satisfied that the application complies with the requirements of the Act and these Regulations, it shall require the applicant to provide any additional details to reconsider its decision before declining the application.

(5) Where the Committee makes a decision to decline funding to a small business enterprise, it shall inform the applicant in writing of its decision within 14 days from the date of the receipt of the application. reg 5LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO
