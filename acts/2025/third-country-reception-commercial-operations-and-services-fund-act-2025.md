Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

## PART 1 — PRELIMINARY

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Third Country Reception Commercial Operations and Services Fund Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Objectives of this Act

The objective of this Act is to establish a fund for a specific purpose under Article 58 of the Constitution:

(a) for the purposes of receiving and expanding commercial operations and services pertaining to the Third Country Reception Arrangement being implemented in the Republic;

(b) as separate and distinct from the general funds deposited or withdrawn from the Treasury Fund under an appropriation law;

(c) providing for the depositing and withdrawal of funds for the purpose of Third Country Reception Arrangement; and

(d) providing for the periodic reporting of the Fund.

#### 4 Interpretation

In this Act:

‘Commercial Operations and Services Fund’ means any monies received by the Republic for the purpose of the commercial operations and services of the Third Country Reception Arrangement but does not include any sum of monies received:

(a) as revenue from a fee, charge, levy or tax imposed by the Republic or an instrumentality of the Republic;

(b) as revenue from the sale of a product or the provision of a service by the Republic or an instrumentality of the Republic; or

(c) for the purpose of general budget support;

‘Minister’ means the Minister responsible for Internal Affairs;

‘Secretary’ means the Secretary responsible for Internal Affairs;

‘the Fund’ means the Commercial Operations and Services Fund established under Section 5; and

‘Third Country Reception Arrangement’ means the arrangement entered into between the Republic and the Government of Australia pursuant to a Memorandum of Understanding between the Republic and the Government of Australia, signed on 22 October 2025 and includes any amendments made to it or supplementary agreements made pursuant to and furtherance of the arrangement. s 4LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

## PART 2 — COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS AND SERVICES FUND

#### 5 Establishment and purpose

(1) The Commercial Operations and Services Fund is established for the purpose of making provisions for:

(a) the payment of contractual or other agreed sum of monies by the Commonwealth of Australia to the Republic for the provision of commercial operations and services by an instrumentality of the Republic; and

(b) the withdrawal of contractual or other amount of monies in subsection

(3) from the Fund for the purposes of the Third Country Reception Arrangement.

(2) All contractual or other amounts for commercial operations and services shall be paid directly into the Fund without being first paid into the Treasury Fund.

(3) The Fund consists of the following:

(a) contractual or other amounts paid into the Fund under subsection (2);

(b) any amount appropriated for the Fund from the Treasury Fund;

(c) any amount received by the Republic by way of interest from the deposit of the Fund in an interest-bearing account with a financial institution in the Republic or a foreign country;

(d) any amount received in reimbursement of an amount withdrawn from the Fund; or

(e) any such funds which is authorised by the Secretary to be deposited into the Fund.

#### 6 Withdrawals from the Fund

(1) A person may only withdraw, authorise, or permit the withdrawal of an amount from the Fund if all of the following conditions are met:

(a) the withdrawal is for the purpose for which the Fund is established;

(b) the purpose of the withdrawal has been authorised by the Minister after approval by the Secretary;

(c) the Secretary and the Secretary for Finance are authorised co-signatories of the Fund; and

(d) any other procedures as may be prescribed for the withdrawal have been complied with.

(2) The Cabinet may approve withdrawal of an amount from the Fund for the sole purpose of paying into the Treasury Fund.

#### 7 Audit of Fund

(1) The Fund is subject to inspection and audit by the Auditor General.

(2) The Fund in a financial year shall be audited:

(a) within 2 months after the end of each financial year; or

(b) another period determined by the Cabinet.

(3) The Auditor shall:

(a) audit the accounts; and

(b) report to the Cabinet on the accounts within:

(i) 3 months after receiving the accounts; or

(ii) another period determined by the Cabinet.

(4) The Minister shall lay the audit report in Parliament as soon as practicable on receipt of such report. s 7LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

## PART 3 — MISCELLANEOUS

#### 8 Regulations

(1) The Cabinet may make regulations prescribing all matters necessary or convenient to be prescribed to give effect to this Act.

(2) Without limiting subsection (1), the Cabinet may make regulations prescribing the following:

(a) the procedures to be followed before and after an amount is withdrawn from the Fund, including the form in which the Cabinet approval of the purpose of the withdrawal is to be evidenced; and

(b) additional reports that shall be given relating to the operation of the Fund.
