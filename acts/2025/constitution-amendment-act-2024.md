# Constitution (Amendment) Act 2024

_____________________________ No.  6 of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Constitution of Nauru 1968.

Certified: 11 March 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Constitution (Amendment) Act 2024.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Constitution of Nauru 1968

The Constitution of Nauru 1968 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Article 19

Article 19 is deleted and substituted as follows:

‘Article 19 Appointment of Ministers Whenever a President is elected, he shall as soon as practicable appoint not more than seven members of Parliament to be Ministers of the Cabinet.’.
