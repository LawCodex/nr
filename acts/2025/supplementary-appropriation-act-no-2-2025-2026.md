# Supplementary Appropriation Act (No 2) 2025-2026

Supplementary Appropriation Act (No 2) 2025–2026

## Table of Amendments

The Supplementary Appropriation Act (No 2) 2025–2026 No 45 was certified and commenced on 23 December 2025.

Amending Legislation Certified Date of Commencement

An Act to provide for the supplementary withdrawal and expenditure of funds from the Treasury Fund to meet the expenditure of the Republic for the financial year ending on 30 June 2026.

Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation Act (No 2) 2025–2026.

#### 2 Commencement and expiry

(1) This Act commences on the day it is certified by the Speaker.

(2) This Act shall cease to have effect on 30 June 2026.

#### 3 Withdrawal of funds from the Treasury Fund

(1) Recognises an additional $5,093,932 in expenditure for the specific purposes identified in the fourth column of Schedule 2 as detailed in the Supplementary Estimates of Expenditure presented to Parliament on 23 December 2025.

(2) To facilitate the additional expenditure referred to in subsection (1) above, an equivalent sum of $5,093,932 shall be reallocated from the Fiscal Cash Buffer (Head 16 — Finance Other), resulting in a net zero impact on the total approved budget for the 2025–2026 financial year.

(3) An amount not exceeding $5,093,932 may be withdrawn from the Treasury Fund and expended as authorised in the fourth column of Schedule 2 and detailed in the Supplementary Estimates of Expenditure presented to Parliament on 23 December 2025.

(4) The amounts in the respective Schedules are in addition to and not in substitution for the amounts previously authorised for withdrawal under the Appropriation Act 2025–2026.

(5) Where the Cabinet considers it necessary to increase the amount assigned in the estimates to a particular subhead within a Head, the Cabinet may direct that there shall be applied to the aid of that subhead a further sum out of any surplus arising on any other subhead within the same Head, but so that the total amount withdrawn and expended does not exceed the amount authorised for that Head in the Schedule to this Act.
