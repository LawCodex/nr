# Nauru Trust Fund Fund No.2 Act 2025

Nauru Trust Fund No 2 Act 2025

## Table of Amendments

The Nauru Trust Fund No 2 Act 2025 No 47 was certified and commenced on 23 December 2025.

Amending Legislation Certified Date of Commencement

An Act to provide for the Nauru Trust Fund No 2 and for other related purposes.

Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

## PART 1 — PRELIMINARY

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Nauru Trust Fund No 2 Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Interpretation

In this Act:

‘Agreement’ means the Memorandum of Understanding Regarding Intergenerational Trust Fund No 2 for the People of the Republic of Nauru;

‘annual budget’ means the budget of the Fund appropriated under Article 59(3) of the Constitution;

‘capital’ of the Fund means money that is invested in the Fund, and includes assets purchased by the Fund;

‘Committee’ means the executive committee, board of directors, or other legally constituted governing body of the Fund mentioned in the Agreement;

‘Fund’ means the Intergenerational Trust Fund No 2 for the People of the Republic of Nauru incorporated under Section 5 and pursuant to Regulation 4 of the International Organisations (Privileges and Immunities — Nauru Trust Fund No 2) Regulations 2025 of the Commonwealth of Australia or any successor legislation or instrument;

‘income’ of the Fund means income generated by the capital of the Fund; and

‘Minister’ means the Minister responsible for this Act; and

‘public moneys’ has the meaning given to it under the Public Finance (Control and Management) Act 1997.

#### 4 Application

The Act shall not affect the operation of the Nauru Trust Fund Act 2012.

## PART 2 — INTERGENERATIONAL TRUST FUND NO 2

#### 5 Incorporation of the Fund

The Fund is a body corporate with perpetual succession.

#### 6 Status of the Fund

The capital and income of the Fund, other than money distributed to the Government by a decision of the Board under the Agreement is not:

(a) public moneys of Nauru; and

(b) subject to Government control and accounting except as provided by the Agreement.

#### 7 Money from the Fund to be paid into the Treasury Fund

Money received by the Government from the Fund shall be paid into the:

(a) Treasury Fund in accordance with Article 58 of the Constitution; or

(b) Fund established under the Third Country Reception Commercial Operations and Services Fund Act 2025.

## PART 3 — MISCELLANEOUS

#### 8 Provision of information

The Minister shall, upon request, provide the Committee or another person authorised by the Committee of the Fund, any of the following:

(a) any information or document which the Auditor General would be entitled to demand from the Government under a written law;

(b) any information or document relating to the annual budget for a specified year;

(c) the annual statements of accounts for a period specified under Section 10 of the Audit Act 1973; or

(d) the annual report of the Auditor General for a period specified under Section 11 of the Audit Act 1973.

#### 9 Annual reporting requirement

(1) The Fund shall prepare the following to report on the Fund’s activities during the preceding financial year:

(a) annual report of the Committee of the Fund;

(b) annual accounts of the Fund; and

(c) Auditor General’s report on the annual accounts of the Fund.

(2) The Fund shall submit the reports under subsection (1) to the Minister within 2 months, after the end of the financial year to which such reports relate.

(3) The Minister shall within 3 sitting days of receiving a report under subsection (2), table such report in Parliament.

#### 10 Fund not liable to taxation

Tax shall not be charged on any contribution to the capital of the Fund or on any income derived from the investments of the Fund.

#### 11 Regulations

The Cabinet may make regulations prescribing all matters necessary or convenient to be prescribed to give effect to this Act.
