# Nauru Superannuation (Amendment) Act 2025

NAURU SUPERANNUATION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2025 _____________________________ No.  49 of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Nauru Superannuation Act 2018 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 23 December 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Nauru Superannuation (Amendment) Act 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on 1 Febuary 2026, after it has been certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Nauru Superannuation Act 2018

The Nauru Superannuation Act 2018 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 4

Section 4 is amended by inserting the following definition after the definition of

‘Board’:

‘business’ has the meaning given under the Business Licences Act 2017;’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 65

Section 65 is deleted and substituted with:

‘65 Regulations

(1) The Cabinet may make regulations prescribing all matters necessary or convenient to be prescribed to give effect to this Act.

(2) Without limiting subsection (1), the Cabinet may make regulations to:

(a)  amend Schedule 2;

(b) prescribing procedures for verifying eligibility in relation to Section 50A(4) and Schedule 2, including documentation and audit requirements.’.

#### 6 Amendment of Schedule 1

Schedule 1 is amended in item (a) by deleting ‘5%’ and substituting with ‘10%’.

#### 7 Amendment of Schedule 2

Schedule 2 is amended by deleting the table and substituting with the following: 1 (a) Ownership: The employer is a corporation in which all shareholders are Nauruans, a partnership in which all partners or beneficial owners are Nauruans or a Nauruan sole trader; and

(b) Business size: The employer operates a business that:

(i) employs no more than 5 employees;

(ii) has annual gross turnover not exceeding $10,000; and

(iii) holds assets not exceeding $5,000 in value.
