# Electoral (Amendment) Act (No. 4) 2025

_____________________________ No.  41 of 2025 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Electoral Act 2016 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 19 September 2025 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Electoral (Amendment) Bill (No. 4) 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Electoral Act 2016

The Electoral Act 2016 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 47

Section 47(1)(b) is amended by deleting ‘21 days before polling day’ and substituting with ‘on the day the roll is closed under Section 50’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 50

(1) Section 50(2) is amended by deleting ‘an election’ and substituting with ‘a by- election’.

(2) Section 50 is amended by inserting a new subsection (2A) after subsection (2) as follows:

‘(2A) In the case of a general election, the Roll shall close at 5:00pm on the day the Parliament is dissolved under Article 41 of the Constitution.’.

#### 6 Amendment of Section 51

Section 51 is amended by inserting a new subsection (2A) after subsection (2) as follows:

‘(2A) The Electoral Commission may as soon as practicable publish the provisional Roll on its website for voters and candidates to have access to such publication for the purposes of the election.’.

#### 7 Amendment of Section 56

Section 56(1) is deleted and substituted with:

‘(1)  Where Parliament has been dissolved or a member has died or vacated his or her seat in accordance with Article 32 of the Constitution, the Speaker shall, subject in the case of a general election to compliance with Article 39, within 14 days of such dissolution, death or vacation cause a writ for the holding of an election in accordance with Form A or Form B of Schedule 8, as the case requires, to be issued to the Electoral Commission.

(1A) The polling day fixed by the Speaker for election shall be within 21 days from the issuance of the writ under subsections (1) and (3).’.

#### 8 Amendment of Section 57

Section 57(2) is amended by deleting ‘during official working hours from 9:00am on the day after the closing of the Roll and not later than 5:00pm 14 days before polling day’ and substituting with ‘a day after the dissolution of the Parliament under Article 41 of the Constitution and not later than 3 days from such dissolution’.

#### 9 Amendment of Section 58

Section 58(1) is amended by deleting ‘72’ and substituting with ‘24’.

#### 10 Amendment of Section 79

Section 79(4) is amended by deleting ‘7:00am’ and substituting with ‘9:00am’.

#### 11 Consequential amendment

A reference to ‘Cabinet’ shall be deleted and substituted with ‘Electoral Commission’ in the following provisions:

(a) Section 78(4);

(b) Section 78(5);

(c) Section 78(8);

(d) Section 78A(1)(c);

(e) Section 78A(4);

(f) Section 78A(7); and

(g) Section 108A(4).
