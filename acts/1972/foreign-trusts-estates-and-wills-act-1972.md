# Foreign Trusts, Estates and Wills Act 1972

Foreign Trusts, Estates and Wills Act 1972

An Act to abolish the rule against perpetuities and the rule against accumulations for some purposes, to provide for the establishment of non-charitable purpose trusts and to make provision in relation to the investment of trust funds and to wills and estates of non-residents.

Enacted by the Parliament of Naoero as follows:

[cl am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 1 Short title and commencement

This Act may be cited as the Foreign Trusts, Estates and Wills Act 1972 and came into effect on 14 April 1972.

#### 2 Interpretation

In this Act “trustee corporation” means a corporation incorporated in the Republic and permitted to carry on business as a trustee corporation pursuant to the provisions of the Corporations Act 1972 or pursuant to any other written law.

#### 3 Abolition of the Rule against Perpetuities

Subject to Section 7, the rule of law known as the Rule Against Perpetuities is hereby abolished.

#### 4 Abolition of restrictions on directions for accumulation of

income Subject to Section 7, the restrictions imposed on the periods for which accumulations of income under a settlement or other disposition of property are permitted are hereby abolished.

#### 5 Abolition of the rule of law against inalienability of trusts of

perpetual duration Subject to Section 7, the rule of law rendering void for remoteness dispositions under which property is limited to be applied for purposes other than the benefit of a person or class of persons in cases where the property may be so applied after the end of the perpetuity period is hereby abolished.

#### 6 Establishment of purpose trusts

(1) Notwithstanding any rule of law to the contrary but subject to Section 7, a person may create in the Republic a trust in perpetuity or for any lesser duration for a purpose or purposes, whether charitable or not, but no such trust may be created except in accordance with subsection (2).

(2) A trust in perpetuity or for any lesser duration for a purpose or purposes other than a charitable purpose or purposes shall be valid only if:

(a) (i) the sale trustee, or at least one of the trustees, of the trust is a trustee corporation which has acknowledged in writing its acceptance of the appointment;

(ii) the trust is created or evidenced by a deed situate in the Republic;

(iii) the deed creating the trust and any deed varying that deed makes provision for the person or persons entitled to enforce the trust;

(iv) where the trust is not a perpetual trust or where it may be terminated, the deed creating or evidencing the trust and any deed varying such deed shall specify the event or the events upon which the trust determines and shall provide for the disposition of surplus assets of the trust upon its termination; and

(v) the deed creating such trust and any deed varying the same shall provide that it is not to take effect until it is delivered to the trustee corporation or, if more than one, to every trustee corporation, which is the trustee of such trust or one of the trustees of such trust; or

(b) (i) the sole trustee or at least one of the trustees of the trust is a trustee corporation which has acknowledged in writing its acceptance of the appointment; and

(ii) the trust is created by will admitted to probate or in respect of which letters of administration have been granted in the Republic:

Provided that, if in respect of any trust created by a will no trustee corporation has been appointed as a trustee or a trustee corporation has been so appointed but has not acknowledged in writing its acceptance of the appointment, the Supreme Court may, upon application by the executor of the will or the administrator to whom letters of administration in respect of the will have been granted, appoint as trustee a trustee corporation which is willing to accept the appointment and such trustee shall be deemed to have been appointed by the testator in his or her will.

(3) Any such trust shall, be enforceable by the person or persons provided in the deed or will creating such trust or the deed varying such trust as the case may be notwithstanding that such person or persons are not beneficiaries under the trust and, in default of there being any such persons to enforce the trust, it may be enforced by a person appointed by the Court upon the application of a person and the Court may order that the costs of the application be paid out of the assets of the trust, if it thinks fit.

(4) Nothing in this Section affects the creation, variation, determination or validity of a Purpose Trust created pursuant to the provisions of any other written law but, save as aforesaid, trusts for purposes other than charitable purposes which do not comply either with subsection (2)(a) or (b) shall be invalid.

(5) In this Section, “a trust for a purpose or purposes” means a trust other than:

(a) a trust for the benefit of particular individual persons whether or not immediately ascertainable; and

(b) a trust for the benefit of some aggregate of individual persons ascertained by reference to some personal relationship.

(6) For the purposes of subsection (5), the expression “individual persons” includes both natural persons and bodies corporate, corporations and incorporated companies.

#### 7 Act not to apply to land in the Republic or to certain trusts

This Act shall not apply to:

(a) any settlement, disposition or trust of any land or of any interest in any land where such land is situate in the Republic; or s 6LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

#### 8 Investments

(1) A trustee unless expressly forbidden by the instrument, if any, creating the trust, may invest any trust funds in his or her hands, whether at the time in a state of investment or not, in any of the securities authorised by this Act.

(2) The securities authorised by this Act are such securities as the trust funds may be invested in pursuant to the instrument creating the trust and such other investments as may be expressed to be trustee investments by regulations made under this Act.

[subs (2) am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[69], opn 15 Apr 2011]

#### 9 Probate and letters of administration of foreign wills and

estates

(1) For the purposes of this Section, “a foreign will” means a will of a person who, at the date of his or her death, was not a citizen of the Republic nor domiciled in the Republic nor permanently resident in the Republic:

Provided that “a foreign will” shall include the will of a citizen of the Republic or a person domiciled or permanently resident in the Republic if such will is expressed to be a foreign will for the purpose of this Section.

(2) For the purposes of this Section “a foreign estate” shall mean the estate in the Republic of a person who was not at the time of his or her death a citizen of the Republic nor domiciled in the Republic nor permanently resident in the Republic.

(3) Probate of a foreign will shall be granted only if the executor or one of the executors appointed thereby is a trustee corporation.

(4) Letters of administration of a foreign estate shall be granted only if the administrator or one of the administrators is a trustee corporation.

(5) This subsection provides for resealing of foreign grant as follows:

[insrt by the Law Revision Commission under powers authorised by Act 10 of 2019]

(a) the probate of a foreign will shall not be re-sealed in the Republic unless the executor or one of the executors appointed by the will is a trustee corporation;

(b) letters of administration of a foreign estate shall not be re-sealed in the Republic unless the administrator or one of the administrators is a trustee corporation;

(c) in any case where the probate of a foreign will cannot be re-sealed in the Republic only because no executor is a trustee corporation, letters of administration cum testamento annexo may be granted in the Republic to a, trustee corporation solely or jointly with the executor or executors named in the will as administrators; and

(d) in any case where letters of administration of a foreign estate granted outside the Republic cannot be re-sealed in the Republic only because no administrator appointed under the grant made outside the Republic is a trustee corporation, letters of administration may be granted in the Republic to a trustee corporation solely or jointly with administrators s 9 FOREIGN TRUSTS, ESTATES AND WILLS ACT 1972 appointed under the grant made outside the Republic. s 9LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

(6) Where by any foreign will the testator appoints as executors persons or corporations none of whom is a trustee corporation, letters of administration cum testamento annexo may, subject to subsection (7) of this Section, be granted if a trustee corporation is either the sole administrator or a joint administrator with one or more of the executors named in such will but not otherwise.

(7) A foreign corporation registered in the Republic or a corporation incorporated in the Republic other than a trustee corporation may, notwithstanding that it is not permitted to carry on business as a trustee, be a co-executor of a foreign will or a co-administrator of a foreign estate with a trustee corporation but save as aforesaid a foreign corporation or a corporation incorporated in the Republic other than a trustee corporation shall not act as executor or administrator of any will or estate in the Republic.

(8) No commission shall be allowed or payable out of, or in respect of the administration of, a foreign estate or an estate under a foreign will except to a trustee corporation, unless the will being so administered otherwise provides and then only in accordance with such provision; in any such case the commission payable to a trustee corporation shall not be reduced by reason of any provision in the will for commission to be payable to an executor or administrator who is not a trustee corporation.

(9) For the purposes of this Section, a natural person shall whether or not he or she was actually in the Republic at the time of his or her death be deemed to have been permanently resident in the Republic at the time of his or her death if:

(a) he or she was, at the time of his or her death or within 6 months of his or her death, employed by the Republic, the Cabinet, the Ronphos Corporation, any instrumentality of the Republic or any other body corporate or unincorporated specified by the Minister by notice in the Gazette on a contract of service the duration of which was to be not less than 12 months or indefinite; and

(b) he or she was required to reside in the Republic for the purpose of his or her employment for the whole or the major part of the 12 months immediately preceding his or her death:

Provided that the provisions of this Section shall be construed so as not to derogate from the natural meaning of the expression “permanently resident” insofar as it may apply to any other person.

#### 10 Tax and duties not payable

No succession, probate or estate taxes or duties shall be payable on account of any will admitted to probate in the Republic or any estate in respect of which letters of administration are granted in the Republic. s 10 FOREIGN TRUSTS, ESTATES AND WILLS ACT 1972
