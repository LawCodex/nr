# Refugee Convention (Amendment) Act 2024

REFUGEE CONVENTION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2024 _____________________________ No.  20 of 2024 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Refugee Convention Act 2012 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 26 November 2024 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Refugee Convention (Amendment) Act 2024.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Refugee Convention Act 2012

The Refugee Convention Act 2012 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 31

Section 31(2)(a) is amended by deleting ‘28’ and substituting with ‘21’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 33

Section 33(1) is amended by deleting ‘90’ and substituting with ‘60’.

#### 6 Amendment of Section 43

Section 43(3) is amended by deleting ‘42’ and substituting with ‘21’.

#### 7 Amendment of Section 46

Section 46(1) is amended by deleting ‘90’ and substituting with ‘60’.

#### 8 Insertion of new Section 56

A new Section 56 is inserted after Section 55 as follows:

‘56  Transitional provision relating to the Refugee Convention (Amendment) Act 2024

(1) The period of time specified in Section 31(2)(a) of the Act prior to the commencement of the Refugee Convention (Amendment) Act 2024, shall continue to apply in respect of determinations made by the Secretary under Section 6 of the Act prior to the commencement of the Refugee Convention (Amendment) Act 2024, notwithstanding that an application for review is filed after the commencement of the Refugee Convention (Amendment) Act 2024.

(2) The period of time specified in Section 43(3) of the Act prior to the commencement of the Refugee Convention (Amendment) Act 2024, shall continue to apply in respect of decisions made by the Tribunal under Section 34 of the Act prior to the commencement of the Refugee Convention (Amendment) Act 2024, notwithstanding that an appeal is filed after the commencement of the Refugee Convention (Amendment) Act 2024.

(3) Where the Supreme Court or the Nauru Court of Appeal directs that a matter be re-considered by the Tribunal prior to the commencement of the Refugee Convention (Amendment) Act 2024, such matter shall be subject to the period under Section 46 prior to the commencement of the Refugee Convention (Amendment) Act 2024, notwithstanding that the direction is made after the commencement of the Refugee Convention (Amendment) Act 2024.’.
