# Customs (Amendment) Act 2024

______________________________ No. 14  of 2024 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Customs Act 2014.

Certified: 20 August 2024 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Customs (Amendment) Act 2024.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Customs Act 2014

This Act amends the Customs Act 2014.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 95

Section 95(1) is amended by deleting ‘the Chief Collector of Customs may amend that assessment, and that amended assessment is the Customs value for the purposes of this Act’ and substituting ‘the assessment shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 2 of the Customs Tariff Act 2014’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 151

Section 151(2) is amended by deleting ‘7 years’ and substituting ‘5 years’.

#### 6 Amendment of Section 184

Section 184(1) is amended by deleting ‘7 years’ and substituting ‘5 years’.

#### 7 Amendment of Section 264

Section 264(2) is amended by deleting ‘7 years’ and substituting ‘5 years’.

#### 8 Amendment of Section 303

Section 303 is amended by:

(a)  deleting ‘under this Act’ in the heading; and

(b)  inserting a new subsection after subsection (2) as follows:

‘(3)  Where a declaration is made to the Chief Collector of Customs or a Customs officer under this Act or any other written law, Customs shall keep or cause to be kept the declaration and all information contained in or related to the declaration for a period of 5 years.’.
