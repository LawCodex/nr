# Naoero Citizenship (Amendment) Act 2024

NAOERO CITIZENSHIP (AMENDMENT) ACT 2024 _____________________________ No.  21 of 2024 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Naoero Citizenship Act 2017 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 26 November 2024 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Naoero Citizenship (Amendment) Act 2024.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Naoero Citizenship Act 2017

The Naoero Citizenship Act 2017 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 24

Section 24 is deleted.
