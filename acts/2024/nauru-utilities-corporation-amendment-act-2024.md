# Nauru Utilities Corporation (Amendment) Act 2024

NAURU UTILITIES CORPORATION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2024 _____________________________ No.  12 of 2024 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Nauru Utilities Corporation Act 2011 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 20 August 2024 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Nauru Utilities Corporation (Amendment) Act 2024.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Nauru Utilities Corporation Act 2011

The Nauru Utilities Corporation Act 2011 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 10

Section 10 is deleted and substituted with the following:

‘10     Functions of Corporation – Sanitation The functions of the Corporation in relation to sanitation are to:

(a) de-sludge, transport, treat, store and dispose sewage material and waste water; and

(b) do anything the Corporation determines is required or incidental to the performance of the function under paragraph (a).’.
