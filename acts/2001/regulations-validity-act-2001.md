# Regulations Validity Act 2001

## Table of Amendments

The Regulations Validity Act 2001 No 7 was certified and commenced on 19 December 2001 (GN No 291/2001; Gaz 78/2001).

Amending Legislation Certified Date of Commencement Revised Written Laws Act 2021 No 7 1 June 2021 1 June 2021 Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026

An Act to make provision for the retrospective validation of the effectiveness of certain Regulations.

Enacted by the Parliament of Naoero as follows:

[cl am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 1 Short title and commencement

This Act may be cited as the Regulations Validity Act 2001 and came into effect 19 December 2001.

#### 2 Interpretation

In this Act:

‘effective date’, in relation to a Regulation or Rule, means the date of publication in the Gazette given in Column 3 of the Schedule opposite the name of the Regulation or Rule; and

‘Regulations’ means the Regulations and Rules in the Schedule.

#### 3 Validation of Regulations

Notwithstanding that:

(a) the Regulations were not laid before Parliament within 6 sitting days next following publication in the Gazette, as required by Section 29(1)( c) of the Interpretation Act 1971; and

(b) by Section 29(2) of the Interpretation Act 1971, the Regulations thereupon ceased to have effect, it is declared that:

(i) the Regulations are deemed to have been laid before Parliament in accordance with Section 29(1)(c) of the Interpretation Act 1971 and not to have been disallowed as permitted by Section 29(2) of the Interpretation Act 1971; and

(ii) the validity or invalidity or anything done, or the omission of anything, on and from the effective dates, is not affected by the failure referred to in paragraph (a) to lay the Regulations before Parliament.
