#### 1 Short title and commencement

This Act may be cited as the Naoero Phosphate Royalties (Payment and Investment) Act 1968 and came into effect on 29 January 1968.

[s 1 subst Act 8 of 2008, opn 1 Aug 2008; am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 2 Definitions

In this Act:

‘fund’ means a fund established by this Act;

‘the Agreement’ means the Agreement, a copy of which is set out in the Schedule to the Naoero Phosphate Agreement Ordinance 1968;

[def am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

‘the Cabinet’ means the Cabinet;

‘the Corporation’ means the Naoero Phosphate Corporation, renamed RONPHOS under the RONPHOS Act 2005; and

[def am Act 8 of 2005 s 3, opn 30 Sep 2005; Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

‘the net proceeds’ means the net proceeds of phosphate operations at the Republic being the proceeds referred to in clause 16 of the Agreement.

[s 2 subst Act 6 of 1968 s 3, opn 1 July 1968]

#### 10 Investment of monies in a fund

(1) Monies standing to the credit of a fund and not immediately required for the purpose of the fund may be invested:

(a) in securities of or guaranteed by the Commonwealth of Australia or a State of that Commonwealth;

(b) by fixed deposit in a bank; or

(c) any such other manner as the Cabinet approves.

(2) Income earned from the investment of monies standing to the credit of a fund forms part of that fund.

(3) This Section has effect only in respect of the Naoero Rehabilitation Fund.

[subs (3) insrt Act 4 of 1997, opn 30 Sep 1997; Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 12 Periodic review of payments

(1) In this Section, s 11LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

‘year of review’ means the year 1970 and every third year thereafter until 2012 and 2014 and every year after 2014.

[subs (1) am Act 23 of 2014 s 9.2, opn 19 Dec 2013]

(2) During each year of review the Cabinet may alter the amount payable under any Section(s) of this Act or pursuant to any Schedule(s) to this Act to any fund.

[subs (3) am Act 4 of 1997, opn 30 Sep 1997; Act 23 of 2014 s 9.2, opn 19 Dec 2013]

(3) Where the Cabinet alters the amounts as provided in subsection (2), it shall by notice in the Gazette and in accordance with that agreement or determination of the Cabinet, fix the amount to be paid into that fund in respect of each tonne of phosphate shipped from the Republic on or after the first day of July in that year, and the Corporation shall pay the amount so fixed into that fund until the amount payable is again altered in accordance with this Section.

#### 13 Saving

Until the Corporation is established, the British Phosphate Commissioners shall make the payments provided for in Section 11 into the funds specified in Schedule 2 before the payment of net proceeds.

[s 13 subst Act 6 of 1968 s 10, opn 1 July 1968]

#### 14 Payments of royalties to land owners

(1) The owners of phosphate bearing land shall be paid from the Naoero Land Owners Cash Royalties Fund an amount calculated by multiplying the number of tons of phosphate mined from their land and shipped from the Republic by the rate per ton at which contributions were made to the fund at the time the phosphate was shipped.

[subs (1) am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

(2) The Cabinet shall make the payments to land owners provided for by this Section as soon as practicable after the phosphate extracted from that owner’s phosphate bearing land is shipped.

[subs (2) am Act 4 of 1997, opn 30 Sep 1997]

(3) The Cabinet may authorise the Corporation or, until the Corporation comes into existence the British Phosphate Commissioners, to pay the amounts calculated in accordance with subsection (2) to the owners of phosphate bearing land and the amounts so paid shall be deemed to have been paid into the fund and then paid by the Cabinet to those land owners pursuant to this Section.

[subs (3) am Act 4 of 1997, opn 30 Sep 1997]
