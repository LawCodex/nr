# Land (Declaration of Ownership) Act 1962

Land (Declaration of Ownership) Act 1962

#### 1 Short title and commencement

This Act may be cited as the Land (Declaration of Ownership) Act 1962 and came into effect on 14 May 1962.

#### 2 Definitions

In this Act:

‘encumbrances’ means interests, trusts, restrictions, dedications, reservations, obligations, contracts, licences and charges; and

‘the former German wireless station land’ means the land described in the Schedule, and includes any estate in that land.

#### 3 Ownership of former German wireless station land

It is hereby declared that:

(a) for the purposes of Section 6 of the Laws Repeal and Adopting Act 1922, the Government formerly exercising authority over the Republic was, immediately before it ceased to exercise that authority, the absolute owner, freed and discharged of all encumbrances, of the former German wireless station land;

(b) that Section applies, and has at all times since the commencement of that Act applied, to that land; and

(c) notwithstanding any claims to the contrary, no person is entitled to any estate in that land other than an estate granted by the Cabinet since the commencement of that Act.

#### 4 Saving of power of Administration to distribute moneys

Nothing in this Act prevents the Cabinet from making available for distribution in accordance with the notice published in the Gazette on the 6th day of May, 1961, amounts equal to the amounts of money that were received by the Cabinet before the date of commencement of this Act or that are received by the Cabinet on or after that date, in respect of the working of the former German wireless station land for phosphate.
