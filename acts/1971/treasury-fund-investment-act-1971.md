An Act to provide for the investment of surplus monies in the Treasury Fund.

Enacted by the Parliament of Naoero as follows:

[cl am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 1 Short title and commencement

This Act may be cited as the Treasury Fund Investment Act 1971 and came into effect on 20 December 1971.

#### 2 Interpretation

In this Act:

‘Treasury Fund’ means the Treasury Fund established under Article 58 of the Constitution and consists of all:

(a) revenues and other monies raised or received by the Republic not being revenues or other monies payable by law into another fund established for a specific purpose; and

(b) investments made in accordance with the provisions of this Act; and

‘withdrawal’ and ‘withdrawn’ means where the context so admits to take away or remove any part of the Treasury Fund for any purpose other than the transfer of revenues or monies in the Treasury Fund to any investment authorised by this Act or vice versa.

#### 3 Investment of surplus monies

(1) Monies and revenues in the Treasury Fund, which from time to time, are not required for or are surplus to estimated current expenditure as authorised by Parliament may be invested:

(a) in the securities of, or guaranteed by, the Commonwealth of Australia or any state of that Commonwealth;

(b) in such other securities or in securities included in such other class of securities as Cabinet may approve;

(c) on fixed deposit in a bank approved by Cabinet; or

(d) in such other manner as Cabinet approves.

(2) Any investment made under the provisions of this Act, may from time to time, be:

(a) redeemed or disposed of and the proceeds reinvested; or

(b) withdrawn in accordance with any other written law.

#### 4 Control of investment

Monies and revenues invested under this Act shall:

(a) be invested in the name of the Republic and shall be and remain part of the Treasury Fund unless and until withdrawn therefrom in accordance with any other written law; and

(b) subject to any general or particular direction of the Cabinet, be invested in and redeemed from such investments and such investments shall be disposed of by the authority and under the hand of such persons, as may from time to time, be authorised to operate on Treasury Fund bank accounts.

#### 5 Saving

Nothing in this Act shall make any person liable for any loss to the Treasury Fund arising out of the revaluation of any investment lawfully made under this Act or arising out of the redemption or disposition of any such investment, provided that such redemption or disposition is done reasonably for the avoidance of further loss to the Fund or for the purpose of withdrawal from the Fund in accordance with any other written law. s 4LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO
