# Customs (Amendment) Act 2022

______________________________ No. 12 of 2022 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Customs Act 2014.

Certified: [8th June 2022]

Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Customs (Amendment) Act 2022.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on 1 July 2022.

#### 3 Amendment of Customs Act 2014

The Customs Act 2014 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 2

Section 2 is amended in the definition of ‘Customs value’ or ‘value’ by inserting ‘of the Customs Tariff Act 2014’ after ‘Schedule 2’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 94

Section 94(1) is amended by inserting ‘of the Customs Tariff Act 2014’ after ‘Schedule 2’.

#### 6 Amendment of Section 95

Section 95 is amended:

(a) in subsection (1)(a), by inserting ‘of the Customs Tariff Act 2014’ after

‘Schedule 2’; and

(b) in subsection (2)(b) by deleting it and substituting as follows:

‘(b) the basis for the amended assessment and where applicable make references to the relevant provisions of this Act, Schedule 2 of the Customs Tariff Act 2014 or any other relevant provisions of that Act.’.

#### 7 Amendment of Section 272

Section 272(1)(b) is amended by inserting ‘of the Customs Tariff Act 2014’ after ‘Schedule 2’.

#### 8 General amendment of reference to Schedule 1

All references to ‘Schedule 1’ are deleted and substituted with ‘the Schedule’ or ‘Schedule’ where the context requires.

#### 9 Repeal Schedule 2

Schedule 2 is repealed.
