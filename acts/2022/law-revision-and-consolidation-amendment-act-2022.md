# Law Revision and Consolidation (Amendment) Act 2022

LAW REVISION AND CONSOLIDATION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2022 _____________________________ No. 23 of 2022 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Law Revision and Consolidation Act 2019.

Certified: [12th December 2022]

Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act shall be cited as the Law Revision and Consolidation (Amendment) Act 2022.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification of the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of Law Revision and Consolidation Act 2019

The Law Revision and Consolidation Act 2019 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 6

Section 6(3) is amended by deleting paragraph (d) and substituting with:

‘(d) Director Legislative Drafting or any other person who for the time being is the Head of the drafting section’.
