# Bail (Amendment) Act (No.2) 2022

BAIL (AMENDMENT) ACT (NO. 2) 2022 ______________________________ No. 20 of 2022 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Bail Act 2018.

Certified: 8th October 2022 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Bail (Amendment) Act (No. 2) 2022.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Bail Act 2018

The Bail Act 2018 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 4A

Section 4A is amended in paragraph (a) by:

(a) deleting ‘or’ in subparagraph (ii);

(b) inserting ‘or’ after the semi-colon in subparagraph (iii); and

(c) inserting the following subparagraph after subparagraph (iii):

‘(iv) under the Illicit Drugs Control Act 2004;’.
