# Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act 2022

PARLIAMENTARY SALARIES AND ALLOWANCES (AMENDMENT) ACT 2022 _____________________________ No.  19  of 2022 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008 and for other related purposes.

Certified: 26 August 2022 Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances (Amendment) Act 2022.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the date it is certified by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008

The Parliamentary Salaries and Allowances Act 2008 is amended by the provisions of this Act.
