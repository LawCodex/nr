# Electoral (Amendment) Act 2022

_____________________________ No.  8 of 2022 ______________________________ An Act to amend the Electoral Act 2016.

Certified: [8th June 2022]

Table of Provisions Enacted by the Parliament of Nauru as follows:

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Electoral (Amendment) Act 2022.

#### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on certification by the Speaker.

#### 3 Amendment of the Electoral Act 2016

The Electoral Act 2016 is amended by the provisions of this Act.

#### 4 Amendment of Section 3

Section 3 is amended in the definition of ‘by-election’, by:

(a) deleting the word ‘one’ and substituting with ‘a’; and

(b) inserting ‘in a seat of Parliament due to a vacancy contemplated under Article 32(1)(b), (c), (d) and (e) of the Constitution’ after the word ‘vacancy’.

#### 5 Amendment of Section 6

Section 6(1)(d) is amended by deleting ‘making regulations’ and substituting with

‘issuance of a code of conduct’.

#### 6 Amendment of Section 41

Section 41(3) is amended by:

(a) deleting the word ‘and’ at the end of paragraph (a);

(b) deleting the fullstop at the end of paragraph (b) and substituting with ‘; and’; and

(c) inserting a new paragraph (c) as follows:

‘(c) subject to the limitations imposed by Section 47.’.

#### 7 Amendment of Section 42

Section 42 is amended:

(a) in subsection (1)(e)(iv) by inserting the words ‘in that District or registered for the purposes of this Act’ before the words ‘in that’; and

(b) in subsection (3) by:

(i) deleting the words ‘a reference to’; and

(ii) deleting the words ‘refers to’ and substituting with ‘includes’.

#### 8 Amendment of Section 44

Section 44 is amended by deleting the word ‘Section’ and substituting with

‘Sections 41 and’.

#### 9 Amendment of Section 57

Section 57(3)(d) is amended by inserting the words ‘a signed code of conduct and’ before the words ‘a fee’.

#### 10 Amendment of Section 59

Section 59 is amended by:

(a) deleting the word ‘other’ in subsection (1);

(b) inserting into subsection (2), the words ‘, Deputy Minister and Member of Parliament’ after the word ‘Minister’ at the end of that subsection; and

(c) inserting a new subsection (2A) immediately after subsection (2) as follows:

‘(2A)  A holder of an office of profit in the service of Nauru as prescribed under subsection (1), shall resign from his or her office 60 days before the dissolution of the full term of Parliament under Article 41(7) of the Constitution.

#### 11 Amendment of Section 66

Section 66(1) is amended by deleting ‘9.00am’ and substituting ‘7:00am’.

#### 12 Amendment of Section 78A

Section 78A(2) is deleted and substituted as follows:

‘(2) Early voting may take place:

(a) outside the constituency, in a secure location designated by the Electoral Commission; or

(b) in a mobile polling station designated by the Electoral Commission under Section 63.’

#### 13 Amendment of Section 79

Section 79(4) is deleted and substituted as follows:

‘(4) A voter who seeks to vote in a mobile polling station:

(a) on polling day, shall, apply in writing to the Electoral Commission at least 48 hours before 7:00am on polling day, and state the reasons for the application and provide any information including documents that the Electoral Commission may require; or

(b) before polling day as provided for under Section 78A shall, apply in the prescribed manner.’.

#### 14 Amendment of Section 136

(1) Section 136 is amended by:

(a) deleting the words ‘Electoral Commission’ and substituting with ‘Cabinet’; and

(b) deleting paragraph (i) and substituting as follows:

‘(i) for the amendment of the Schedules; or

(j) any matter necessary or convenient for giving effect to this Act.’.

#### 15 General amendments

All references in the Act vesting the power to make Regulations in the Electoral Commission are deleted and substituted by vesting such power in the Cabinet.

#### 16 Consequential Amendments

As a consequence of the amendment to Section 42(1)(e)(iv), Schedule 2 is amended in Forms 1, 2 and 3 and Schedule 3 is amended to reflect the new criterion that a person may be registered or transferred if he or she has a substantial connection through his or her spouse being born or ‘registered’ in that District.
