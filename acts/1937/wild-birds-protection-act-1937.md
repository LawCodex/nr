# Wild Birds Protection Act 1937

## Table of Amendments

The Wild Birds Protection Act 1937 No 14 commenced on 30 November 1937 (s 2).

Amending Legislation Certified Date of Commencement Ordinances Revision Ordinance 1967 No 25 29 December 1967 Statute Law Revision Act 2011 No 8 15 April 2011 15 April 2011 Revised Written Laws Act 2021 No 7 1 June 2021 1 June 2021

An Act to provide for the preservation of wild birds.

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Wild Birds Protection Act 1937.

[s 1 am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[156], opn 15 Apr 2011]

#### 2 Commencement

This Act shall have force and effect as on and from 30 November 1937.

[s 2 am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[158], opn 15 Apr 2011]

#### 3 Repeal

The Wild Birds Preservation Ordinance (No 12 of 1924) and the Wild Birds Preservation Ordinance 1936 are hereby repealed.

#### 4 Interpretation

In this Act:

‘close season’ means in respect to any bird specified in Schedule 1 the period set opposite the name of the bird in that Schedule.

[s 4 am Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[157], opn 15 Apr 2011]

#### 5 Restriction on taking certain species of bird at any time

A person who without permission of the Minister takes any bird specified in Schedule 2, shall be liable on conviction to a penalty not exceeding $10.

[s 5 am Ord 25 of 1967 s 4 and Sch 2, opn 29 Dec 1967; Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[158], opn

#### 15 Apr 2011]

6 Restriction on taking certain species of bird during close season A person who:

(a) knowingly and wilfully destroys, takes, causes to be destroyed, or taken;

(b) uses any lime, trap, snare, net or other instrument for the purpose of destroying or taking; or

(c) has in his or her possession, any bird specified in Schedule 1 during the period of close season set opposite the name or the bird, shall be liable on the first conviction to a penalty not exceeding $2 and on a subsequent conviction to a penalty not exceeding $10 or imprisonment not exceeding 14 days or to both.

[s 6 am Ord 25 of 1967 s 4 and Sch 2, opn 29 Dec 1967; Act 8 of 2011 s 12 and Sch 1[157], opn 15 Apr 2011] 7 Restriction on taking, etc, of eggs A person who takes or destroys, or has in his or her possession, any eggs of the birds specified in Schedule 1 during the period of close season set opposite the name of the birds shall be liable on the first conviction to a penalty not exceeding $2 and on a subsequent conviction to a penalty not exceeding $10 or imprisonment not exceeding 14 days or to both.
