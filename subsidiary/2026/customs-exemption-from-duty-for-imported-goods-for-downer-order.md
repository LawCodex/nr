# Customs (Exemption from Duty for imported goods for Downer) Order

Customs (Exemption from Duty for imported goods by Downer EDI Engineering Power Pty Ltd and Downer New Zealand Ltd and its Sub-contractors, including fuel purchase from Vital Energy for the Naoero Airport Upgrade and Road Rehabilitation Project) Order

## Table of Amendments

The Customs (Exemption from Duty for imported goods by Downer EDI Engineering Power Pty Ltd and Downer New Zealand Ltd and its Sub-contractors, including fuel purchase from Vital Energy for the Naoero Airport Upgrade and Road Rehabilitation Project) Order SL 30 was notified and commenced on 24 August 2026 (GN No 847/2026;

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement

[The next page is 449,702.801]

Customs (Exemption from Duty for imported goods by Downer EDI Engineering Power Pty Ltd and Downer New Zealand Ltd and its Sub-contractors, including fuel purchase from Vital Energy for the Naoero Airport Upgrade and Road Rehabilitation Project) Order (Section 103(1)) PURSUANT to the powers vested in me under Section 103(1) of the Customs Act 2014, I Honourable Charmaine Eraidinomo Scotty, M.P., Acting President and Acting Minister for Finance, on the recommendation of the Chief Collector of Customs, hereby issue this ORDER to grant exemptions from duty in respect of goods or classes of goods imported into the Republic, including fuel purchased from Vital Energy by the Downer EDI Engineering Power Pty Ltd and Downer New Zealand Ltd and the Sub-contractors of these two entities for the purposes of the Naoero Airport Upgrade and Road Rehabilitation Project.

Dated this 24th day of August 2026.

Her Excellency, Hon. Charmaine Eraidinomo Scotty, MP Acting President and Acting Minister for Finance
