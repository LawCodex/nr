# National Disaster Risk Management (Declaration of State of Disaster for the management and minimisation of the impacts of the UXO removal)

National Disaster Risk Management (Declaration of State of Disaster for the management and minimisation of the impacts of the UXO removal)

## Table of Amendments

The National Disaster Risk Management (Declaration of State of Disaster for the management and minimisation of the impacts of the UXO removal) were notified on 13 February 2026 (GN No 156/2026; Gaz 50/2026).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement National Disaster Risk Management (Declaration Of State of Disaster for the management and minimisation of the impacts of the UXO removal) (Section 26) PURSUANT to the powers vested in me under Section 26 of the National Disaster Risk Management Act 2016 and on the advice of the National Disaster Risk Management Council, I, Lionel Rouwen Aingimea, MP, hereby declare a State of Disaster for a part of the Republic of Nauru, specifically:

(a) the following distance from an unexploded ordnance (‘UXO’) that is discovered within the Nauru Rehabilitation Corporation dumpsite located at Monon, Yaren District:

(i) the 150-meter radius from the location of any UXO; or

(ii) in the event of a justified circumstance requiring it, a radius greater, than 150-metres and less than 500 metres from the location of any UXO; and

(b) along the UXO transport route to Topside.

This declaration takes effect from 11th February 2026 for a period of 30 days unless varied, revoked or expires.

DATED this 13th day of February 2026.

His Excellency, Hon. Lionel Rouwen Aingimea, M.P.
