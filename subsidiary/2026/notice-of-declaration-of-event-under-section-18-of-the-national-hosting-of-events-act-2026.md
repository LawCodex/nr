Amending Legislation Notification Date of Commencement Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026 Notice of Declaration of Event under Section 18 of the National Hosting of Events Act 2026

PURSUANT to the powers vested in me under Section 18 of the National Hosting of Events Act 2026, I declare the XI Micronesian Games to be held in the Republic of Naoero in 2028 as an event for the purposes of the National Hosting of Events Act 2026.

Dated this 25th day of May 2026.
