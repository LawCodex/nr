# Consumer Protection Price Control Order No 1 2026

Price Control Order No 1 2026

## Table of Amendments

The Price Control Order No 1 2026 SL 12 was notified and commenced on 3 March 2026 (GN No 236/2026; Gaz 79/2026).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Price Control Order No 1 2026 I, HE Hon David W R Adeang MP, Minister for Finance, acting pursuant to Section 36 of the Consumer Protection Act 2024, repeal all previous price orders for the same items in paragraphs (i) to (vi) below and determine that this Price Control Order replaces all repealed Orders and has an immediate effect within 7 days after its publication in the Government Gazette:

(a) Maximum wholesale price at which diesel may be sold as $1.8418/litre;

(b) Maximum wholesale price at which petrol may be sold as $1.7981/litre;

(c) Maximum wholesale price at which JetA1 may be sold as $1.2275/litre;

(d) Maximum retail price at which diesel may be sold as $2.1418/litre;

(e) Maximum retail price at which petrol may be sold as $2.0981/litre; and

(f) Maximum retail price at which Jet A1 may be sold as $1.5275/litre.

Dated this 27th day of February 2026.

H.E. HON. DAVID W. R. ADEANG, MP MINISTER FOR FINANCE
