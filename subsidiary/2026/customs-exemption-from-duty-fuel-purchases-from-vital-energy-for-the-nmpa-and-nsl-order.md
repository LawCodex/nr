Customs (Exemption from Duty — Fuel Purchases from Vital Energy for the Naoero Marine Port Authority and Naoero Shipping Line) Order

[instr am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

PURSUANT to the powers vested in me under Section 103, I, Hon David WR Adeang, MP, President, and Minister for Finance, on the recommendation of the Chief Collector of Customs, hereby issue this ORDER to grant exemptions from duty in respect of goods or classes of goods imported into the Republic for the purposes of the fuel purchases from Vital Energy for the Naoero Marine Port Authority (NMPA) and Naoero Shipping Line (NSL) for the duration of the construction period.

Dated this 4th day of April 2026.

His Excellency, Hon. David W.R. Adeang, MP President and Minister for Finance
