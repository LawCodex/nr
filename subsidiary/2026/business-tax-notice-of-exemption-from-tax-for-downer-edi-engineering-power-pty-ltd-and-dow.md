# Business Tax Notice of Exemption from Tax for Downer EDI Engineering Power Pty Ltd and Downer

Notice of Exemption from Tax for Downer EDI Engineering Power Pty Ltd and Downer New Zealand Ltd and its Sub-Contractors for the Purposes of the Naoero Airport Upgrade and Road Rehabilitation Project under the Business Tax Act 2016

## Table of Amendments

Notice of Exemption from Tax for Downer EDI Engineering Power Pty Ltd and Downer New Zealand Ltd and its Sub-Contractors for the Purposes of the Naoero Airport Upgrade and Road Rehabilitation Project under the Business Tax Act 2016 SL 32 was notified and commenced on 24 August 2026 (GN No 849/2026; Gaz 294/2026).

Amending Legislation Notification Date of Commencement Notice of Exemption from Tax for Downer EDI Engineering Power Pty Ltd and Downer New Zealand Ltd and its Sub-Contractors for the Purposes of the Naoero Airport Upgrade and Road Rehabilitation Project under the Business Tax Act 2016 (Section 18(1)(a)) Notice is hereby given that in accordance with Section 18(1)(a) of the Business Tax Act 2016 , the income of Downer EDI Engineering Power Pty Ltd and Downer New Zealand Ltd and its Sub-contractors has been exempted and such exemption has received the concurrence of the Cabinet as confirmed in the Cabinet resolution dated Tuesday, 21 st January, 2025.

Dated this 24 th day of August, 2026 DAMON ADEANG ACTING SECRETARY FOR FINANCE
