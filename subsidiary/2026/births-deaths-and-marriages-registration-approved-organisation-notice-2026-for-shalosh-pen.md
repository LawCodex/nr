# Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration (Approved Organisation) Notice 2026 for Shalosh Pentecostal Church SL 17

Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration (Approved Organisation) Notice 2026

## Table of Amendments

The Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration (Approved Organisation) Notice 2026 SL 17 was notified and commenced on 30 April 2026.

Amending Legislation Notification Date of Commencement Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration (Approved Organisation) Notice 2026 I, Rosie Raidi, Acting Registrar of Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration, acting pursuant to Section 77A(4) of the Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration Act 2017 (‘Act’) and upon Cabinet’s approval as per Cabinet Resolution dated 26th March 2026 declare that the Shalosh Pentecostal Church is an Approved Organisation under the Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration Act 2017.

This Notice commences on the date of its publication in the Gazette.

Dated this 30th day of April 2026.

ROSIE RAIDI ACTING REGISTRAR OF BIRTHS, DEATHS AND MARRIAGES
