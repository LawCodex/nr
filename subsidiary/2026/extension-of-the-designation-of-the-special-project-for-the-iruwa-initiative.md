# Extension of the Designation of the Special Project for the Iruwa Initiative

Extension of the Designation of the Special Project for the Iruwa Initiative

## Table of Amendments

Extension of the Designation of the Special Project for the Iruwa Initiative SL 28 was notified and commenced on 5 June 2026 (GN No 570/2026; Gaz 197/2026).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 12 May 2026 Extension of the Designation of the Special Project for the Iruwa Initiative

[instr am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

PURSUANT to the powers vested in Cabinet under Section 9(7) of the Naoero Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Act 2024 (‘Act’) and as per Cabinet Resolution 539/2026 dated 3rd June 2026, Cabinet hereby extends the duration of the Special Project for the ‘The Iruwa Initiative’ to 31 December 2026.

DATED this 3rd day of June 2026.

OPHELIA CALEB SECRETARY TO CABINET
