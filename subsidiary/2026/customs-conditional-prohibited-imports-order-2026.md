Customs (Conditional Prohibited Imports) Order 2026 In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by Section 91(3) and in accordance with Section 91(5)(a) of the Customs Act 2014, as the Minister responsible for Customs, I make the following Order:

THAT the following goods are prohibited imports and as such, shall not be imported into the Republic, except where a licence for importation of the specified goods are obtained from the Eigigu Holdings Corporation:

(a) Mineral Composite Fibre-Reinforced (MCFR) bricks;

(b) Fibre-Reinforced Polymer (FRP) Rebar tendons including such made from glass fibre, carbon fibre or basalt fibre; or

(c) related construction material including composite wall panels, composite floor or roof panels, composite reinforcing mesh, bent composite bars or cages or bonding agents used to assemble MCFR systems.

Dated this 16th day of February 2026. (His Excellency, Hon. David W.R. Adeang, MP) PRESIDENT AND MINISTER FOR FINANCE AND SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT
