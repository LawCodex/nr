# Notice of Exemption from Tax for Downer under the Employment and Services Tax Act 2014

Notice of Exemption from Tax for Downer EDI Engineering Power Pty Ltd and Downer New Zealand Ltd and its Sub-Contractors for the Purposes of the Naoero Airport Upgrade and Road Rehabilitation Project under the Employment and Services Tax Act 2014

## Table of Amendments

The Notice of Exemption from Tax for Downer EDI Engineering Power Pty Ltd and Downer New Zealand Ltd and its Sub-Contractors for the Purposes of the Naoero Airport Upgrade and Road Rehabilitation Project under the Employment and Services Tax Act 2014 SL 31 was notified and commenced on 24 August 2026 (GN No 848/2026;

Gaz 293/2026).

Amending Legislation Notification Date of Commencement Notice of Exemption from Tax for Downer EDI Engineering Power Pty Ltd and Downer New Zealand Ltd and its Sub-Contractors for the Purposes of the Naoero Airport Upgrade and Road Rehabilitation Project under the Employment and Services Tax Act 2014 (Section 13(1)(a)) Notice is hereby given that, in accordance with Section 13(1)(a) of the Employment and Services Tax Act 2014, the income of the Downer EDI Engineering Power Pty Ltd and Downer New Zealand Ltd and its Sub-contractors has been exempted, and such exemption has received the concurrence of the Cabinet, as confirmed in the Cabinet resolution dated Tuesday, 21st January, 2025.

Dated this 24th day of August, 2026 DAMON ADEANG ACTING SECRETARY FOR FINANCE
