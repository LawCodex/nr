# Tobacco Control Notice of National Prohibition of Import Distribution and Sale of Tobacco Products

Notice of National Prohibition of Import, Distribution and Sale of Tobacco Products, Cigars, E-Cigarette and Adulterated Smokeless Tobacco Product on 31st May

## Table of Amendments

The Notice of National Prohibition of Import, Distribution and Sale of Tobacco Products, Cigars, E-Cigarette and Adulterated Smokeless Tobacco Product on 31st May SL 26 was notified and commenced on 30 May 2026 (GN No 559/2026; Gaz 192/2026).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Notice of National Prohibition of Import, Distribution and Sale of Tobacco Products, Cigars, E-Cigarette and Adulterated Smokeless Tobacco Product on 31st May PURSUANT to the powers vested in Cabinet under Section 18A of the Tobacco Control Act 2009 (‘Act’) and as per Cabinet Resolution 514/2026 dated 29th May, 2026, this notice is to notify the public that the Cabinet has made a determination on the following:

(a) that on the 31st May 2026 from 12am to 11:59pm it is prohibited to import, distribute or sell any type of tobacco products, cigars, e-cigarettes and adulterated smokeless tobacco products in Naoero; and

(b) for the 31st May of each year to be officially recognised, legally regulated and observed annually within the Republic of Naoero as “Wõra ibûmin eatow Tobacco” (“Our Day to Let Go of Tobacco”) in alignment with World No Tobacco Day where the import, distribution or sale of all types of tobacco products, cigars, e-cigarettes and adulterated smokeless tobacco products is prohibited.

DATED this 29th day of May 2026.

OPHELIA CALEB SECRETARY TO CABINET
