# Sanitary Inspectors Notice of Appointment of Sanitary Inspectors under the Sanitary Inspectors’ Act 1921

Notice of Appointment of Sanitary Inspectors under the Sanitary Inspectors’ Act 1921

## Table of Amendments

The Notice of Appointment of Sanitary Inspectors under the Sanitary Inspectors’ Act 1921 SL 10 was notified and commenced on 13 March 2026 (GN No 261/2026;

Gaz 93/2026).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Notice of Appointment of Sanitary Inspectors under the Sanitary Inspectors’ Act 1921 PURSUANT to the power vested in me under Section 2 of the Sanitary Inspectors’ Act 1921 (‘Act’), I hereby appoint the following persons as Sanitary Inspectors to enter and inspect any house, premises or land for the purposes of the Act: 1. Iivan Aingimea 2. Felila Peter 3. Yukio Peter 4. Rirry Ribena Andrews 5. Goska Diema 6. Wyatt Bonang Benjamin 7. Dartagneu Bop 8. Hyden Duburiya 9. Erik Duburiya 10. John-Dale Akibwib 11. Opah Thoma Powers of Sanitary Inspector(s):

The Sanitary Inspectors shall exercise such powers provided under the Act in the performance of his or her functions.

DATED this 13th day of March 2026 His Excellency, Hon. David W.R. Adeang, M.P.
