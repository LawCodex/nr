# Food Safety Revocation of Declaration of Prohibited Product — Poultry and Poultry Products from certain countries Order 2026

Revocation of Declaration of Prohibited Product — Poultry and Poultry Products from certain countries Order 2026

## Table of Amendments

The Revocation of Declaration of Prohibited Product — Poultry and Poultry Products from certain countries Order 2026 SL 1 was notified and commenced on 29 January 2026 (GN No 96/2026; Gaz 30/2026).

Amending Legislation Notification Date of Commencement Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026 Revocation of Declaration of Prohibited Product — Poultry and Poultry Products from certain countries Order 2026

[instr am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

I Hon David WR Adeang, MP am satisfied that there is no justification for the continuation of the Order for Declaration of Prohibited Product — Poultry and Poultry Products from certain countries, dated 21 December 2006. There is no further risk posed to the health and welfare of Naoero citizens in relation to the Avian Influenza Virus (type H5).

In that regard, pursuant to the power vested in me under Section 18(3) of the Food Safety Act 2005 and Section 89 of the Interpretation Act 2011, I hereby revoke the Declaration of Prohibited Product — Poultry and Poultry Products from certain countries, dated 21 December 2006.

Dated this 28th day of January 2026.

His Excellency, Hon. David, W.R. Adeang, M.P.

President and Minister for Health and Medical Services
