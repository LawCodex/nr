# Gaming (Bingo) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

REPUBLIC OF NAOERO GAMING (BINGO) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2026 _____________________________ No. 29 of 2026 ______________________________ Certified: 10 July 2026 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 31 of the Gaming Act 2011:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming (Bingo) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Gaming (Bingo) Regulations 2023

The Gaming (Bingo) Regulations 2023 are amended by these Regulations.

#### 4 Amendment of Regulation 3

Regulation 3 is amended as follows:

(a) in the definition of ‘bingo market operator’ delete wherever it appears ‘mini or major’; and

(b) in the definition of ‘Secretary’ delete ‘Department of Internal Affairs’ and substitute with ‘Department of Community and Youth’.

#### 5 Amendment of Regulation 6

Regulation 6(a) is amended by deleting ‘the form’ and substitute with ‘Form 1’.

#### 6 Amendment of Regulation 10

Regulation 10 is amended by deleting subregulations (2), (3) and (4) and substitute with:

‘(2)  The Secretary shall allocate the time slot for the conduct of a bingo fundraiser.

(3)  A bingo fundraiser operator shall conduct his or her bingo fundraiser in accordance with the time slot allocated by the Secretary.

(4) A bingo fundraiser operator shall only conduct up to two sessions per week consisting of:

(a) one session of 5 games; and

(b) one session of 10 games.’.

#### 7 Amendment of Regulation 11

Regulation 11 is amended by inserting new subregulation (4) as follows:

‘(4) A take-home bingo operator shall only conduct up to two sessions per week consisting of:

(a) one session of 5 games; and

(b) one session of 10 games.’.

#### 8 Amendment of Regulation 12

Regulation 12 is deleted and substituted with:

‘12  Bingo Market (Kashock)

(1)  A bingo market operator shall be licensed to operate a bingo market.

(2)  A bingo market shall have an unlimited number of bingo players.

(3) A bingo market operator shall comply with the conditions provided under the Act, these Regulations and as specified in the Gaming (Bingo) Licence.

(4)  Subject to Section 13(5) and (6) of the Act, where a bingo market operator’s Gaming (Bingo) Licence is suspended or cancelled during the conduct of the bingo, the operator shall immediately cease the bingo and inform all the bingo players of the suspension or cancellation of the licence.’.

#### 9 Amendment of Regulation 13

Regulation 13 is amended by deleting ‘as set out in Schedule 5’ and substitute it with ‘as allocated by the Secretary’.

#### 10 Amendment of Regulation 14

Regulation 14 is deleted and substituted with:

‘14 Extension

(1)  A bingo operator may only extend the time slots for a bingo market or online bingo upon approval by the Secretary of an extension application and payment of the fee provided under Schedule 3.

(2)  An extension application shall be in Form 2 of Schedule 2.

(3)  The Secretary’s approval of an extension application shall be in Form 3 of Schedule 2.

(4)  The Secretary’s refusal of an extension application shall be in Form 4 of Schedule 2 and shall state the reasons for refusal.

(5) A bingo operator shall not conduct bingo on a Sunday, Christmas Day, Good Friday and any other statutory public holiday which the Secretary prohibits by notice published in the Gazette.’.

#### 11 Amendment of Regulation 18

Regulation 18 is amended by inserting the following after subregulation (5):

‘(6)  A bingo fundraiser operator shall pay out the prizes as advertised and specified in the licence application provided to the Secretary.

(7) A bingo fundraiser operator shall not change prizes after the first bingo player has purchased a booklet.

(8)  A bingo fundraiser operator who contravenes subregulation (6) or (7):

(a) shall be issued with a Notice to Comply in the Form under Schedule 5; and

(b) such bingo fundraiser operator shall pay the winner the prize as advertised and provided in the licence application within 2 weeks from receiving of the Notice to Comply.

(9)  Upon receipt of a Notice to Comply under subregulation (8), a bingo fundraiser operator shall:

(a) where he or she has paid the winner the correct prize under subregulation (8), provide proof of payment within 2 weeks of receiving the Notice to Comply to the Secretary; or

(b) dispute the Notice to Comply, by submitting a written explanation to the Secretary and proof, that the correct prize was paid in accordance with subregulation (6), within 2 weeks of receiving the Notice to Comply.

(10) A bingo fundraiser operator who fails to comply with subregulation (8) shall not be issued a bingo fundraiser licence for a period of 2 weeks from the expiry of the period under subregulation (8)(b).’.

#### 12 Amendment of Regulation 21

Regulation 21 is amended by deleting subregulation (1) and substitute with:

‘(1) A person may make a complaint to the Secretary where a provision of the Act, these Regulations or a condition of a Gaming (Bingo) Licence has been breached.’.
