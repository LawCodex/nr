# Food Safety Notice of Appointment of Food Inspectors under the Food Safety Act 2005

Notice of Appointment of Food Inspectors under the Food Safety Act 2005

## Table of Amendments

The Notice of Appointment of Food Inspectors under the Food Safety Act 2005 SL 9 was notified and commenced on 13 March 2026 (GN No 260/2026; Gaz 92/2026).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026 Notice of Appointment of Food Inspectors under the Food Safety Act 2005

[instr am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

PURSUANT to the power vested in me under Section 19(1) of the Food Safety Act 2005 (‘Act’), I hereby appoint the following suitably qualified persons as food inspectors to administer and enforce the provisions of the Act, its regulations and standards: 1. Iivan Aingimea 2. Felila Peter 3. Yukio Peter 4. Rirry Ribena Andrews 5. Goska Diema 6. Wyatt Bonang Benjamin 7. Dartagneu Bop 8. Hyden Duburiya 9. Erik Duburiya 10. John-Dale Akibwib 11. Opah Thoma Powers of food inspector(s):

The food inspectors(s) shall exercise such powers provided under the Act in the performance of his or her functions.

DATED this 13th day of March 2026 His Excellency, Hon. David W.R. Adeang, M.P.

PRESIDENT AND MINISTER FOR NAOERO PUBLIC HEALTH
