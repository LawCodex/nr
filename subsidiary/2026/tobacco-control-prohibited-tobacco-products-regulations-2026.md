# Tobacco Control (Prohibited Tobacco Products) Regulations 2026

Tobacco Control (Prohibited Tobacco Products) Regulations 2026

## Table of Amendments

The Tobacco Control (Prohibited Tobacco Products) Regulations 2026 SL 21 were notified and commenced on 7 May 2026 (GN No 481/2026; Gaz 158/2026).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 30(f) of the Tobacco Control Act 2009:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Tobacco Control (Prohibited Tobacco Products) Regulations 2026.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Interpretation

In these Regulations:

‘manufacture’ includes the production, assembly, preparation or making of a prohibited tobacco product; and

‘prohibited tobacco product’ means a prohibited tobacco product listed in the Schedule.

#### 4 Prohibition of manufacture of prohibited tobacco products

(1) A person shall not manufacture a prohibited tobacco product in the Republic, for any purpose.

(2) A person who contravenes subregulation (1) commits an offence and upon conviction is liable to a fine not exceeding $10,000 or a term of imprisonment not exceeding 6 months or both.

#### 5 Prohibition of sale or supply of prohibited tobacco products

(1) A person shall not sell or supply a prohibited tobacco product.

(2) A person who contravenes subregulation (1) commits an offence and is liable upon conviction:

(a) in the case of an individual, a fine not exceeding $5,000 or a term of imprisonment not exceeding 6 months or both; or

(b) in the case of a body corporate, a fine not exceeding $25,000.

#### 6 Prohibition of advertising of prohibited tobacco products

(1) A person shall not advertise a prohibited tobacco product.

(2) A person who contravenes subregulation (1) commits an offence and is liable upon conviction:

(a) in the case of an individual, a fine not exceeding $5,000 or a term of imprisonment not exceeding 6 months or both; or

(b) in the case of a body corporate, a fine not exceeding $25,000.

#### 7 Prohibition of sponsorship relating to prohibited tobacco

products

(1) A person shall not provide sponsorship relating to prohibited tobacco products.

(2) A person who contravenes subregulation (1) commits an offence and is liable upon conviction:

(a) in the case of an individual, a fine not exceeding $5,000 or an imprisonment term of 6 months or both; or

(b) in the case of a body corporate, a fine not exceeding $25,000.

#### 8 Prohibition of import or export of prohibited tobacco products

(1) A person shall not import or export a prohibited tobacco product whether for commercial purpose or personal use.

(2) A person who contravenes subregulation (1) commits an offence and is liable upon conviction:

(a) in the case of an individual, a fine not exceeding $5,000 or an imprisonment term of 6 months or both; or

(b) in the case of a body corporate, a fine not exceeding $25,000.

(3) For the purposes of any law regulating the importation of goods, a tobacco prohibited product is a prohibited import.

#### 9 Prohibition of possession, smoking or using prohibited tobacco

products

(1) A person shall not possess, smoke or use a prohibited tobacco product in any place in the Republic.

(2) A person who contravenes subregulation (1) commits an offence and is liable to fine not exceeding $5,000 or an imprisonment term of 6 months.
