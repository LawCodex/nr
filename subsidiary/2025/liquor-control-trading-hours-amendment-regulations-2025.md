# Liquor Control (Trading Hours) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

LIQUOR CONTROL (TRADING HOURS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2025 _____________________________ SL No. 28 of 2025 ______________________________ Notified: 19 May 2025 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 96 of the Liquor Control Act 2017:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Liquor Control (Trading Hours) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Liquor Control (Trading Hours) Regulations 2020

The Liquor Control (Trading Hours) Regulations 2020 is amended by these Regulations.

#### 4 Insertion of Regulation 4

Regulation 4 is inserted after Regulation 3 as follows:

‘4 Trading hours under restaurant licence The trading hours for the sale and supply of liquor under a restaurant licence shall be from 1.00pm to 10pm, from Monday to Friday.’.

#### 5 Insertion of Regulation 5

Regulation 5 is inserted after Regulation 4 as follows:

‘5 Trading hours under a bar licence The trading hours for sale and supply of liquor under a bar licence shall be from 1.00pm to 10pm, from Monday to Friday.’.
