# Declaration of a Public Holiday (Remembrance Day)

Declaration of a Public Holiday

## Table of Amendments

The Declaration of a Public Holiday SL 29 was notified and commenced on 23 June 2025 (GN No 716/2025; Gaz 258/2025).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Declaration of a Public Holiday Pursuant to the powers in that behalf vested in me under Section 81(1)(k) of the Public Service Act 2016, I, HON DAVID WR ADEANG, MP, PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU, DO HEREBY, declare Monday 30th June 2025 as a public holiday to be observed by the officers and employees of the Public Service and its instrumentalities in commemoration of “Remembrance Day”.

DATED this 20th Day of June 2025.

HON. DAVID WR ADEANG, MP PRESIDENT MINISTER FOR PUBLIC SERVICE
