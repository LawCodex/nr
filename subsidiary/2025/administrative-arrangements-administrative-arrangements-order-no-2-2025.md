# Administrative Arrangements Administrative Arrangements Order (No 2) 2025

Administrative Arrangements Order (No 2) 2025

## Table of Amendments

The Administrative Arrangements Order (No 2) 2025 SL 11 was notified and commenced on 16 April 2025 (GN No 433/2025; Gaz 155/2025).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Administrative Arrangements Order (No 2) 2025 I, Hon David WR Adeang, MP, President of the Republic of Nauru, pursuant to Section 4(1) of the Administrative Arrangements Act 2011, make the following Order with effect from 12 March 2025: 1. Each Minister specified in Column 1 of the Schedule to this Order is responsible for the administration of the written law or part of the written law specified in Column 3 of the Schedule. 2. Each Department, Office or instrumentality or part of a Department, Office or instrumentality specified in Column 2 of the Schedule is responsible to the Minister specified in Column 1 for the administration of the law or part of the written law specified in Column 3. 3. Each Deputy Minister may be assigned responsibilities under the Deputy Ministers Act 2023 by the President, of the written law or part of the written law specified in column 3 of the Schedule. 4. This Order revokes the Administrative Arrangements Order 2025.
