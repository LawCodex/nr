# Civil Aviation (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

CIVIL AVIATION (FEES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2025 _____________________________ SL No. 12 of 2025 ______________________________ Notified: 24 April 2025 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 171 of the Civil Aviation Act 2011:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Civil Aviation (Fees) Regulations 2016

The Civil Aviation (Fees) Regulations 2016 are amended by these Regulations.

#### 4 Amendment of the Schedule

The Schedule is deleted and substituted as follows:
