# Naoero Small Business Enterprises Regulations 2025

Naoero Small Business Enterprises Regulations 2025

## Table of Amendments

The Naoero Small Business Enterprises Regulations 2025 SL 39 were notified and commenced on 28 July 2025.

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026 The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Sections 10 and 14 of the Naoero Small Business Enterprises Act 2025:

[cl am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Naoero Small Business Enterprises Regulations 2025.

[reg 1 am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Application process

(1) A small business enterprise eligible under Section 10 may apply to the Committee for funding under the Act.

(2) An application for funding shall:

(a) be in the Form under the Schedule;

(b) provide the required information specified under Regulation 4 and in the Form; and

(c) such other information as requested by the Committee.

#### 4 Required information

The following information is required to be provided when an applicant submits his or her application:

(a) applicant’s name, address, occupation and contact details;

(b) tax identification number from the Naoero Revenue Office;

(c) type of business (individual, partnership, corporation or trust);

(d) detailed nature of the business activity to be undertaken by the small business enterprise;

(e) number of years in business (if applicable);

(f) number of persons to be employed (if applicable);

(g) amount of the funding requested;

(h) statement of the purpose for requesting the funds and details of the use of such funds;

(i) copies of birth certificate, drivers licence or bio page of passport of the applicant or owners of the small business enterprise;

(j) copy of the business proposal or plan;

(k) business licence (if already issued); and

(l) proof of ownership of the business.

[reg 4 am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 5 Consideration of the application

(1) The Committee shall consider each application and inform the applicant of its decision within 14 days of the date of the receipt of the application.

(2) In determining an application under this regulation, the Committee shall consider the requirements under Section 10 of the Act and the following matters:

(a) that the applicant demonstrates the need of funding to establish or support the business; and

(b) information required under regulation 4 and in the Form.

(3) Where the Committee is satisfied that the application complies with the requirements of the Act and these Regulations, it shall grant funding to the applicant.

(4) Where the Committee is not satisfied that the application complies with the requirements of the Act and these Regulations, it shall require the applicant to provide any additional details to reconsider its decision before declining the application.

(5) Where the Committee makes a decision to decline funding to a small business enterprise, it shall inform the applicant in writing of its decision within 14 days from the date of the receipt of the application. reg 5LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO
