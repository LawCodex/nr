# Price Control Order No 1 - 2025

Price Control Order No 1 2025

## Table of Amendments

The Price Control Order No 1 2025 SL 1 was notified and commenced on 22 January 2025.

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Price Control Order No 1 2025 I, HE Hon David W R Adeang MP, Minister for Finance, acting pursuant to Section 36 of the Consumer Protection Act 2024, repeal all previous price orders for the same items in paragraphs (i) to (vi) below, issued under the Prices Regulations Act 2008, and determine that this Price Control Order replaces all repealed Orders and has an immediate effect within 7 days after its publication in the Government Gazette:

(a) Maximum wholesale price at which diesel may be sold is $1.9498/litre;

(b) Maximum wholesale price at which petrol may be sold is $1.8438/litre;

(c) Maximum wholesale price at which JetA1 may be sold is $1.3821/litre;

(d) Maximum retail price at which diesel may be sold is $2.2498/litre;

(e) Maximum retail price at which petrol may be sold is $2.1438/litre; and

(f) Maximum retail price at which Jet A1 may be sold is $1.6821/litre.
