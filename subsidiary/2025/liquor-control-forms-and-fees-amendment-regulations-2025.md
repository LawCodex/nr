# Liquor Control (Forms and Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

LIQUOR CONTROL (FORMS AND FEES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2025 _____________________________ SL No. 27 of 2025 ______________________________ Notified: 19 May 2025 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 96 of the Liquor Control Act 2011:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Liquor Control (Forms and Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Liquor Control (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2021

The Liquor Control (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2021 are amended by these Regulations.

#### 4 Amendment of Schedule 2

(1) Items 1, 2, 3 and 4 of the Schedule are deleted and substituted as follows:

Item Relevant provision of the Act Description Fees 1 Section 13 Application for bar licence $3,000 2 Section 13 Application for restaurant licence $3,000 3 Section 13 Application for off-licence $3,000 4 Section 13 Application for liquor import licence $3,000

#### 5 Section

30(1)(c) Application for renewal of bar licence, restaurant licence, off-licence or liquor import licence $3,000

(2) Items 5 to 11 are renumbered as Items 6 to 12 respectively.
