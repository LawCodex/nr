# Naoero Phosphate Royalties (Payment and Investment) Act 1968 Notice of Increase in Ronphos Landowners Cash Royalties

Naoero Phosphate Royalties (Payment and Investment) Act 1968 Notice of Increase in Ronphos Landowners Cash Royalties

## Table of Amendments

The Naoero Phosphate Royalties (Payment and Investment) Act 1968 Notice of Increase in Ronphos Landowners Cash Royalties SL 30 was notified and commenced on 20 June 2025 (GN No 704/2025; Gaz 251/2025).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026 Naoero Phosphate Royalties (Payment and Investment) Act 1968 Notice of Increase in Ronphos Landowners Cash Royalties

[instr am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

PURSUANT to the powers vested in Cabinet under Section 12 of the Naoero Phosphate Royalties (Payment and Investment) Act 1968 and as per Cabinet Resolution dated 13 January 2025, Cabinet has determined to increase the RONPHOS landowners cash royalties payout to 25 AUD per metric tonne.
