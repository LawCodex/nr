# Food Safety (Prohibited Imports) (Repeal) Regulations 2025

FOOD SAFETY (PROHIBITED IMPORTS) (REPEAL) REGULATIONS 2025 _____________________________ SL No. 4 of 2025 ______________________________ Notified: 20 February 2025 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 39 of the Food Safety Act 2005:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Food Safety (Prohibited Imports) (Repeal) Regulations 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Repeal

The Food (Prohibited Imports) Regulations 2016 are hereby repealed.
