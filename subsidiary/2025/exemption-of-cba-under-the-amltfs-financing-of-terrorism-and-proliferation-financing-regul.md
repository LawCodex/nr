# Exemption of CBA under the AMLTFS (Financing of Terrorism and Proliferation Financing) Regulations 2023

Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Anti- Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions (Financing of Terrorism and Proliferation Financing) Regulations 2023

## Table of Amendments

The Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Anti-Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions (Financing of Terrorism and Proliferation Financing) Regulations 2023 SL 21 was notified and commenced on 19 May 2025 (GN No 579/2025; Gaz 197/2025).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Anti- Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions (Financing of Terrorism and Proliferation Financing) Regulations 2023 PURSUANT to the powers vested in me under Regulation 72 of the Anti-Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions (Financing of Terrorism and Proliferation Financing) Regulations 2023 (‘Regulations’), I, Lionel Rouwen Aingimea, MP, Minister for Justice and Border Control, in consultation with the Cabinet affirmed by Cabinet resolution of 15th May 2025 hereby EXEMPT the Commonwealth Bank of Australia from the requirements of the Regulations.

EXEMPTION is granted on the basis that the Cabinet is satisfied that the Commonwealth Bank of Australia is adequately:

(a) regulated under anti-money laundering and combatting financing of terrorism and proliferation financing laws of Australia; and

(b) supervised by the relevant authorities of the Commonwealth of Australia.
