# Consumer Protection Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Consumer Protection Act 2024

Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Consumer Protection Act 2024

## Table of Amendments

The Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Consumer Protection Act 2024 SL 24 was notified and commenced on 19 May 2025 (GN No 582/2025;

Gaz 200/2025).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Consumer Protection Act 2024 PURSUANT to the powers vested in me under Section 74 of the Consumer Protection Act 2024 (‘Act’), I, Maverick Eoe, MP, Minister for Consumer Protection, in consultation with the Cabinet affirmed by Cabinet resolution of 15th May 2025 hereby EXEMPT the Commonwealth Bank of Australia from the requirements of the Act.

EXEMPTION is granted on the basis that the Cabinet is satisfied that the Commonwealth Bank of Australia is adequately regulated under comprehensive competition and consumer protection laws of the Commonwealth of Australia.
