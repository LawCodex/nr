# Motion of Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Corporations Act 1972

Motion of Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Corporations Act 1972

## Table of Amendments

The Motion of Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Corporations Act 1972 SL 19 was notified and commenced on 29 April 2025 (GN No 475/2025; Gaz 175/2025).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Motion of Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Corporations Act 1972 PURSUANT to the powers vested in me under Section 246 of the Corporations Act 1972, by this Motion, I exempt the Commonwealth Bank of Australia, from the requirements of the Corporations Act 1972.

Hon. Lionel Rouwen Aingimea, M.P.

Minister
