# Education (Fixed Penalty Notices) Regulations 2025

Education (Fixed Penalty Notices) Regulations 2025

## Table of Amendments

The Education (Fixed Penalty Notices) Regulations 2025 SL 47 were notified and commenced on 13 September 2025 (GN No 1105/2025; Gaz 416/2025).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026 The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Sections 113C and 114 of the Education Act 2011:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Fixed Penalty Notices) Regulations 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Objectives

The objectives of these Regulations are to:

(a) establish a system for the issuance and enforcement of fixed penalty notices in respect of compulsory school attendance of students;

(b) deter the non-attendance of students to school; and

(c) encourage parents to ensure their children attend school.

#### 4 Interpretation

In these Regulations:

‘Act’ means the Education Act 2011; and

‘fixed penalty notice’ means the fixed penalty notice issued in accordance with Section 113C of the Act and these Regulations.

#### 5 Fixed penalty offences

For the purpose of Section 113C(2)(a) of the Act, an offence against any of the following provisions shall be deemed to be a fixed penalty offence:

(a) Section 8, obligation of parent to ensure the child is enrolled at a school until the child competes the school year during which the child attains the age of 18;

(b) Section 14, obligation of parent to ensure attendance of a child to school;

(c) Section 16, parent to ensure non-attendance of child in certain circumstances;

(d) Section 17, employment of school-age children during school hours; and

(e) Section 37, using corporal punishment for any circumstances other that those permitted under Section 37.

#### 6 Penalties for fixed penalty offences

For the purpose of Section 113C(2)(b) of the Act, the following penalties shall apply for each corresponding fixed penalty offence:

(a) for contravention of Section 8, a person shall be liable to the following fixed penalty:

(i) for a first offence $25; or

(ii) for a subsequent offence, whether or not relating to the same child of the parent, $125;

(b) for contravention of Section 14, a person shall be liable to the following fixed penalty:

(i) for a first offence $25; or

(ii) for a subsequent offence in the same school year, whether or not relating to the same child of the parent, $125;

(c) for contravention of Section 16, a person shall be liable to a fixed penalty of $25;

(d) for contravention of Section 17, a person shall be liable to a fixed penalty of $500; and

(e) for contravention of Section 37, a person shall be liable to a fixed penalty of $250.

#### 7 Fixed penalty notice

A fixed penalty notice which an authorised officer issues to a person, shall be in the Form under the Schedule.

#### 8 Failure to pay fixed penalty

A person, who has been issued with a fixed penalty notice and fails to pay the fixed penalty as required by the Act and these Regulations shall be:

(a) summoned to attend court;

(b) in addition to the fixed penalty, liable to a fine not exceeding $1,000 or a term of imprisonment not exceeding 12 months or to both; and

(c) liable to pay a mandatory court cost of $100. reg 6LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO
