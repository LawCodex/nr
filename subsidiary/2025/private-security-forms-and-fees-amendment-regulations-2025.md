# Private Security (Forms and Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

PRIVATE SECURITY (FORMS AND FEES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2025 _____________________________ SL No. 33 of 2025 ______________________________ Notified: 25 June 2025 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 36 of the Private Security Act 2012:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Private Security (Forms and Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Private Security (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2019

The Private Security (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2019 is amended by these Regulations.

#### 4 Insertion of new Regulation 7A

Regulation 7A is inserted after Regulation 7:

‘7A   Application for restoration of business licence For the purpose of Section 22A of the Act, the application for the restoration of a security licence is in Form 6 of Schedule 1.’.
