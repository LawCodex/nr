# Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Business Licences Act 2017

Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Business Licences Act 2017

## Table of Amendments

The Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Business Licences Act 2017 SL 23 was notified and commenced on 19 May 2025 (GN No 581/2025;

Gaz 199/2025).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Business Licences Act 2017 PURSUANT to the powers vested in me under Section 27 of the Business Licences Act 2017 (‘Act’), I, Hon Lionel Rouwen Aingimea, MP, Minister for Justice and Border Control, in consultation with the Cabinet affirmed by Cabinet resolution of 15th May 2025 hereby EXEMPT the Commonwealth Bank of Australia from the requirements of the Act.

EXEMPTION is granted on the basis that the Cabinet is satisfied that the Commonwealth Bank of Australia is adequately regulated under business licence registration laws of the Commonwealth of Australia.
