# Referendum Procedures (Amendment of the Schedule) Regulations 2025

REFERENDUM PROCEDURES (AMENDMENT OF THE SCHEDULE) REGULATIONS 2025 _____________________________ SL No. 56 of 2025 ______________________________ Notified: 2 October 2025 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 27 of the Referendum Procedures Act 2025:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Referendum Procedures (Amendment of the Schedule) Regulations 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Referendum Procedures Act 2025

The Schedule of the Referendum Procedures Act 2025 is amended by these Regulations.

#### 4 Amendment of the Schedule

Form 1 of the Schedule is deleted and substituted as follows:

‘ FORM 1 Republic of Nauru REFERENDUM PROCEDURES ACT 2025

[Sections 13 and 14]

BALLOT PAPER – ARTICLE 84 REFERENDUM Note for Voters For the purposes of voting in this Referendum, the Parliament has passed the Constitution (Amendment) Bill 2025 to extend the term of Parliament from 3 to 4 years.

This Bill can only become law if two thirds of the votes cast in the referendum are in favour of it. The Bill is to extend the current term of Parliament from 3 to 4 years. As a voter you have to answer the question below. The sample of how to vote on whether you agree or disagree with the extension of the term from 3 to 4 years is provided below.

Do you agree that the term of the Parliament be increased from 3 to 4 years as approved and passed by the Parliament pursuant to the Constitution (Amendment) Bill 2025?
