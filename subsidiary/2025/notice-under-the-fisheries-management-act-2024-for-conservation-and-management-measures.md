# Notice under the Fisheries Management Act 2024 for Conservation and Management Measures

Notice under the Fisheries Management Act 2024 for Conservation and Management Measures TABLE OF AMENDENTS The Notice under the Fisheries Management Act 2024 for Conservation and Management Measures SL 57 was notified and commenced on 12 November 2025 (GN No 1372 /2025;

Gaz 530/2025).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Notice under the Fisheries Management Act 2024 for Conservation and Management Measures Notice by the Minister pursuant to Section 24 of the Fisheries Management Act 2024. The Schedule of Conservation and Management Measures (CMMs) adopted by the Western and Central Pacific Fisheries Commission (WCPFC) is hereby publish in the following Schedule. The CMMs adopted by the WCPFC shall apply to Nauru’s Exclusive Economic Zone and all fishing vessels flagged to Nauru and operating in waters outside of Nauru’s Fisheries waters.

In the event any WCPFC CMM as listed herein is updated, amended or revised by a further CMM, the provisions of such further CMM shall be immediately published under a further Notice.
