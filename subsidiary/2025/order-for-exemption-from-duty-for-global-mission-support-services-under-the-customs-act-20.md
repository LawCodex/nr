Order for Exemption from Duty for Global Mission Support Services under the Customs Act 2014

[instr am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

In accordance with Section 103(1) of the Customs Act 2014, this Order is made to exempt from duty, the goods that are imported by the Global Mission Support Services (GMSS DMCC), for the implementation of the Naoero Health Services Management Agreement.

This exemption has received the concurrence of the Cabinet as confirmed in the Cabinet resolution dated Thursday, 19th June 2025.

Dated this 8th day of September 2025 His Excellency, Hon. David W.R. Adeang, MP PRESIDENT AND MINISTER FOR FINANCE
