# Nauru Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship (Amendment) Regulations (No.2) 2025

NAURU ECONOMIC AND CLIMATE RESILIENCE CITIZENSHIP (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS (NO.2) 2025 _____________________________ SL No. 52 of 2025 ______________________________ Notified: 18 September 2025 Table of Provisions Republic of Nauru The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 30 of the Nauru Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Act 2024:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Nauru Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship (Amendment) Regulations (No.2) 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 4 Savings and transitional provisions

Where an application in relation to Form 1 under Schedule 6 is made to the Program Office prior to the commencement of the Nauru Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship (Amendment) Regulations (No.2) 2025, the application shall be deemed to be made under these Regulations and shall be considered under the Nauru Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship (Amendment) Regulations (No.2) 2025.
