# AMLTFS Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia from the requirements of Part 4 of the AMLTFS Act 2023

Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia from the requirements of Part 4 of the Anti- Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions Act 2023

## Table of Amendments

The Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia from the requirements of Part 4 of the Anti-Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions Act 2023 SL 18 was notified and commenced on 29 April 2025 (GN No 474/2025; Gaz 174/2025).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia from the requirements of Part 4 of the Anti- Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions Act 2023 PURSUANT to the powers vested in me under Section 14 of the Anti-Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions Act 2023 (‘Act’), I, Lionel Rouwen Aingimea, MP, Minister for Justice and Border Control, in consultation with the Cabinet and affirmed by Cabinet resolution of 28th April 2024, hereby EXEMPT the Commonwealth Bank of Australia from the requirements of Part 4 of the Act.

EXEMPTION is granted on the basis that the Cabinet is satisfied that the Commonwealth Bank of Australia is adequately:

(a) regulated under anti-money laundering and combatting financing of terrorism laws of Australia; and

(b) supervised by the relevant authorities of Australia.
