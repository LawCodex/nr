# Referendum Procedures (Article 84) Notice 2025

Referendum Procedures (Article 84) Notice 2025

## Table of Amendments

The Referendum Procedures (Article 84) Notice 2025 SL 41 were notified and commenced on 15 August 2025 (GN No 946/2025; Gaz 351/2025.

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Referendum Procedures (Article 84) Notice 2025 The CABINET in its meeting of Wednesday, 13th August 2025, by its resolution fixed the date for the Article 84 referendum for the Constitution (Amendment) Bill 2025 to be undertaken in accordance with Section 6(4) of the Referendum Procedures Act 2025, that is, the referendum to be held concurrently with the dates fixed for the General Elections in 2025 under Article 39 of the Constitution.

Dated this 15th day of August 2025.

His Excellency, Hon. David W.R. Adeang, M.P President and Chairperson of the Cabinet
