# Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime Act 2004

Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime Act 2004

## Table of Amendments

The Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime Act 2004 SL 22 was notified and commenced on 19 May 2025 (GN No 580/2025; Gaz 198/2025).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime Act 2004 PURSUANT to the powers vested in me under Section 81 of the Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime Act 2004 (‘Act’), I, Hon Lionel Rouwen Aingimea, MP, Minister for Justice and Border Control, in consultation with the Cabinet, affirmed by Cabinet resolution of 15th May 2025 hereby EXEMPT the Commonwealth Bank of Australia from the requirements of the Act.

EXEMPTION is granted on the basis that the Cabinet is satisfied that the Commonwealth Bank of Australia is adequately regulated under counter terrorism and transnational organised crime laws of the Commonwealth of Australia.
