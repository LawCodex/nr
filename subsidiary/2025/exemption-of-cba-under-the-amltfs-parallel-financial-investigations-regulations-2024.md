# Exemption of CBA under the AMLTFS (Parallel Financial Investigations) Regulations 2024

Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Anti- Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions (Parallel Financial Investigations) Regulations 2024

## Table of Amendments

The Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Anti-Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions (Parallel Financial Investigations) Regulations 2024 SL 20 was notified and commenced on 19 May 2025 (GN No 578/2025;

Gaz 196/2025).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Exemption of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia under the Anti- Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions (Parallel Financial Investigations) Regulations 2024 PURSUANT to the powers vested in me under Regulation 14 of the Anti-Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions (Parallel Financial Investigations) Regulations 2024 (‘Regulations’), I, Lionel Rouwen Aingimea, MP, Minister for Justice and Border Control, in consultation with the Cabinet affirmed by Cabinet resolution of 15th May 2024 hereby EXEMPT the Commonwealth Bank of Australia from the requirements of the Regulations.

EXEMPTION is granted on the basis that the Cabinet is satisfied that the Commonwealth Bank of Australia is adequately:

(a) regulated under parallel financial investigation relating to anti-money laundering and targeted financial sanctions laws of Australia; and

(b) supervised by the relevant authorities of the Commonwealth of Australia.
