# Electoral (Amendment of Schedules 2 and 3) Regulations 2025

ELECTORAL (AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULES 2 AND 3) REGULATIONS 2025 _____________________________ SL No.  6  of 2025 ______________________________ Notified: 14 March 2025 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 136(i) of the Electoral Act 2016:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Electoral (Amendment of Schedules 2 and 3) Regulations 2025.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Schedules 2 and 3 of the Electoral Act 2016

Schedules 2 and 3 of the Electoral Act 2016 are amended by the provisions of these Regulations.
