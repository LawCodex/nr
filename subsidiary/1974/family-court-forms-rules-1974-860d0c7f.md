# Family Court (Forms) Rules 1974

FAMILY COURT ACT 1973 FAMILY COURT (FORMS) RULES 1974 IN EXERCISE of the powers conferred to me by section 29 of the Family Court Act 1973.

I hereby make the following rules of court.

SHORT TITLE 1. These Rules may be cited as the Family Court (Forms) Rules 1974.

FORMS 2. The forms set out in the Schedule to these Rules with such modification if any as may be appropriate shall be used for the purposes stated thereon.

MADE this thirteenth day of Septmber 1974 ___________________
