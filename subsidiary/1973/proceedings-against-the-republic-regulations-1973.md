# Proceedings Against the Republic Regulations 1973

Proceedings Against the Republic Regulations 1973

## Table of Amendments

The Proceedings Against the Republic Regulations 1973 were made and commenced on 31 October 1973.

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Revised Written Laws Act 2021 No 7 1 June 2021 1 June 2021 Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026 IN EXERCISE of the powers conferred on it by Section 3 of the Republic Proceedings Act 1972, the Cabinet has made the following regulations:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Proceedings Against the Republic Regulations 1973.

#### 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations ‘civil proceedings against the Republic’ has the same meaning as in the Act, which includes civil proceedings by or against an instrumentality of the Republic or a statutory corporation to which the provisions of Section 3 of the Act have been applied by the Cabinet but does not include any of the proceedings specified in Section 16 of the Act.

#### 3 Application for leave to commence proceedings

(1) Application to the Cabinet for leave under Section 3 of the Act to take civil proceedings against the Republic shall be made in writing in the form set out in the Appendix to these Regulations, signed by the person in whose name it is intended that the proceedings should be taken or where that person is a body corporate or instrumentality of the Republic, by an officer thereof and shall be sent or delivered to the Secretary to the Cabinet.

(2) Every such application shall be accompanied by a copy of:

(a) the statement of claim which the applicant intends to indorse on the writ of summons or to serve;

(b) the counterclaim which he or she intends to add to his or her defence; or

(c) the third party notice which he or she intends to issue, as the case may be in pursuance of his or her claim against the Republic and shall be indorsed with details of the applicant’s full name and occupation and of his or her usual places of residence and of business if any.

(3) The statement of claim, counterclaim or third party notice referred to in subregulation (2) shall include a statement of the circumstances in which the Republic’s liability is alleged to have arisen and as to the Government department, instrumentality of the Republic or statutory corporation or the President or the officer of the Republic concerned.

#### 4 Cabinet may require further details of claim

(1) If the Cabinet having obtained the advice of the Secretary for Justice thereon considers that the statement of claim, counterclaim or third party notice referred to in Regulation 3(2) does not comply with Regulation 3(3), it may direct the Secretary to the Cabinet to notify the applicant accordingly.

(2) If the applicant is seeking leave to take proceedings against the Republic on behalf of or as the representative of any other person or in respect of any cause of action which he or she alleges or which appears from his or her statement of the circumstances in which the Republic’s liability is alleged to have arisen to be vested in any other person in addition to himself or herself, the Cabinet may direct the Secretary to the Cabinet to require him or her to supply to it details of the full name, occupation and places of residence and of business, if any of that other person.

(3) Where the Cabinet gives any direction to the Secretary to the Cabinet under subregulation (1) or subregulation (2), he or she shall forthwith notify the applicant in writing of the Cabinet’s requirement and the applicant shall send or deliver to the Secretary to the Cabinet in writing for transmission to the Cabinet any information necessary to comply with that requirement. reg 4LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO APPENDIX REPUBLIC OF NAOERO
