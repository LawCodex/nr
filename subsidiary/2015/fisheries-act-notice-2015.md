# Fisheries Act Notice 2015

TABLE OF AMENDENTS The Fisheries Act Notice 2015 SL 16 was notified and commenced on 11 November 2015 (GN No 708/2015; Gaz 160/2015).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Fisheries Act Notice 2015 I, Hon Valdon Dowiyogo, Minister for Fisheries, under Section 11(1) of the Fisheries Act 1997 hereby prohibit the commercial fishing and export of all species of sea cucumbers listed below and any species of sea cucumbers not listed that may be found in the future in Nauru waters.
