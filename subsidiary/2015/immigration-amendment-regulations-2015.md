#### 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

These Regulations commence on the day on which they are published in the Gazette1.

#### 3 Regulations Amended

The Schedule amends the Immigration Regulations 2014.

## SCHEDULE – AMENDMENT OF IMMIGRATION REGULATIONS 2014

[1]  Amendment of regulation 9(6) (c) (ii) After the first occurrence of the words ‘service provider’ delete the following words ‘and the holder is in the company of a service provider or another person approved by a service provider’.

New clause will now read:

‘(c) after a health and security clearance certificate is granted to the holder, the holder must remain at those premises or at common areas notified to the holder by a service provider, except:

(i) in case of emergency or other extraordinary circumstances; or

(ii) in circumstances where the absence is organised or permitted by a service provider.’
