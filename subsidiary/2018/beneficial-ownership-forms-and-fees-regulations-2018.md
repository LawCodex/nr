# Beneficial Ownership (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2018

Beneficial Ownership (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2018

## Table of Amendments

The Beneficial Ownership (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2018 SL 29 was notified and commenced on 20 November 2018 (GN No 878/2018; Gaz 171/2018).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Revised Written Laws Act 2021 No 7 1 June 2021 1 June 2021 Beneficial Ownership (Identity and Declaration) (Amendment) Regulations 2024 SL 4 28 March 2024 28 March 2024 Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026 The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 34 of the Beneficial Ownership Act 2018:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Beneficial Ownership (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2018.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Legal entities to which the forms apply

These Regulations shall apply to legal entities under the Act.

#### 4 Notice of appointment or change of nominated officer

(1) For the purposes of Section 8(5) of the Act, the form for notifying the Authority of:

(a) an appointment of a nominated officer, shall be in Form 1 of Schedule 1; and

(b) a change of a nominated officer, shall be in Form 2 of Schedule 1.

(2) The legal entity shall pay the fee for the notice of appointment or change of nominated officer as contained in Schedule 2.

#### 5 Registration of beneficial ownership information

(1) The registration of the beneficial ownership information of a legal entity for the purposes of Section 21(1) of the Act, shall be in Form 3 of Schedule 1.

(2) The legal entity shall pay the fee as contained in Schedule 2 for the registration of beneficial ownership information.

#### 6 Notice of change of beneficial ownership

(1) Any change in the beneficial ownership of a legal entity shall be registered by the legal entity with the Authority within 30 days after the date of the decision of such change.

(2) The registration of any change of beneficial ownership shall be in Form 4 of Schedule 1.

(3) The legal entity shall pay the fee as contained in Schedule 2 for registering the notice of change of beneficial ownership.

#### 7 Beneficial ownership information shall be submitted when

requested The form for providing beneficial ownership information by a legal entity when required by the Authority shall be in Form 5 of Schedule 1.

#### 8 Annual Return

(1) The form for filing annual return by corporations under Section 43 of the Act shall be in Form 6 of Schedule 1.

(2) The annual return filed by corporations under the Corporations Act 1972 shall accompany Form 6.

(3) The legal entity shall pay the fee as contained in Schedule 2 for the filing of annual return. reg 8LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO
