# Motor Traffic (Amendment) Regulations No 2 2017

MOTOR TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS NO. 2 2017 ______________________________ SL No. 16 of 2017 ______________________________ Notified: 2nd August 2017 Table of Contents Cabinet makes the following Regulations under section 132 of the Motor Traffic Act 2014:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Traffic (Amendment) Regulations No. 2 2017.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of Motor Traffic Regulations 2014

The Motor Traffic Regulations 2014 is amended by the provisions of these Regulations.

#### 4 Amendment of Regulation 6

(1) Regulation 6 (1)(cf) is omitted and substituted with the following:

(cf)  for the official vehicles of the Parliament – the letters “PN” followed by numerals in black on a white background.

(2)  Regulation 6 (1)(h) is omitted and substituted with the following:

(h)  for privately owned motor vehicles:

(i) three letters followed by three numerals in a series commencing with “AAA 001” and ending with “AAA 999”, in black on a white background; and

(ii) at the end of “AAA 999” series, the following numbering series shall apply: a. the middle letter of ‘AAA’ only changes in the order ‘A to Z’; and b. the numerals shall commence as ‘001 to 999’ at the change in each numbering series.
