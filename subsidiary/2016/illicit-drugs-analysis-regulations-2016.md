# Illicit Drugs (Analysis) Regulations 2016

ILLICIT DRUGS (ANALYSIS) REGULATIONS 2016 ______________________________ SL No. 18 of 2016 ______________________________ Notified: 8 July 2016 Table of Contents The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under section 45 (4) of the Illicit Drugs Control Act 2004:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Illicit Drugs (Analysis) Regulations 2016.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Interpretation

Words and expressions defined under the Act have the same meaning under these Regulations.

#### 4 Persons qualified to conduct analysis

(1) A person who has undergone training and has experience in the analysis of an illicit drug, controlled chemical or controlled equipment is qualified under these Regulations to analyse such drug, chemical or equipment.

(2) A person qualified in accordance with sub-regulation (1) must have his or her name published in the Government Gazette.

#### 5 Analysis certificate to be provided

For every test conducted in accordance with regulation 4, the person conducting such test must certify the results in the form prescribed in the Schedule to these Regulations.

#### 6 Certificate to be accepted by Court

A certificate provided in accordance with regulation 5 must be accepted by a Court as proof of the truth and accuracy of the statements contained therein unless the statements are shown to be false in a material aspect.
