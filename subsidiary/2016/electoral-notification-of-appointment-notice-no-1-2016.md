# Electoral (Notification of Appointment) Notice No 1 2016

OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT ELECTORAL ACT 2016 ELECTORAL (NOTIFICATION OF APPOINTMENT) NOTICE NO. 1 2016 S.L No. 4 I, Honourable BARON DIVAVESI WAQA, M.P and President acting in accordance with powers vested in me by sections 14 and 24 of the Electoral Act 2016 hereby notify the public of the following appointments which are to take effect from the date of this notice: 1.) Mr. Joseph Cain to the position of Electoral Commissioner; and 2.) Mrs. Corinne Joram and Mrs. Sylvanna Deireragea to the positions of Deputy Electoral Commissioners.

Dated this 21st day of March 2016 HON. BARON DIVAVESI WAQA M.P.

PRESIDENT
