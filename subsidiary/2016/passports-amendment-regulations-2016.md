# Passports (Amendment) Regulations 2016

______________________________ SL No. 10 of 2016 ______________________________ Notified: 7th June 2016 Table of Contents Cabinet makes the following Regulations under section 57 of the Passports Act 2011:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Passports (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations are deemed to have taken effect from 30th May 2016.

#### 3 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the contrary intention appears, words and expressions defined under the Act have the same meaning under these Regulations.

#### 4 Amendment of the Passport Regulations 2011

These Regulations amend the Passport Regulations 2011.

#### 5 Amendment of Regulation 5

Regulation 5 (a) is amended by deleting the number ‘5’ and inserting the number ‘10’.

#### 6 Amendment of Schedule 3

The fee in Part 1 Item 1 is amended by deleting the number ‘40’ and inserting the number ‘60’.
