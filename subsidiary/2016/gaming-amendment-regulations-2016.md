# Gaming (Amendment) Regulations 2016

______________________________ SL No. 22 of 2016 ______________________________ Notified: 9th September 2016 Table of Contents Cabinet makes the following Regulations under section 31 of the Gaming Act 2011:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Gaming Regulations 2011

These Regulations amend the Gaming Regulations 2011.

#### 4 Repeal and substitution of Schedule 1

Schedule 1 is repealed and substituted with the following:
