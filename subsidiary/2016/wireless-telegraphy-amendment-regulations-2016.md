# Wireless Telegraphy (Amendment) Regulations 2016

WIRELESS TELEGRAPHY (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2016 ______________________________ SL No. 25 of 2016 ______________________________ Notified: 4th November 2016 Table of Contents Cabinet makes the following Regulations under section 57 of the Telecommunications Act 2002:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of Regulation 61

Regulation 61 is omitted and replaced with the following: 61  Failure to comply Any person operating without a licence or without a valid licence or who breaches a condition of a licence commits an offence and may:

(a) have their licence revoked; and

(b) be required to pay twice the amount of the licence fee for the duration of the default period.
