# Motor Traffic (Approved Insurance Company) Notice 2014

MOTOR TRAFFIC ACT 2014 (As in force 10th September, 2014) MOTOR TRAFFIC (APPROVED INSURANCE COMPANY) NOTICE 2014 S.L No. 13 I, VALDON DOWIYOGO, Minister responsible for Transport, pursuant to the Motor Traffic Act 2014 give notice that as of 11th of September, 2014: 1. Eigigu Holdings Corporation trading as Eigigu Financial Services Inc. is an approved Insurance Company for the purposes of carrying out any business authorised under the Motor Traffic Act 2014.

Dated this 19th day of September 2014.

Hon. Valdon Dowiyogo M.P Minister for Transport
