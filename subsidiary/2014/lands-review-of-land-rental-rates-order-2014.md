# Lands (Review of Land Rental Rates) Order 2014

Lands (Review of Land Rental Rates) Order 2014

## Table of Amendments

The Lands (Review of Land Rental Rates) Order 2014 SL 6 was notified on 12 June 2014 and commenced on 1 July 2014 (order 2).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement The Cabinet in exercise of its powers under Section 16 of the Lands Act 1976 makes the following Order:

#### 1 Citation

This Order may be cited as the Lands (Review of Land Rental Rates) Order 2014.

#### 2 Commencement

This Order shall commence on 1 July 2014.
