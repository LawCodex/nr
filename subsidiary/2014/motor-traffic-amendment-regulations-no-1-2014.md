## SCHEDULE - AMENDMENT OF MOTOR TRAFFIC REGULATIONS 2014

[1] Amendment of Regulation 6 (1) (c)…………………………………………..2 Cabinet makes the following regulations under section 132 of the Motor Traffic Act 2014:

#### 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Traffic (Amendment) Regulations No. 2, 2014.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day on which they are published in the Gazette.

#### 3 Act Amended

The Schedule amends the Motor Traffic Regulations 2014.

## SCHEDULE - AMENDMENT OF MOTOR TRAFFIC REGULATIONS 2014

[1]       Amendment of Regulation 6 (1) (c) Omit

‘for the official vehicles of members of the Diplomatic Corps–the letters

“DC” followed by numerals, in black on a white background’ Substitute

‘for the official vehicles of members of the Diplomatic Corp – a maximum of three letters identifying the country followed by the letters DC and followed by numerals beginning with 01’.
