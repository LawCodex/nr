## SCHEDULE - AMENDMENT OF IMMIGRATION REGULATIONS 2014

Immigration (Amendment) Regulations 2014 Cabinet makes the following regulations under section 33 of the Immigration Act 2014:

#### 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration (Amendment) Regulations 2014.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day on which they are published in the Gazette.

#### 9A Temporary settlement visa

(1) If the Secretary for Justice or the Nauru Refugee Status Review Tribunal notifies the holder of a regional processing centre visa in writing that a determination has been made that the holder is recognized as a refugee, is granted derivative status or is in need of complementary protection, the regional processing centre visa currently held by the person automatically becomes a temporary settlement visa.

(2) The duration of a temporary settlement visa is six months.

(a)  The holder of a temporary settlement visa may- i. engage in employment for an authorized employer who has been granted authorization by the Secretary; ii. apply for a business licence under the Business Licences Act; iii. apply for a refugee travel document; iv. leave and re-enter Nauru during the period of the temporary settlement visa; and v. apply for a dependant to join them in Nauru on a dependant’s visa if so permitted by the Secretary.

(b)  The holder of a temporary settlement visa- i. must not behave in a manner prejudicial to the peace and good order of Nauru; and ii. must take all reasonable steps to ensure that a person who is his or her dependant and holds a temporary settlement visa complies with the conditions of that visa.

(3) A temporary settlement visa may be granted subject to any other reasonable  condition  (not  inconsistent  with  the  Act, the Refugees  Convention  Act  2012, the Refugees Convention (Amendment) Act 2014 or  these  Regulations)  that  the Secretary, considers necessary or desirable.

(4) The Minister may at any time cancel a temporary settlement visa.

(5) If the temporary protection visa of a person mentioned in subregulation (1) is cancelled, the visa of a person) who is a dependant of that person is also cancelled.
