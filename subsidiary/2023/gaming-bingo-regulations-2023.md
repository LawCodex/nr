# Gaming (Bingo) Regulations 2023

## Table of Amendments

The Gaming (Bingo) Regulations 2023 SL 18 were notified and commenced on 7 July 2023 (GN No 748/2023; Gaz 132/2023).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026 Gaming (Bingo) (Amendment) Regulations 2026 No 29 10 July 2026 10 July 2026 The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 31 of the Gaming Act 2011:

## PART 1 — PRELIMINARY

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming (Bingo) Regulations 2023.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Interpretation

(1) In these Regulations:

‘Act’ means the Gaming Act 2011;

‘bingo’ means an exempt game with a chance of winning that is dependent on a set of numbers in a bingo booklet purchased and daubed by a bingo player according to whether the numbers are called out by the bingo caller, and where the winner of the game is the first bingo player to completely daub the required sequence of numbers as they are called;

‘bingo booklet’ means the booklet used in bingo which contains a set of numbers to be daubed by a bingo player;

‘bingo caller’ means a person, an application, software or any other device that calls out the numbers during bingo;

‘bingo fundraiser’ means a single event bingo that is licensed to be conducted by a session or multiple sessions on a one-off basis, and where the Gaming (Bingo) Licence for the bingo fundraiser expires at the end of that one single event upon which time a new licence shall be applied for if another bingo fundraiser event is to be conducted;

‘bingo fundraiser operator’ means a person who is licensed to conduct a bingo fundraiser and includes any person who is hired, employed or authorised by the former person to conduct the bingo fundraiser;

‘bingo market’ means a continuous bingo game that is licensed to be conducted for a limited daily time period as per the weekly frequency applied for, and where the Gaming (Bingo) Licence for the bingo market expires at the end of 12 months upon which time a new licence shall be applied for, to continue the bingo market;

‘bingo market operator’ means a person who is licensed to conduct a bingo market and includes any person who is hired, employed or authorised by the former person to conduct the bingo market;

[def am SL 29 of 2026 reg 4(a), opn 10 July 2026]

‘bingo operator’ means a bingo fundraiser operator, take-home bingo operator, bingo market operator or online bingo operator;

‘bingo player’ means a person who purchases a valid bingo booklet for the purposes of playing bingo;

‘bingo runner’ means a person who verifies the winning numbers of a bingo player with the bingo caller;

‘Business Licence’ means a business licence granted to a person under the Business Licences Act 2017;

‘Business Licensing Division’ means the Business Registration, Business Licensing, Security Licensing, Import Licensing and Beneficial Ownership Division under the Department of Justice and Border Control;

‘child’ means a person who is under the age of 18 years;

‘daub’ means to highlight a number that is called out by a bingo caller;

‘Gaming (Bingo) Licence’ means a gaming licence granted to a person under Section 6 of the Act authorising the person to conduct bingo, and includes an Interim Gaming (Bingo) Licence granted to a person under Regulation 22;

‘jackpot prize’ means the top prize in a game or contest that is typically a large fund of money formed by the accumulation of unwon prizes;

‘legitimate items’ means items that are legal in Naoero;

[def am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

‘licence fee’ means the Gaming (Bingo) Licence fee;

‘no taker prize’ means the giveaway prize where the jackpot prize is not won;

‘online bingo’ means bingo that requires the use of Internet data to be conducted and played online;

‘online bingo operator’ means a person who is licensed to conduct online bingo and includes any person who is hired, employed or authorised by the former person to conduct the online bingo;

‘premises’ means the place where a bingo market is authorised to be conducted under a Gaming (Bingo) Licence;

‘Secretary’, for the purposes of these Regulations, means the Head of the Department of Community and Youth;

[def am SL 29 of 2026 reg 4(b), opn 10 July 2026]

‘statutory public holiday’ means a public holiday under Section 81 of the Public Service Act 2016;

‘take-home bingo’ means a single event bingo where a bingo booklet:

(a) is purchased by a bingo player;

(b) includes a set sequence of pre-called numbers;

(c) may be daubed at the bingo player’s home or any other place; and

(d) is submitted to the take-home bingo operator at a particular date to determine the winning booklet;

‘take-home bingo operator’ means a person who is licensed to conduct a take-home bingo and includes any person who is hired or employed by the former person to conduct the take-home bingo;

‘time slots’ means the allocated times permitted for bingo fundraisers to be conducted; reg 3LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

‘valid bingo booklet’ means a bingo booklet marked by a bingo operator to show that it was purchased or obtained by a bingo player at that particular bingo;

‘vehicle’ has the meaning given to it under Section 5(1) of the Motor Traffıc Act 2014; and

‘venue’ means the place where a bingo, other than a bingo market, is authorised to be conducted under a Gaming (Bingo) Licence.

(2) In these Regulations, words and phrases have the same meaning as those under the Act.

#### 4 Application

These Regulations shall apply to all the types of bingo set out in Schedule 1. reg 4 GAMING (BINGO) REGULATIONS 2023

## PART 2 — LICENSING

#### 5 Licences required to conduct bingo

A person shall not conduct bingo unless the person holds a valid:

(a) Business Licence; and

(b) Gaming (Bingo) Licence.

#### 6 Application for Gaming (Bingo) Licence

For the purposes of Section 5 of the Act, an application for a Gaming (Bingo) Licence shall be:

(a) made to the Secretary in Form 1 as set out in Schedule 2;

(b) accompanied by the required particulars; and

(c) accompanied by evidence of payment of the licence fee as set out in Schedule 3; or

(d) where waiver of the licence fee is sought under Section 14 of the Act, accompanied by an application for a waiver of the licence fee.

[reg 6 am SL 29 of 2026 reg 5, opn 10 July 2026]

#### 7 Decision of the Secretary

(1) Where the Secretary grants the Gaming (Bingo) Licence, the licence shall be issued in the form as set out in Form 1 of Schedule 4.

(2) Where the Secretary refuses to grant the Gaming (Bingo) Licence, he or she shall:

(a) inform the applicant in writing in the form as set out in Form 2 of Schedule 4; and

(b) provide the reason for the refusal.

#### 8 Duration of bingo

The licence fee for a Gaming (Bingo) Licence as set out in Schedule 3 applies to the duration of the bingo as set out in column 2 of the table in Schedule 3 for which the Gaming (Bingo) Licence is granted.

#### 9 Display of licences

(1) The Business Licence and Gaming (Bingo) Licence shall be displayed at a conspicuous place at the premises or venue of the bingo.

(2) Subject to Regulation 15, where a person is licensed to conduct bingo at multiple premises or venues, the person shall display his or her Business Licence and Gaming (Bingo) Licence at a conspicuous place at each of the premises or venues.

(3) An online bingo operator shall display his or her Business Licence and Gaming (Bingo) Licence on the social media group cover page for the online bingo.

## PART 3 — TYPES OF BINGO

#### 10 Bingo fundraiser

(1) A bingo fundraiser operator shall be licensed to operate a bingo fundraiser.

(2) The Secretary shall allocate the time slot for the conduct of a bingo fundraiser.

[subreg (2) subst SL 29 of 2026 reg 6, opn 10 July 2026]

(3) A bingo fundraiser operator shall conduct his or her bingo fundraiser in accordance with the time slot allocated by the Secretary.

[subreg (3) subst SL 29 of 2026 reg 6, opn 10 July 2026]

(4) A bingo fundraiser operator shall only conduct up to two sessions per week consisting of:

(a) one session of 5 games; and

(b) one session of 10 games.

[subreg (4) subst SL 29 of 2026 reg 6, opn 10 July 2026]

#### 11 Take-home bingo

(1) A take-home bingo operator shall be licensed to operate a take-home bingo.

(2) A take-home bingo operator shall be subject to payment of the licence fee prescribed for a bingo fundraiser operator in Schedule 3.

(3) A take-home bingo operator shall produce a copy of his or her valid Business Licence and Gaming (Bingo) Licence when:

(a) requested to do so by a bingo player; or

(b) required to do so by an authorised officer.

(4) A take-home bingo operator shall only conduct up to two sessions per week consisting of:

(a) one session of 5 games; and

(b) one session of 10 games.

[subreg (4) insrt SL 29 of 2026 reg 7, opn 10 July 2026]

#### 12 Bingo Market (Kashock)

(1) A bingo market operator shall be licensed to operate a bingo market.

(2) A bingo market shall have an unlimited number of bingo players.

(3) A bingo market operator shall comply with the conditions provided under the Act, these Regulations and as specified in the Gaming (Bingo) Licence.

(4) Subject to Section 13(5) and (6) of the Act, where a bingo market operator’s Gaming (Bingo) Licence is suspended or cancelled during the conduct of the bingo, the operator shall immediately cease the bingo and inform all the bingo players of the suspension or cancellation of the licence.

[reg 12 subst SL 29 of 2026 reg 8, opn 10 July 2026]

## PART 4 — CONDITIONS FOR BINGO OPERATIONS

#### 13 Days and time slots

A bingo operator shall conduct bingo only on the specified days and time slots as allocated by the Secretary.

[reg 13 am SL 29 of 2026 reg 9, opn 10 July 2026]

#### 14 Extension

(1) A bingo operator may only extend the time slots for a bingo market or online bingo upon approval by the Secretary of an extension application and payment of the fee provided under Schedule 3.

(2) An extension application shall be in Form 2 of Schedule 2.

(3) The Secretary’s approval of an extension application shall be in Form 3 of Schedule 2.

(4) The Secretary’s refusal of an extension application shall be in Form 4 of Schedule 2 and shall state the reasons for refusal.

(5) A bingo operator shall not conduct bingo on a Sunday, Christmas Day, Good Friday and any other statutory public holiday which the Secretary prohibits by notice published in the Gazette.

[reg 14 subst SL 29 of 2026 reg 10, opn 10 July 2026]

#### 15 Premises, venues or page

(1) A bingo market shall be conducted at the premises authorised by a Gaming

(Bingo) Licence that has been granted for the bingo market.

(2) Bingo, other than a bingo market, shall be conducted at the venue authorised by a Gaming (Bingo) Licence that has been granted for the bingo.

(3) Each premises and venue shall be charged separate licence fees.

(4) Each premises and venue shall display the Business Licence and Gaming

(Bingo) Licence for the bingo conducted at that particular premises and venue.

#### 16 Bingo caller to show proof

A bingo caller shall show proof to a bingo player:

(a) of all the numbers that have been called;

(b) that the winning numbers are included in the sequence of numbers that have been called; and

(c) that the winner of a bingo fundraiser or jackpot prize has a valid bingo booklet.

#### 17 Bingo runner to verify with a bingo caller in the presence of a

witness A bingo runner shall verify with the bingo caller in the presence of a witness that the:

(a) winner of a bingo fundraiser, take-home bingo or jack pot prize holds a valid bingo booklet; and

(b) winning numbers are included in the sequence of numbers that have been called.

#### 18 Bingo prizes may be monetary or legitimate items

(1) Bingo prizes may be monetary or legitimate items that shall be given to the winner of a particular bingo after proof of winning has been shown and validated.

(2) Monetary prizes shall be given to a winner of a particular bingo in the form of cash in hand transaction or through electronic bank transfer.

(3) Monetary prizes shall not exceed $20,000 for each bingo type notwithstanding whether the bingo is for a jackpot prize.

(4) Where the jackpot prize with the inclusion of the no taker prize reaches $20,000, it shall be played off at the very next bingo market or online bingo.

(5) A bingo fundraiser or take-home bingo shall not offer a jackpot prize.

(6) A bingo fundraiser operator shall pay out the prizes as advertised and specified in the licence application provided to the Secretary.

[subreg (6) insrt SL 29 of 2026 reg 11, opn 10 July 2026]

(7) A bingo fundraiser operator shall not change prizes after the first bingo player has purchased a booklet.

[subreg (7) insrt SL 29 of 2026 reg 11, opn 10 July 2026]

(8) A bingo fundraiser operator who contravenes subregulation (6) or (7):

(a) shall be issued with a Notice to Comply in the Form under Schedule 5; and

(b) such bingo fundraiser operator shall pay the winner the prize as advertised and provided in the licence application within 2 weeks from receiving of the Notice to Comply.

[subreg (8) insrt SL 29 of 2026 reg 11, opn 10 July 2026]

(9) Upon receipt of a Notice to Comply under subregulation (8), a bingo fundraiser operator shall:

(a) where he or she has paid the winner the correct prize under subregulation (8), provide proof of payment within 2 weeks of receiving the Notice to Comply to the Secretary; or

(b) dispute the Notice to Comply, by submitting a written explanation to the Secretary and proof, that the correct prize was paid in accordance with subregulation (6), within 2 weeks of receiving the Notice to Comply.

[subreg (9) insrt SL 29 of 2026 reg 11, opn 10 July 2026]

(10) A bingo fundraiser operator who fails to comply with subregulation (8) shall not be issued a bingo fundraiser licence for a period of 2 weeks from the expiry of the period under subregulation (8)(b).

[subreg (10) insrt SL 29 of 2026 reg 11, opn 10 July 2026]

#### 19 Other conditions

(1) There shall be a designated smoking area at the premises or venue where bingo is conducted. reg 17LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

(2) A bingo operator shall ensure that the vehicle of a bingo player does not obstruct:

(a) the movement of neighbouring residents;

(b) access to roads; and

(c) main road traffic.

(3) Alcohol shall not be permitted into, consumed or sold at the premises or venue.

(4) All premises or venues shall have toilet facilities.

(5) A child shall not be permitted to:

(a) enter and remain at the premises or venue;

(b) purchase a bingo booklet at the premises or venue; and

(c) play bingo at the premises or venue.

#### 20 Bingo operators

(1) A bingo operator may conduct activities relevant to bingo operations, including:

(a) selling bingo booklets;

(b) performing the functions and responsibilities of a bingo caller;

(c) performing the functions and responsibilities of a bingo runner;

(d) preparing and giving out prizes;

(e) assisting the elderly bingo players; and

(f) assisting bingo players with disabilities.

(2) Any bingo player who mistreats or acts in an offensive manner toward a bingo operator may be removed from the premises or venue.

(3) Where a person is removed from the premises or venue:

(a) his or her right to participate any further as a bingo player ceases; and

(b) he or she is not entitled to redeem or receive any prize eventhough a prize may be declared in his or her bingo booklet.

#### 21 Complaints

(1) A person may make a complaint to the Secretary where a provision of the Act, these Regulations or a condition of a Gaming (Bingo) Licence has been breached.

[subreg (1) subst SL 29 of 2026 reg 12, opn 10 July 2026]

(2) Where a bingo player has not received his or her prize from an unlicensed bingo operator, he or she shall not have any right to make a complaint as a consequence of his or her participation in an unlawful bingo.

#### 22 Application for payment plans

(1) A person may apply to the Secretary for a payment plan to be made between the person and the Secretary for the payment of the licence fee for a bingo market.

(2) The Secretary may approve the application for a payment plan and grant an Interim Gaming (Bingo) Licence to the person.

(3) Where the person fails to meet the payment deadlines according to the approved payment plan, the Secretary shall:

(a) revoke the Interim Gaming (Bingo) Licence; and

(b) give notice to the person of the decision and reason for the revocation. reg 22 GAMING (BINGO) REGULATIONS 2023

(4) Where the person pays the licence fee for a bingo market in full, the Secretary shall revoke the Interim Gaming (Bingo) Licence and grant the person a Gaming (Bingo) Licence. reg 22LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

## PART 5 — MISCELANEOUS

#### 23 Protection from liability

(1) The Department of Internal Affairs, the Secretary or any other staff or employee of the Department of Internal Affairs shall not be liable for any act done or omitted to be done in good faith in the performance of a function or exercise of a power under the Act, these Regulations or any other written law.

(2) Any person acting under the Act, these Regulations or any other written law, in relation to online bingo shall not be liable to any person participating in the online bingo.

#### 24 Transition

Where an application for a gaming licence for bingo is made in accordance with the Gaming Regulations 2011 and the application is not determined by the Secretary before the commencement of the Gaming (Bingo) Regulations 2023, the application shall be dealt with by the Secretary in accordance with the requirements of the Gaming Regulations 2011.

#### 25 Consequential amendments

The Gaming Regulations 2011 is amended by:

(a) in the table in Schedule 1, deleting the row for Item 1; and

(b) in the table in Schedule 2, deleting the fields and note in relation to the game of bingo in the sixth row.
