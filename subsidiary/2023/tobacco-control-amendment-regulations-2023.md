# Tobacco Control (Amendment) Regulations 2023

TOBACCO CONTROL (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2023 _____________________________ SL No. 27 of 2023 ______________________________ Notified: 26 September 2023 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 30 of the Tobacco Control Act 2009:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Tobacco Control (Amendment) Regulations 2023.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Tobacco Control Regulations 2009

These Regulations amend the Tobacco Control Regulations 2009.

#### 4 Regulation 6 amended

Regulation 6(1) is amended by deleting ‘For the purposes of Section 13 of the Act, the prescribed requirements for the no smoking signs to be displayed within a school, a hospital, an enclosed public place or an enclosed workplace are:’ and substituting ‘For the purposes of Section 14(2) of the Act, the prescribed requirements for a ‘no smoking’ sign are as follows:’.

#### 5 Part 6 inserted

Part 6 is inserted after Regulation 25 as follows:

‘PART 6 – LICENCES 26  Licence to import tobacco products

(1) For the purposes of Section 17A of the Act, a person shall not import tobacco products for a commercial purpose unless the person has obtained an importer licence in accordance with the Act and these Regulations.

(2) For the purposes of Section 17F of the Act, an application for an importer licence shall be made in the form as set out in Form 1 of Schedule 6.

(3) The fee for an application for an importer licence shall be as set out in Schedule 7. 27 Licence to sell or distribute tobacco products to retailers

(1) For the purposes of Section 17A of the Act, a person shall not sell or distribute tobacco products to retailers unless the person has obtained a distributor licence in accordance with the Act and these Regulations.

(2) For the purposes of Section 17F of the Act, an application for a distributor licence shall be made in the form as set out in Form 2 of Schedule 6.

(3) The fee for an application for a distributor licence shall be as set out in Schedule 7. 28 Licence to sell tobacco products to consumers

(1) For the purposes of Section 17A of the Act, a person shall not sell tobacco products to consumers unless the person has obtained a retail licence in accordance with the Act and these Regulations.

(2) For the purposes of Section 17F of the Act, an application for a retail licence shall be made in the form as set out in Form 3 of Schedule 6.

(3) The fee for an application for a distributor licence shall be as set out in Schedule 7. 29 Renewal of licence

(1) An application for the renewal of an importer licence, a distributor licence or a retail licence shall be made in the application form for an importer, distributor or retail licence, as the case may be, as set out in Schedule 6.

(2) The fee for an application for the renewal of an importer licence, a distributor licence or a retail licence shall be the same as the fee for the application for an importer, distributor or retail licence, as the case may be, as set out in Schedule 7. 30 Transfer or assignment of licence

(1) For the purposes of Section 17K of the Act, an application for the transfer or assignment of a licence shall be in the form as set out in Form 4 of Schedule 6.

(2) The fee for an application for the transfer or assignment of a licence shall be as set out in Schedule 7. 31 Decision of the Secretary

(1) Where the Secretary grants an importer licence, distributor licence or retail licence to an applicant, the licence shall be issued in the form as set out in Form 5 of Schedule 6.

(2) Where the Secretary refuses to grant an importer licence, distributor licence or retail licence to an applicant, he or she shall:

(a) inform the applicant of the refusal in writing in the form as set out in Form 6 of Schedule 6; and

(b) provide the reason for the refusal.’
