# Proceeds of Crime (Border Declaration Form) Regulations 2023

Proceeds of Crime (Border Declaration Form) Regulations 2023

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Proceeds of Crime (Border Declaration Form) Regulations 2023.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Border Declaration Form

(1) A person transporting into or out of the Republic, in the value of more than $5,000 of or its equivalence in any other currency:

(a) cash;

(b) bearer negotiable instrument; or

(c) precious metals or precious stones, shall make a declaration in the Form in the Schedule.

[subreg (1) renum and am SL 5 of 2024 reg 4, opn 28 Mar 2024]

(2) The Naoero Customs Service shall provide a copy each of the Border Declaration Form to the Financial Intelligence Unit.

[subreg (2) insrt SL 5 of 2024 reg 4, opn 28 Mar 2024; am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 4 Record keeping

(1) A declaration made in the Form in the Schedule, shall be kept and maintained for a period of 7 years by the:

(a) Naoero Customs Service; and

(b) Financial Intelligence Unit.

[subreg (1) renum and am SL 5 of 2024 reg 5, opn 28 Mar 2024; am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

(2) An electronic declaration made for the purposes of this Regulation or any other written law, shall be kept and maintained in an electronic storage database or system.

[subreg (2) insrt SL 5 of 2024 reg 5, opn 28 Mar 2024]

#### 5 Declaration of transportation of cash and other items by mail

or cargo

(1) Where cash, bearer negotiable instruments, precious metals or stones including sapphire, gold and silver are transported from or into the Republic through mail or cargo, the manifest for such transportation shall be appropriately recorded the following in the Customs system:

(a) amount in Australian dollars or its equivalent in foreign currency; and

(b) value of any of the precious sapphire, gold and silver in Australian dollars or its equivalent in foreign currency.

(2) The Naoero Customs Service shall report the transportation of cash, bearer negotiable instruments, precious metals or stones including sapphire, gold and silver under subregulation (1) to the Financial Intelligence Unit.

[subreg (2) am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

(3) The Financial Intelligence Unit shall ensure to keep and maintain all reports received under subregulation (2), on a monthly basis, for a period of 7 years.

(4) For the purpose of this Regulation ‘Customs system’ has the meaning given to it under the Customs Regulations 2023.
