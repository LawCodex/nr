# Plant and Animal Quarantine (Amendment) Regulations 2023

PLANT AND ANIMAL QUARANTINE (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2023 _____________________________ SL No. 7 of 2023 ______________________________ Notified: 28 February 2023 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 38 of the Agricultural Quarantine Act 1999:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Plant and Animal Quarantine (Amendment) Regulations 2023.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Plant and Animal Quarantine Regulations 2004

These Regulations amend the Plant and Animal Quarantine Regulations 2004.
