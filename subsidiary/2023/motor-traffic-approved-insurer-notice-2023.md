# Motor Traffic (Approved Insurer) Notice 2023

Motor Traffic (Approved Insurer) Notice 2023

## Table of Amendments

The Motor Traffic (Approved Insurer) Notice 2023 SL 13 was notified and commenced on 14 June 2023 (GN No 629/2023; Gaz 108/2023).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement I, Honourable Martin Hunt, MP, Minister responsible for Transport, acting pursuant to Section 5 of the Motor Traffıc Act 2014 (‘Act’) give notice that the Department of Transport is the approved insurer.

The Motor Traffıc (Approved Insurer) Notice 2014 is repealed.

This Notice commences on the date of its publication in the Gazette.
