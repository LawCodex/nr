# Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters Regulations 2023

## Table of Amendments

The Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters Regulations 2023 SL 35 were notified and commenced on 7 October 2023 (GN No 1110/2023; Gaz 218/2023).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026 The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 62 of the Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters Act 2004:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters Regulations 2023.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Interpretation

‘Minister’ means the Minister for Justice; and

‘Secretary’ means the Secretary for Justice.

#### 4 Request by Republic or assistance generally

Where the Republic needs any international assistance in a criminal matter, the Minister shall under Section 6, make such request in Form 1.

#### 5 Request by foreign countries for general assistance

Where a foreign country requests international assistance in a criminal matter, the country shall under Section 7, make such request to the Minister, in Form 2.

#### 6 Request by the Republic for assistance in evidence or

production of documents or articles

(1) A request by the Republic for taking any evidence in a criminal matter in a foreign country under the law of that country, shall be made by the Minister under Section 10(1)(a), in Form 3.

(2) A request by the Republic for the production of any documents or articles in a foreign country under the law of that country, shall be made by the Minister under Section 10(1)(b) in Form 4.

#### 7 Request by the foreign country for assistance with evidence

(1) A request by a foreign country for taking evidence in the Republic shall be made under Section 11(1) to the Minister, in Form 5.

(2) Where the Minister accepts the request of the foreign country, the Minister shall under Section 11(1), authorise the taking of evidence and transmission of the evidence to the requesting foreign country in Form 6.

#### 8 Production of documents or articles

(1) A request by a foreign country for the production of any documents or articles in the Republic shall be made under Section 11(2) to the Minister, in Form 7.

(2) Where the Minister accepts the request of the foreign country, the Minister shall under Section 11(2) authorise the taking of evidence and transmission of the evidence to the requesting foreign country in Form 8.

#### 9 Request by the Republic for search and seizure in a foreign

country With regard to a request by the Republic for search and seizure under Section 17 of the Act in a criminal matter against the laws of the Republic in a foreign country, the Minister may request the authority in the foreign country to obtain a warrant or any other instrument for the search and seizure in Form 9.

#### 10 Request by foreign country for search and seizure

(1) A request by a foreign country for search and seizure under Section 18(1)(c), shall be made to the Minister in Form 10.

(2) Where the Minister accepts the request, he or she shall under Section 18(1) issue a direction to a police officer or any other authorised officer in the Republic to apply to the court for a warrant in Form 11.

#### 11 Request for enforcement orders made in the Republic

A request by the Republic for an order made in the Republic for enforcement in a foreign country shall be made under Section 36 by the Minister, in Form 12.

#### 12 Request for issue of orders in foreign countries

A request for an order under Section 37 in relation to an instrument issued under the Proceeds of Crime 2004 shall be made by the Minister, in Form 13.

#### 13 Request for registration and enforcement of foreign orders

Where a foreign country requests the Minister to make arrangements for the registration and enforcement of a foreign order under Section 38, the Minister may apply for an order in court in Form 14.

#### 14 Cancellation of registration of order

Where the Minister requests a cancellation of registration of a foreign pecuniary penalty order or a foreign restraining order, he or she shall apply under Section 43 to the court, in Form 15.

#### 15 Request for search and seizure or warrant for tainted property

The Minister may direct a police officer, authorised officer or any other law enforcement officer under the Proceeds of Crimes Act 2004, to apply to the court for a search warrant under Section 44, in Form 16.

#### 16 Minister requesting restraining orders

(1) The Minister may under Section 45, apply to the court, for a restraining order under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2004 in Form 17 or any other procedure permitted under the Criminal Procedure Act 1972.

(2) Where appropriate, the Minister may for the purposes of Section 45 require the Secretary to apply to the court for a restraining order by way of civil proceedings under the Civil Procedure Act 1972. reg 8LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

#### 17 Minister may direct application for production order

The Minister may under Section 46, direct a police officer to apply to the court under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2004 for the purposes of an offence to obtain:

(a) search warrant for the property tracking-document, in Form 18;

(b) production order for the production of the property-tracking document, in Form 19; or

(c) both, a search warrant and production order.

#### 18 Forms or the order made by court

(1) Where an application or order of the court is required, the application or the order shall be made in Form 20 or where forms are available under the Criminal Procedure Act 1972, Civil Procedure Act 1972 or any other written law.

(2) The strict compliance of the form of the application or an order shall not invalidate any application or order, provided the substance contained in the application or order is capable of being understood and clear.

#### 19 Register of requests

The Secretary shall be responsible for establishing and maintaining a Register of requests:

(a) of mutual assistance in criminal matters made by the Republic to a foreign country in Form 21; and

(b) for mutual assistance in criminal matters received by the Republic from a foreign country in Form 22.

#### 20 Conviction as a pre-requisite for any request or order

(1) For any request, application or order, unless the Act or any other written law expressly requires a conviction of a person, any person authorised or the court may accede to such application, request or order without the conviction of that person.

(2) Where a request is made to the Minister for the purposes of furthering any investigation or inquiry for that offence, it is deemed that there is no conviction for that particular offence.

#### 21 Urgent applications

(1) Where a request is made urgently, the Minister, court or any other person authorised under the Act or written law may direct any person or authority to summarily deal with the substance of the applications be attended to without the requirement to follow all the intricate procedures provided in this Act or the written law.

(2) Where the summary procedure in subregulation (1) is applied, any person carrying out any statutory responsibilities under the Act or other written law shall ensure that:

(a) the person affected is given the right to be heard at a later stage; and

(b) any properties seized or restrained are properly accounted for by way of an inventory and preserved until such time the Minister, court or any authorised person is able to consider the application on merits. reg 21 MUTUAL ASSISTANCE IN CRIMINAL MATTERS REGULATIONS 2023

(3) Subject to subregulation (2), an action taken for the purposes of this Regulation shall not invalidate any decisions, orders or directions made or given, which may not have been in strict compliance with the Act or any other written law.

(4) The ordinary cooperation amongst the relevant authorities of different countries for the purposes of any investigation, inquiry, collating of evidence or other incidental matters shall continue in urgent or appropriate circumstances of an investigation or inquiry shall supplement the procedures and requirements to ensure the intended purpose of the Act or other written laws is fulfilled.

#### 22 Application of Foreign Judgments (Reciprocal Enforcement)

Act 1973 The Foreign Judgments (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1973 applies mutatis mutandis to the process under the Act and these Regulations.

#### 23 Forms and procedure

(1) Where there are no specific forms or procedures prescribed, any proceeding so filed under these Regulations shall be conducted in accordance with existing written laws, including Rules of the court or Practice Directions issued by the Chief Justice.

(2) An appeal from one hierarchy of a court to another shall be instituted and heard in the same manner as is undertaken in other causes or matters as prescribed in the District Court Act 2018, Supreme Court Act 2018 and the Naoero Court of Appeal Act 2018 respectively.

(3) The enforcement of an order shall be undertaken in accordance with existing procedures under any written law including, the Administration of Justice Act 2018. reg 21LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO
