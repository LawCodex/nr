# Motor Traffic (Amendment) Regulations 2020

MOTOR TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2020 ______________________________ SL No. 18 of 2020 ______________________________ Notified: 17th July 2020 Table of Contents Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 132 of the Motor Traffic Act 2014:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Traffic (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations come into effect on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of Motor Traffic Regulations 2014

These Regulations amends the Motor Traffic Regulations 2014.

#### 4 Amendment of Regulation 6

Regulation 6(1) is amended by inserting new a paragraph (q) immediately after paragraph (p) as follows:

‘ (q)   for vehicles of the Naoero Postal Services Corporation - the letters ‘POST’ followed by two numerals in sequential order in a series commencing with

“POST 10” and ending with “POST 99” in black on a white background.’
