# Customs Tariff (Alteration to Tariff) Regulations 2020

CUSTOMS TARIFF (ALTERATION TO TARIFF) REGULATIONS 2020 ______________________________ SL No. 16 of 2020 ______________________________ Notified: 30th June 2020 Table of Contents Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 12 of the Customs Tariff Act 2014:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Customs Tariff (Alteration to Tariff) Regulations 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day they are notified in the Gazette.
