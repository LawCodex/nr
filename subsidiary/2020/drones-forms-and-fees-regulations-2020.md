# Drones (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2020

Drones (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2020

## Table of Amendments

The Drones (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2020 SL 28 was notified and commenced on 18 September 2020 (GN No 710/2020; Gaz 177/2020).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Revised Written Laws Act 2021 No 7 1 June 2021 1 June 2021 The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 25 of the Drones Act 2018:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Drones (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations shall commence on the day they are notified in the Gazette and came into effect on 18 September 2020.

#### 3 Application for permit to import drone

For the purpose of Section 8 of the Act, an application for a permit to import drone shall be in Form 1 of Schedule 1.

#### 4 Application for licence to sell drones

For the purpose of Section 10 of the Act, an application for a licence to sell drones shall be in Form 2 of Schedule 1.

#### 5 Application for permit to use drones

For the purpose of Section 13(1)(a) of the Act, an application for a permit to use drones shall be in Form 3 of Schedule 1.

#### 6 Permit to import drones

A permit to import drones shall be in Form 4 of Schedule 1.

#### 7 Licence to sell drones

A licence to sell drones shall be in Form 5 of Schedule 1.

#### 8 Permit to use drones

A permit to use drones shall be in Form 6 of Schedule 1.

#### 9 Permit or licence not granted to minors

A permit or licence shall not be granted to a person under the age of 18 years.

#### 10 Fees

The fees to be paid for the purposes of the Act and Regulations are set out in Schedule 2.
