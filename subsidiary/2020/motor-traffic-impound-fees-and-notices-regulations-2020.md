# Motor Traffic (Impound Fees and Notices) Regulations 2020

Motor Traffic (Impound Fees and Notices) Regulations 2020

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Traffıc (Impound Fees and Notices) Regulations 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations come into effect on the date of notification in the Gazette.

#### 3 Definition

In these Regulations:

‘Act’ means the Motor Traffıc Act 2014; and

[def am SL 18 of 2024 reg 4, opn 14 June 2024]

‘mechanic’ means a mechanic employed in the Government of Naoero Garage or any such other qualified person authorised by the Commissioner of Police to appraise the condition and value of a motor vehicle.

[def insrt SL 18 of 2024 reg 4, opn 14 June 2024; am Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 4 Impound fee

(1) For the purpose of Section 116 of the Act:

(a) the impound fee shall be imposed and calculated on a weekly basis in accordance with Schedule 1; and

(b) the impound fee shall be paid by the owner of the impounded vehicle at the Naoero Revenue Office and a receipt shall be produced to the Naoero Police Force before the impounded vehicle is released.

[subreg (1) am SL 18 of 2024 reg 5, opn 14 June 2024; Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

(2) The impound fee in Schedule 1 shall not include any other costs under the Act.

(3) The impound fee shall become payable immediately upon expiry of 24 hours from the time the vehicle is impounded by the Naoero Police Force.

[subreg (3) am SL 18 of 2024 reg 5, opn 14 June 2024; Act 21 of 2026 s 5, opn 12 May 2026]

#### 5 Notice to seize and remove motor vehicle or trailer to impound

facility

(1) For the purpose of Section 115 of the Act, where a motor vehicle or trailer is seized by a police officer:

(a) a notice to seize and remove the vehicle shall be issued immediately to the owner of the vehicle by the police officer; and

(b) the vehicle shall be removed to an impound facility by the police officer.

(2) For the purpose of subregulation (1)(a), the police officer shall issue the notice in the form set out in Schedule 2.

#### 6 Notice for payment of impound fee

(1) For the purpose of Section 116(3)(a) of the Act, a police officer authorised by the Commissioner of Police shall issue a notice for payment of an impound fee, after 30 days, in the form in Schedule 3.

(2) For the purpose of Section 116(3)(b) of the Act, a police officer authorised by the Commissioner of Police shall issue a notice for payment of an impound fee, after 45 days, in the form in Schedule 4.

[reg 6 subst SL 18 of 2024 reg 6, opn 14 June 2024]

#### 7 Non-payment of impound fee

(1) A motor vehicle owner issued a notice of payment under Regulation 6(2), who does not pay the impound fee, shall be liable to have his or her motor vehicle disposed of by auction or public tender.

(2) For the purpose of Section 116(3)(a) and (b) of the Act, a police officer authorised by the Commissioner of Police shall before disposing a vehicle by auction or public tender, issue a notice to the owner in the form in Schedule 4.

[reg 7 insrt SL 18 of 2024 reg 7, opn 14 June 2024]

#### 8 Conduct of auction or public tender

(1) For the purpose of carrying out an auction or public tender of a motor vehicle under Section 116(3)(b) of the Act, the Commissioner of Police, shall ensure that:

(a) a certificate of appraisement is obtained from the mechanic containing the value of the motor vehicle;

(b) arrange for the auction or public tender through advertisement to the general public;

(c) the motor vehicle is sold at the best price reasonably obtainable at the time of the sale;

(d) immediately upon the completion of the sale, pay the proceeds for the disposed motor vehicle into the Treasury Fund;

(e) a notice of the sale, in the Form in Schedule 5, is issued to the Registrar to enable registration of change of ownership of the motor vehicle;

(f) a report is provided to the Minister on the sale of the motor vehicle by auction which includes:

(i) value of the motor vehicle as appraised by the mechanic;

(ii) actual sale price of the motor vehicle; and

(iii) account of the defraying of the sale of the motor vehicle in accordance with paragraph (g);

(g) the monies obtained through the sale of vehicle by an auction or advertisement shall be, defrayed on the authorisation of the Commissioner of Police as follows:

(i) impounding fees;

(ii) fines or penalties;

(iii) arrears of any motor vehicle registration fees payable under the Act;

(iv) expenses for the auction or public tender; and

(v) any balance remaining to be paid to the owner of the motor vehicle.

(2) A police officer shall not be eligible to take part in the auction or public reg 6LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO tender of a motor vehicle and any bid belonging to a police officer, shall not be considered in the auction or public tender process.

(3) Upon the completion of the disposal and defraying of any proceeds from the sale of the impounded vehicle, the Commissioner of Police shall inform the owner of the sale of the impounded vehicle and the and the manner in which the proceeds were defrayed.
