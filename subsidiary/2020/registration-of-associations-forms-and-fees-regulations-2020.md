# Registration of Associations (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2020

Registration of Associations (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2020

## Table of Amendments

The Registration of Associations (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2020 SL 33 were notified and commenced on 10 November 2020.

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Revised Written Laws Act 2021 No 7 1 June 2021 1 June 2021 Anti-Money Laundering and Targeted Financial Sanctions Act 2023 No 2 7 June 2023 s 140: 7 June 2023 Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026 The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 52 of the Registration of Associations Act 2020:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Associations (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Register of Associations

For the purposes of Section 11(2) of the Act, the Register of Associations may be kept as set out in Schedule 1:

(a) in writing; and

(b) in digital or electronic form.

#### 4 Application for registration of association

(1) For the purposes of Section 13 of the Act, an application form for registration of an association shall be as set out in Form 1 of Schedule 2.

(2) The person nominated or authorised to register the association or in case of renewals, the person authorised to apply for renewal shall:

(a) be duly nominated or authorised by the executive body of the association; and

(b) such person shall duly complete the declaration of such appointment in the form.

(3) An association shall also complete the declaration that the proposed association or in case of an existing association, its activities:

(a) are eligible for registration under Section 5 of the Act;

(b) of the association does not contravene Section 6 of the Act; and

(c) are not for the purposes associated for any pecuniary gain for its members as required by Section 7 of the Act.

#### 5 Certificate of registration of association

For the purposes of Sections 14(3) and 16 of the Act, a certificate of registration of an association shall be as set out in Form 2 of Schedule 2.

#### 6 Application for duplicate copy of certificate of registration

For the purposes of Section 19 of the Act, an application form for a duplicate copy of a certificate of registration shall be as set out in Form 3 of Schedule 2.

#### 7 Renewal of registration of association

For the purposes of Section 20 of the Act, an application form for a renewal of a registration of an association shall be as set out in Form 4 of Schedule 2.

#### 8 Surrender of certificate

(1) For the purposes of Section 21 of the Act, a notice to surrender a certificate of registration shall be as set out in Form 5 of Schedule 2.

(2) Where the association surrenders a certificate of registration, its members shall:

(a) provide a resolution of the association for the surrender of the certificate of registration;

(b) provide the reasons for surrender of the certificate of registration; and

(c) require the person surrendering the certificate of registration to make a declaration that the association has or will cease all its activities.

#### 9 Notice of intention to cancel certificate

For the purposes of Sections 22 and 23 of the Act, a notice of intention to cancel a certificate of registration shall be as set out in Form 6 of Schedule 2.

#### 10 Notice of cancelled certificate

For the purposes of Section 24 of the Act, a notice of cancelled certificate shall be as set out in Form 7 of Schedule 2.

#### 11 Notice to suspend operations of association

For the purposes of Section 25 of the Act, a notice to suspend the operations of an association shall be as set out in Form 8 of Schedule 2.

#### 12 Notice to vary or amend registration

For the purposes of Section 26 of the Act, a notice to vary or amend the registration shall be as set out in Form 9 of Schedule 2.

#### 13 Notice of voluntary dissolution of association

For the purposes of Section 27 of the Act, a notice of voluntary dissolution of an association shall be as set out in Form 10 of Schedule 2.

#### 14 Application for certificate of compliance

For the purposes of Section 30(1) of the Act, an application form for a certificate of compliance shall be as set out in Form 11 of Schedule 2.

#### 15 Certificate of compliance

For the purposes of Section 30(3) of the Act, a certificate of compliance shall be as set out in Form 12 of Schedule 2.

#### 16 Notice to association to show cause

For the purposes of Section 33(3) of the Act, a notice to an association to show cause shall be as set out in Form 13 of Schedule 2. reg 8LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAOERO

#### 17 Notice to executive to provide information or documents or

both For the purposes of Section 35 of the Act, a notice to the executive of an association to provide information or documents or both shall be as set out in Form 14 of Schedule 2.

#### 18 Fees

The prescribed fees to be paid for the purposes of the Act and these Regulations are set out in Schedule 3. reg 18 REGISTRATION OF ASSOCIATIONS (FORMS AND FEES) REGS 2020
