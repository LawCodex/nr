# Motor Traffic (Traffic Infringement Notices) (Amendment) No 2 Regulations 2020

MOTOR TRAFFIC (TRAFFIC INFRINGEMENT NOTICES) (AMENDMENT) NO. 2 REGULATIONS 2020 ______________________________ SL No. 30 of 2020 ______________________________ Notified: 22nd September 2020 Table of Contents Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 117 of the Motor Traffic Act 2014:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Traffic (Traffic Infringement Notices) (Amendment) No. 2 Regulations 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations come into effect on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of Motor Traffic (Traffic Infringement Notices)

Regulations 2018 The Motor Traffic (Traffic Infringement Notices) Regulations 2018 are amended by the provisions of these Regulations.

#### 4 Amendment to Regulation 6

Regulation 6 is deleted and substituted as follows:

#### 6 Prescribed Traffic Infringement Notice

For the purposes of Section 117(2)(c) of the Act, the Traffic Infringement Notice which a Police officer may serve to an offender shall be in:

(a) the prescribed form in Schedule 2; or

(b) the prescribed form in Schedule 2A.
