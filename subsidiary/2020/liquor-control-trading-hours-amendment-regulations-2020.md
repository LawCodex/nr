# Liquor Control (Trading Hours) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

LIQUOR CONTROL (TRADING HOURS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2020 ______________________________ SL No. 6 of 2020 ______________________________ Notified: 27th March 2020 Table of Contents Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 96 of the Liquor Control Act 2017:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Liquor Control (Trading Hours) (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of Liquor Control (Trading Hours) Regulations 2020

The Liquor Control (Trading Hours) Regulations 2020 is amended by these Regulations.

#### 4 Amendment of Regulation 3

Regulation 3(1) is repealed and replaced as follows:

(1) The trading hours for the sale and supply of liquor under an off licence shall be from 10am and 6pm from Monday to Saturday.
