# Passports (Forms) Regulations 2020

## Table of Amendments

The Passports (Forms) Regulations 2020 SL 24 were notified and commenced on 20 August 2020 (GN No 619/2020; Gaz 163/2020).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 57 of the Passports Act 2011:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Passports (Forms) Regulations 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations come into effect on the date of notification in the Gazette.

#### 3 Application for ordinary Nauruan passport

(1) For the purpose of Section 7 of the Act, an application form for an ordinary Nauruan passport shall be in:

(a) Form 1 of Schedule 1, if a Nauruan citizen by birth or descent; and

(b) Form 2 of Schedule 1, if a naturalised citizen.

(2) An application under subregulation (1) shall be accompanied by the following:

(a) 2 recent photographs, one of which is to be certified:

(i) by an authorised officer of the Passport Division in case of personal lodgement of application; and

(ii) in any other case, by a Commissioner for Oaths;

(b) a birth certificate issued by the Registrar of Births, Deaths and Marriages for a Nauruan citizen born in the Republic;

(c) a birth certificate from a foreign country of birth in case of a naturalised citizen;

(d) a certificate of registration of birth from the Registrar of Births, Deaths and Marriages of a Nauruan citizen born in a foreign country or a birth certificate from the foreign country of birth where the birth is not registered in the Republic;

(e) marriage certificate;

(f) an order of a court in case where the applicant is divorced and has not remarried;

(g) death certificate of spouse in case of a widow;

(h) a certificate of identity with photograph;

(i) certificate of Nauruan citizenship in case of naturalised citizen;

(j) in case of renewal of a passport, the biodata page of the expired or current passport and any other documents which the Secretary may require; and

(k) such other information which the Secretary may deem necessary.

#### 4 Details in Nauruan passports

A Nauruan passport shall include the following details:

(a) photograph;

(b) secondary photograph showing as watermark;

(c) passport type;

(d) the Republic’s issuing code of ‘NRU’; and

(e) passport serial number:

(i) for a Presidential passport – the letters ‘PN’ followed by 6 numerals;

(ii) for a diplomatic passport – the letters ‘DN’ followed by 6 numerals;

(iii) for an official passport – the letters ‘ON’ followed by 6 numerals; and

(iv) for an ordinary Nauruan passport – the letters ‘NN’ followed by 6 numerals;

(f) surname;

(g) given names;

(h) nationality;

(i) date of birth;

(g) gender;

(k) place of birth;

(l) date of issue;

(m) date of expiry;

(n) issuing authority; and

(o) signature of holder.

#### 5 Application for refugee travel document

(1) For the purposes of Section 12 of the Act, an application form for a refugee travel document shall be in Form 3 of Schedule 1.

(2) An application under subregulation (1) shall be accompanied by the following:

(a) 2 recent photographs, one of which to be certified by a Commissioner for Oaths;

(b) Refugee Determination Record;

(c) Refugee Identification Card;

(d) birth certificate, if available;

(e) marriage certificate, if available;

(f) death certificate of spouse in case of a widow, if applicable; and

(g) such other information which the Secretary may deem necessary.

#### 6 Application for certificate of identity

(1) For the purposes of Section 12 of the Act, an application form for a certificate of identity shall be in:

(a) Form 4 of Schedule 1 for:

(i) a lost or stolen passport by a Nauruan citizen in a foreign country;

(ii) a foreigner overstaying in the Republic;

(iii) a child born in the Republic to parents who are not Nauruan citizens; and

(iv) an asylum seeker; or

(b) Form 5 of Schedule 1, for a Nauruan child born in a foreign country.

(2) An application under subregulation (1) shall be accompanied by the following:

(a) 2 recent photographs, one of which to be certified:

(i) by an authorised officer of the Passport Division in case of personal lodgement of application; and

(ii) in any other case, by a Commissioner for Oaths;

(b) a certificate of identity with photograph;

(c) birth certificate;

(d) marriage certificate, if applicable;

(e) death certificate of spouse in case of a widow, if applicable; reg 4LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

(f) final destination for travel; and

(g) any such other information which the Secretary may deem necessary.

(3) Where a certificate of identity is required by a Nauruan citizen due to the loss or theft of passport whilst in a foreign country, the Secretary may issue a certificate of identity from the records kept in his or her custody, power or control.

(4) Where a certificate of identity is required for the travel of a child born of Nauruan descent in a foreign country, the Secretary may issue a certificate of identity for the child based on:

(a) a certificate issued by a Nauruan embassy or consulate based in that foreign country certifying the child;

(b) in case where there is no Nauruan embassy or consulate, from the records of the biological mother of the child kept in the custody, power and control of the Secretary and a record of the hospital or medical facility where the child was born; and

(c) such other requirements that the Secretary may deem necessary.

#### 7 Refugee travel document

For the purposes of Section 13 of the Act, a refugee travel document shall include the following details:

(a) photograph;

(b) surname;

(c) given names;

(d) gender;

(e) nationality;

(f) date of birth;

(g) document number – the letters ‘RS’ followed by 6 numerals;

(h) date of issue;

(i) date of expiry;

(j) signature of holder; and

(k) issuing authority.

#### 8 Certificate of identity

(1) For the purposes of Section 14 of the Act, a certificate of identity shall be in:

(a) Form 6 of Schedule 1 for:

(i) lost or stolen passport of a Nauruan citizen in a foreign country;

(ii) foreigner overstaying in the Republic;

(iii) child born in the Republic to parents who are not Nauruan citizens; and

(iv) for a Nauruan child born in a foreign country;

(b) Form 7 of Schedule 1 for an asylum seeker.

(2) For the purposes of subregulation (1), a certificate of identity shall include the following details:

(a) photograph;

(b) surname;

(c) given names;

(d) gender;

(e) nationality of origin; reg 8 PASSPORTS (FORMS) REGULATIONS 2020

(f) date of birth;

(g) country of birth;

(h) signature of holder;

(i) final destination for travel; and

(j) issuing authority.

(3) The certificate of identity shall be valid for 12 months from the date of issue or earlier travel to the final destination.

#### 9 Cancellation of Nauruan travel document

For the purposes of Section 24 of the Act, the notice cancelling a Nauruan travel document shall be in Form 8 of Schedule 1.

#### 10 Notice for lost or stolen travel document

For the purposes of Section 33 of the Act, a notice for a lost or stolen travel document shall be in Form 9 of Schedule 1.

#### 11 Diplomatic passport

An application for a diplomatic passport shall be accompanied by the following:

(a) approval by the Minister for office holders under Sections 8(2)(a),(b),(c),

(d) and (e) of the Act;

(b) a supporting letter from the Department of Foreign Affairs for office holders under Sections 8(2)(c) and (d) of the Act; or

(c) a decision of the Cabinet for a person under Section 8(2)(f) of the Act.

#### 12 Official passport

An application for an official passport shall be accompanied by the following:

(a) a request by the relevant Head of Department; and

(b) approval by the Minister.

#### 13 Passport photograph requirements

A passport photograph submitted under these Regulations shall have the following specifications:

(a) be 4.5 centimetres x 3.5 centimetres;

(b) have adequate brightness and contrast;

(c) have natural skin tone;

(d) have a close up of the head, ears and partial shoulders;

(e) straight looking, centred with neutral expression;

(f) the face shall be in sharp focus and clear with no ink marks, creases or lines;

(g) the face, from forehead edge to bottom of chin, shall be seventy to eighty percent of the photograph;

(h) the eyes shall be open and no hair obstructing the face;

(i) prescription glasses, if worn, shall be clear and thin framed and should not have flash reflection or obscure the eyes;

(j) head coverings, hair, head-dress or facial ornaments shall not obscure the face;

(k) the photograph shall have plain light background;

(l) there shall be no other person or object in the photograph; reg 8LAWS OF THE REPUBLIC OF NAURU

(m) the lighting shall be uniform with no shadows on the face or behind;

(n) photograph shall not have red eyes;

(o) photograph with adequate digital resolution; and

(p) shall be no more than 3 months old.

#### 14 Register of travel documents

For the purposes of Section 56 of the Act, the Register may be kept as set out in Schedule 2:

(a) in writing; and

(b) electronic form that is readily available. reg 14 PASSPORTS (FORMS) REGULATIONS 2020
