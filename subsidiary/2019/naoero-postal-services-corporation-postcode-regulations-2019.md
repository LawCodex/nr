# Naoero Postal Services Corporation (Postcode) Regulations 2019

Naoero Postal Services Corporation (Postcode) Regulations 2019

## Table of Amendments

The Naoero Postal Services Corporation (Postcode) Regulations 2019 SL 5 was notified and commenced on 19 February 2019 (GN No 526/2019; Gaz 30/2019).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement Revised Written Laws Act 2021 No 7 1 June 2021 1 June 2021 Constitution (Amendment) (No 2) Act 2026 No 21 12 May 2026 s 5: 12 May 2026 The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 45 of the Naoero Postal Services Corporation Act 2018:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Naoero Postal Services Corporation (Postcode) Regulations 2019.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations come into effect on the date of notification in the Gazette.

#### 3 Definition

In these Regulations:

‘postcode’ means a series of numbers or letters or combination of both which are added to a postal address as the universally applicable postcode for the Republic.

#### 4 Designation of postcode

(1) There shall be established a national postcode for the Republic.

(2) The postcode in subregulation (1) shall be ‘NRU68’.

#### 5 Postcode

The Corporation shall:

(a) enlist the postcode with the International Bureau Addressing Unit of the Universal Postal Union; and

(b) deliver or receive all such postal articles which bear the postcode in the ordinary course of business.

#### 6 Requirement for correct postal address

For the purposes of these Regulations, the correct postal address shall be in the form contained in the Schedule.
