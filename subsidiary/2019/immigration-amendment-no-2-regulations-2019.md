# Immigration (Amendment) No 2 Regulations 2019

IMMIGRATION (AMENDMENT) NO. 2 REGULATIONS 2019 ______________________________ SL No.  30 of 2019 ______________________________ Notified: 15th October 2019 Table of Contents Cabinet makes the following Regulations under section 33 of the Immigration Act 2014:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration (Amendment) No. 2 Regulations 2019.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations come into effect on the date of notification in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of Immigration Regulations 2014

The Immigration Regulations 2014 are amended by these Regulations.

#### 4 Amendment of Schedule 3

Schedule 3 is amended by inserting in alphabetical order ‘Thailand, Kingdom of’ immediately after ‘Solomon Islands’ as follows:
