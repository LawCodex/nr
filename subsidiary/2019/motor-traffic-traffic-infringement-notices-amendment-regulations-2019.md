# Motor Traffic (Traffic Infringement Notices) (Amendment) Regulations 2019

MOTOR TRAFFIC (TRAFFIC INFRINGEMENT NOTICES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2019 ______________________________ SL No. 31 of 2019 ______________________________ Notified: 24th October 2019 Table of Contents Cabinet makes the following Regulations under section 117 of the Motor Traffic Act 2014:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Traffic (Traffic Infringement Notices) (Amendment) Regulations 2019.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations come into effect on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of Motor Traffic (Traffic Infringement Notices)

Regulations 2018 The Motor Traffic (Traffic Infringement Notices) Regulations 2018 are amended by these Regulations.

#### 4 Amendment of Schedule 1

Schedule 1 is deleted and substituted.
