# Births Deaths and Marriages Registration (Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 2019

BIRTHS DEATHS AND MARRIAGES REGISTRATION

(FORMS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2019 ______________________________ SL No. 25 of 2019 ______________________________ Notified: 24th August 2019 Table of Contents Cabinet makes the following Regulations under section 102(2)(b) of the Births Deaths and Marriages Registration Act 2017:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Births Deaths and Marriages Registration

(Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 2019.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations come into effect on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of Births Deaths and Marriages Registration (Forms)

Regulations 2018 The Births Deaths and Marriages Registration (Forms) Regulations 2018 are amended by the provisions of these Regulations.

#### 4 Amendment to Schedule

Form 8 of the Schedule is deleted and substituted.
