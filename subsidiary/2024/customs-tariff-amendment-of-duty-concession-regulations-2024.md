# Customs Tariff (Amendment of Duty Concession) Regulations 2024

CUSTOMS TARIFF (AMENDMENT OF DUTY CONCESSION) REGULATIONS 2024 _____________________________ SL No. 1 of 2024 ______________________________ Notified: 15 February 2024 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 12 of the Customs Tariff Act 2014

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Customs Tariff (Amendment of Duty Concession) Regulations 2024.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of Part 2 of the Tariff

The condition for Code 109 under Part 2, Chapter 98 of the Tariff, is amended in paragraph (a) by deleting ‘$200’ and substituting with ‘$600’.
