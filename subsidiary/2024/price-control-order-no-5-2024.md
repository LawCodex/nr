# Price Control Order No.5 - 2024

CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT 2024

[Section 36]

Price Control Order No. 5 - 2024 S.L. No.  34  of 2024 I, H.E. Hon. David W R. Adeang M.P., Minister for Finance, acting pursuant to Section 36 of the Consumer Protection Act 2024, repeal all previous price orders for the same items in paragraphs (i) to (vi) below, and determine that this Price Control Order replaces all repealed Orders and has an immediate effect within 7 days after its publication in the Government Gazette:

(a) Maximum wholesale price at which diesel may be sold is $1.9453/litre;

(b) Maximum wholesale price at which petrol may be sold is $1.8451/litre;

(c) Maximum wholesale price at which JetA1 may be sold is $1.4396/litre;

(d) Maximum retail price at which diesel may be sold is $2.2453/litre;

(e) Maximum retail price at which petrol may be sold is $2.1451/litre; and

(f) Maximum retail price at which Jet A1 may be sold is $1.7396/litre.

Dated this   20th  day of December 2024.

H.E. HON. DAVID W. R. ADEANG, MP MINISTER FOR FINANCE
