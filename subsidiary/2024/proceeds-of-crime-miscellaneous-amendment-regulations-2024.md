# Proceeds of Crime (Miscellaneous) (Amendment) Regulations 2024

PROCEEDS OF CRIME (MISCELLANEOUS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2024 _____________________________ SL No. 6 of 2024 ______________________________ Notified: 28 March 2024 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 107 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2004:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Proceeds of Crime (Miscellaneous) (Amendment) Regulations 2024.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Proceeds of Crime (Miscellaneous) Regulations 2024

The Proceeds of Crime (Miscellaneous) Regulations 2024 are amended by these Regulations.

#### 4 Insertion of new Regulation 11

The following new subregulation 11, shall be inserted after Regulation 10:

‘11 Forfeiture order where person is untraceable or unknown For the purpose of Section 21(2) of the Act, the following category of persons, may also be considered by the Court, against which a forfeiture order may be issued:

(a) a person who cannot be traced or his or her whereabouts is unknown; or

(b) the person is not known.’.
