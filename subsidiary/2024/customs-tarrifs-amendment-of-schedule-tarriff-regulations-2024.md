# Customs Tarrifs (Amendment of schedule) (Tarriff) Regulations 2024

CUSTOMS TARIFF (AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE 1)

(TARIFF) REGULATIONS 2024 _____________________________ SL No. 19 of 2024 ______________________________ Notified: 14 June 2024 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 12(1)(a) of the Customs Tariff Act 2014:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Customs Tariff (Amendment of Schedule 1)(Tariff) Regulations 2024.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of Schedule 1

Schedule 1 of the Customs Tariff Act 2014 is amended by the provisions of these Regulations.
