# Customs (Prohibited Imports) (Repeal) Regulations 2024

CUSTOMS (PROHIBITED IMPORTS) (REPEAL) REGULATIONS 2024 _____________________________ SL No. 12 of 2024 ______________________________ Notified: 2 May 2024 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes these Regulations under Sections 91(3) and 310 of the Customs Act 2014:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Customs (Prohibited Imports) (Repeal) Regulations 2024.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Repeal of the Customs (Prohibited Imports) Regulations 2016

The Customs (Prohibited Imports) Regulations 2016 notified in the Gazette and commenced on 10 June 2016, is repealed.
