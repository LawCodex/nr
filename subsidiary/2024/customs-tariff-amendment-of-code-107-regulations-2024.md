# Customs Tariff (Amendment of Code 107) Regulations 2024

CUSTOMS TARIFF (AMENDMENT OF CODE 107) REGULATIONS 2024 _____________________________ SL No. 9 of 2024 ______________________________ Notified: 15 April 2024 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 12(2)(c) of the Customs Tariff Act 2014.

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Customs Tariff (Amendment of Code 107) Regulations 2024.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Customs Tariff Act 2014

Part 2 of Schedule 1 of the Customs Tariff Act 2014 shall be amended by these Regulations.

#### 4 Amendment of Schedule 1, Part 2, Code 107

Code 107, Part 2 of Schedule 1 is deleted and substituted as follows:

“ 107  Government or the Parliament of Nauru All goods Free  Free  That are for the use of the Government or the Parliament of Nauru and are not for private purposes Minister for Finance ”
