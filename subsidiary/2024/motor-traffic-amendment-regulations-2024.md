# Motor Traffic (Amendment) Regulations 2024

MOTOR TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2024 _____________________________ SL No. 11  of 2024 ______________________________ Notified: 2 May 2024 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 132 of the Motor Traffic Act 2014:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Traffic (Amendment) Regulations 2024.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Motor Traffic Regulations 2014

These Regulations amend the Motor Traffic Regulations 2014.

#### 4 Amendment of Regulation 6

Regulation 6 is amended by:

(a) in subregulation (1)(r), deleting the word ‘and’, where it appears after the phrase ‘white background;’;

(b) in subregulation (1)(s), deleting the full stop, where it appears after the phrase

‘white background’ and substituting with a semicolon;

(c) inserting the word ‘and’ after subregulation (1)(s); and

(d) inserting a new paragraph (t) after paragraph (s) as follows:

‘(t)    for vehicles of the Asian Development Bank, the letters “ADB” followed by 2 numerals in sequential order commencing with “01” in black on a white background.’.
