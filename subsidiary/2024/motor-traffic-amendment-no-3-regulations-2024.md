# Motor Traffic (Amendment) (No.3) Regulations 2024

MOTOR TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT) (NO 3) REGULATIONS 2024 _____________________________ SL No.  26  of 2024 ______________________________ Notified: 15 August 2024 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 132 of the Motor Traffic Act 2014:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Traffic (Amendment) (No 3) Regulations 2024.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Motor Traffic Regulations 2014

These Regulations amend the Motor Traffic Regulations 2014.

#### 4 Amendment of Regulation 6

Regulation 6 is amended by:

(a) deleting ‘and’ at the end of paragraph (t); and

(b) inserting after paragraph (u) the following paragraphs:

‘(v)  for the official vehicle of a Deputy Minister, the letters “DM” followed by numerals commencing with “01” in sequential order in black on a white background; and

(w)   for the official vehicle of the People’s Republic of China, the letters “CN DC” followed by numerals commencing with “01” in sequential order in black on a white background.’.
