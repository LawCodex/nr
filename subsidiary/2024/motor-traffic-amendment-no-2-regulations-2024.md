# Motor Traffic (Amendment) (No 2) Regulations 2024

MOTOR TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT) (NO 2) REGULATIONS 2024 _____________________________ SL No. 15  of 2024 ______________________________ Notified: 2 May 2024 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 132 of the Motor Traffic Act 2014:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Traffic (Amendment) (No 2) Regulations 2024.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of the Motor Traffic Regulations 2014

These Regulations amend the Motor Traffic Regulations 2014.

#### 4 Amendment of Regulation 6

Regulation 6 is amended by:

(a) deleting ‘and’ at the end of paragraph (s);

(b) deleting the fullstop and substituting with ‘; and’ at the end of paragraph (t); and

(c) inserting a new paragraph (u) after paragraph (t) as follows:

‘(u)    for the official vehicle of the Deputy Speaker of Parliament, the letters

“PN DEPUTY” in black on a white background.’.
