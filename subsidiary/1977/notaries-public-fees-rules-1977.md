# Notaries Public (Fees) Rules 1977

LEGAL PRACTITIONERS ACT 1973 NOTARIES PUBLIC (FEES) RULES 1977 ARRANGEMENT OF RULES Rule 1. Short title 2. Scale of fees Schedule _________ IN EXERCISE of the powers conferred on me by section 34 of the Legal Practitioners Act 1973, I hereby make the following Rules - SHORT TITLE l. These Rules may be cited as the Notaries Public (Fees) Rules 1977.

SCALE OF FEES 2. The fees chargeable by a notary public for discharging his duties under the provisions of the Act are those set out in the Schedule to these Rules.

Made this 7th day of January, 1977.

I. R. Thompson, Chief Justice. _______
