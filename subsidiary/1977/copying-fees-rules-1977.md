# Copying (Fees) Rules 1977

CIVIL PROCEDURE ACT 1972 COPYING (FEES) RULES 1977 ARRANGEMENT OF RULES Rule 1. Title and commencement 2. Fees to be collected for copies of documents 3. Repeal Schedule - Fees payable for copies of documents ________ IN EXERCISE of the powers conferred on me by section 76 of the Civil Procedure Act 1972, I hereby make the following Rules - TITLE AND COMMENCEMENT 1. These Rules may be cited as the Copying (Fees) Rules 1977 and shall come into force on 1st February, 1977.

FEES TO BE COLLECTED FOR COPIES OF DOCUMENTS 2. Where any person applies for a copy of any document in the possession of the Supreme Court or the District Court to be made and supplied to him by the Registrar of the Supreme Court or the Clerk of the District Court he shall pay a fee for the copying in accordance with the scale set out in the Schedule to these Rules:

Provided that the Registrar or the Clerk, as the case may be, may waive payment of the fee in any instance on the ground of the applicant's poverty.

REPEAL 3. The Copying (Fees) Rules 1973 are repealed.

Made this 7th day of January, 1977.

I. R. Thompson, Chief Justice. _______
