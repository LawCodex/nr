## PART 1 — PRELIMINARY MATTERS

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Passports Regulations 2011.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on 1 July 2011.

#### 3 Definitions

In these Regulations:

‘citizen investor’ means a person who has retained citizenship under Section 10(1) of the Naoero Citizenship Act 2005.

## PART 2 — PRESCRIBED OFFENCES

#### 4 Potential for harmful conduct – prescribed serious offences

For the purposes of Section 18(a)(iv) of the Act, an offence against a provision in Schedule 1 is a prescribed serious offence against another written law.

## PART 3 — VALIDITY OF TRAVEL DOCUMENTS

#### 5 Period of validity for passports

For the purposes of Section 22(a) of the Act, the maximum period of validity for a passport is as follows:

(a) subject to paragraphs (b), (c) and (d), 10 years;

(b) for a concurrent passport issued under Section 21(a) of the Act, 12 months;

(c) for an official, diplomatic or Presidential passport, 3 years; and

(d) for a replacement passport for which the application fee is waived under Regulation 9(a), the period ending when the passport being replaced would otherwise have ceased to be valid.

[reg 5 subst SL 10 of 2016 reg 5, opn 30 May 2016]

#### 6 Period of validity for travel-related document

For the purposes of Section 22(a) of the Act, the maximum period of validity for a travel-related document is as follows:

(a) subject to paragraphs (d) and (e), for a refugee travel document issued to a person qualified for the issue of the document under Section 13(3) of the Act, 2 years;

(b) subject to paragraphs (d) and (e), for a refugee travel document issued to a person qualified for the issue of the document under Section 13(4) of the Act, 12 months;

(c) subject to paragraphs (d) and (e), for a certificate of identity, 12 months;

(d) for a concurrent travel-related document issued under Section 21(a) of the Act, 12 months; and

(e) for a replacement travel-related document for which the application fee is waived under regulation 9(a), the period ending when the document being replaced would otherwise have ceased to be valid.

## PART 4 — DIPLOMATIC AND OFFICIAL PASSPORTS

#### 7 Conditions of diplomatic, official or Presidential passports

(1) The conditions specified in Schedule 2 apply to a diplomatic, official or Presidential passport.

(2) Where a condition specified in Schedule 2 is breached by the holder of a diplomatic, official or Presidential passport who is employed or otherwise engaged by the Republic, the holder is taken to have breached a condition of the person’s employment or engagement.

## PART 5 — FEES

#### 8 Prescribed fees

(1) For the purposes of Section 7(2)(b) of the Act, the prescribed fees for an application for a new or replacement passport other than a diplomatic, official or Presidential passport are set out in Part 1 of Schedule 3.

(2) For the purposes of Section 12(2)(b) of the Act, the prescribed fee for an application for a travel related document is set out in Part 2 of Schedule 3.

[reg 8 subst SL 32 of 2017, reg 4, opn 6 Dec 2017]

#### 9 Waiver of fee

The Minister may waive a fee prescribed under Regulation 8 in any of the following circumstances:

(a) the fee is for an application for a replacement travel document to reflect a change of name of a person who:

(i) holds a current travel document of the same class; and

(ii) has changed his or her name to a name that is different to the name that appears on the current document because the person has:

(A) married or divorced or the person’s spouse has died; or

(B) been adopted under the Adoption of Children Act 1965 or a corresponding law of another jurisdiction;

(b) the fee is for an application for a new travel document to replace a travel document that is faulty as a result of an error by the Department;

(c) the fee is for an application for a certificate of identity issued for the removal of a person from Nauru under the Immigration Act 2014; or

(d) the fee is for a travel document that is required so a person can travel because of a medical emergency or family crisis and the person demonstrates to the Minister’s satisfaction that the person does not have the means to pay the fee.

## SCHEDULE 2

[Regulation 7]

CONDITIONS OF DIPLOMATIC, OFFICIAL AND PRESIDENTIAL PASSPORTS 1. A diplomatic, official or Presidential passport may be used only for travel for diplomatic, official or Presidential purposes. 2. A diplomatic, official or Presidential passport shall be returned to the Department when it is no longer required for travel for diplomatic, official or Presidential purposes. 3. The loss or theft of a diplomatic, official or Presidential passport shall be reported immediately to the Secretary.

Note: Under Section 33 of the Act, the loss or theft of a passport shall also be reported to an authorised offıcer, and failure to do so is an offence.
