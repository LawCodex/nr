## Table of Amendments

The Environmental Management and Climate Change (Notices) Regulations 2021 SL 12 was notified and commenced on 22 June 2021 (GN No 395/2021; Gaz 88/2021).

Amending Legislation Notified Date of Commencement The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 62 of the Environmental Management and Climate Change Act 2020:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Environmental Management and Climate Change (Notices) Regulations 2021.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations come into effect on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Precautionary Notice

For the purposes of Section 43 of the Act, an authorised officer may issue a Precautionary Notice in Form 1 of the Schedule.

#### 4 Notice to Cease

For the purposes of Section 44 of the Act, the Secretary may issue a Notice to Cease an act or activity in Form 2 of the Schedule.

#### 5 Notice to Improve

For the purposes of Section 45 of the Act, an authorised officer may issue a Notice to Improve in Form 3 of the Schedule.

#### 6 Prohibition Notice

For the purposes of Section 46 of the Act, the Secretary may issue a Prohibition Notice in Form 4 of the Schedule.
