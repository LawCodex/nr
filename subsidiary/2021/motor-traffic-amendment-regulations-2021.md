# Motor Traffic (Amendment) Regulations 2021

MOTOR TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2021 ______________________________ SL No. 10 of 2021 ______________________________ Notified: 17th June 2021 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 132 of the Motor Traffic Act 2014:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Traffic (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations come into effect on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of Motor Traffic Regulations 2014

These Regulations amends the Motor Traffic Regulations 2014.

#### 4 Amendment of Regulation 6

Regulation 6(1) is amended:

(a) in paragraph (p)(ii), by deleting the word ‘and’ after the semicolon;

(b) in paragraph (q), by deleting the fullstop and substituting with a semicolon; and

(c) by inserting new paragraphs (r) and (s) immediately after paragraph (q) as follows:

‘(r)  for vehicles of the Nauru Maritime and Port Authority, the letters

“NMPA” followed by 2 numerals in sequential order commencing with

‘01’ in black on a white background; and

(s) for vehicles of the Nauru Shipping Line, the letters “NSL” followed by 2 numerals in sequential order commencing with ‘01’ in black on a white background.’
