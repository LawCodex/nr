# Naoero Citizenship (Forms and Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

NAOERO CITIZENSHIP (FORMS AND FEES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2021 ______________________________ SL No. 6 of 2021 ______________________________ Notified: 23rd April 2021 Table of Provisions The Cabinet makes the following Regulations under Section 32 of the Naoero Citizenship Act 2017:

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Naoero Citizenship (Forms and Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

#### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day they are notified in the Gazette.

#### 3 Amendment of Naoero Citizenship (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2019

The Naoero Citizenship (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2019 is amended by these Regulations.

#### 4 Amendment of Regulation 5

Regulation 5 is amended as follows:

(a) for subparagraph (d), by deleting the word ‘or’ after the semicolon;

(b) for subparagraph (e), by:

(i)  deleting the full stop and inserting a semicolon after ‘Schedule 2’; and

(ii) inserting the word ‘or’ after the semicolon; and

(c) inserting a new subparagraph (f):

‘(f) by a parent or parents of a minor or dependent under Section 19A of the Act, shall be in Form 8 of Schedule 2.’

#### 5 Amendment of Regulation 6

Regulation 6 is amended by inserting a new subregulation (5) as follows:

‘(5) For the purpose of an application for citizenship under Section 19A of the Act, the applicant shall provide:

(a) full name of minor or dependent;

(b) original or certified true copy of the original birth certificate of the minor or dependent;

(c) evidence that one or both parents are citizens;

(d) original or certified true copy of the applicant’s birth certificate; and

(e) evidence that the minor or dependent is present in the Republic; or

(f) evidence that the dependent is wholly dependent on the applicant.
